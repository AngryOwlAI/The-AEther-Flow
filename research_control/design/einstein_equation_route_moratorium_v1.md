# Einstein-Equation Route Moratorium

## Purpose

This control note implements the v15 P8-T01 Einstein-equation route
moratorium. It blocks direct Einstein-equation route selection until tracked
authority establishes or explicitly routes the prerequisite source-side burdens
needed for any honest field-equation attempt.

The moratorium is a routing and claim-boundary control. It is not a physics
theorem, not a no-go result, not a claim that Einstein equations are impossible,
and not authority to reject future source-side construction.

## Authority Status

This note is project-control guidance only. It does not alter canonical
ontology, source-law status, `MetricData(E)` status, `g_eff` status,
matter-semantics status, detector-semantics status, coupling-law status,
matter-coupling status, stress-energy status, matter-action status,
Einstein-equation status, benchmark status, or completed-derivation status.

It may be cited by Directors, selectors, auditors, validators, and future task
overlays as a route-control source. It must not be cited as proof authority.

## Direct Route Moratorium

No Einstein-equation route may be selected until a tracked source establishes
or explicitly routes:

1. source-side matter semantics;
2. detector semantics or a source-side replacement, if required;
3. coupling-law target;
4. coupling-law candidate;
5. coupling-law audit/stress;
6. matter-coupling derivation or precise obstruction;
7. stress-energy semantics target;
8. stress-energy tensor semantics;
9. matter action or alternative dynamics principle;
10. variation principle or field-equation dynamics; and
11. protected Gate Chair authority for any benchmark-promotion step.

If any listed prerequisite is absent, unresolved, or only available as scoped
evidence/precondition, direct EFE route from scoped evidence/precondition alone is blocked.
The correct outcome is a narrower prerequisite packet, selector packet, audit
packet, stress packet, checklist packet, or precise obstruction record.

## Allowed Prerequisite Routes

This moratorium does not block bounded prerequisite work. Lawful narrower
routes include:

- source-side matter-semantics target, candidate, audit, stress, or adoption
  readiness work with no matter-semantics adoption unless separately
  authorized;
- detector-semantics target or explicitly source-side replacement work;
- coupling-law target, candidate, audit, stress, or obstruction work with no
  coupling-law adoption unless separately authorized;
- matter-coupling derivation, adoption-readiness, or precise obstruction work
  under its own tracked route and claim boundary;
- stress-energy semantics target work that does not construct a stress-energy
  tensor;
- stress-energy tensor semantics work only after its source-side prerequisites
  are explicitly tracked;
- matter action or alternative dynamics-principle target work with no imported
  physical action unless authorized by tracked source authority;
- variation-principle or field-equation dynamics target work that preserves all
  upstream blocks;
- Gate Chair readiness work for benchmark-promotion authority without issuing
  the protected verdict; and
- validator, linter, checklist, role-contract, graph, or public-status packets
  that enforce the moratorium without treating enforcement as physics proof.

Allowed prerequisite routes must state their exact object, assumptions, source
certificates or missing certificates, forbidden downstream overreads, adoption
status, and next lawful route. They must preserve all blocked downstream
claims unless a separate tracked protected authority changes the boundary.

## Forbidden Overreads

No future packet may treat this moratorium, a validator PASS, or any scoped
evidence/precondition referenced by it as establishing any of the following:

- source-law adoption;
- `RR_ETransportCompletenessOrInvarianceLaw_v1` adoption;
- unrestricted `RR_E` theorem authority;
- `PositiveMSProfile_v1` adoption;
- `SourceMatterSemanticsAdoptionReadinessLaw_v1` law adoption;
- source-extension data adoption beyond exact scoped gate result;
- `MetricData(E)` adoption;
- `g_eff` adoption or scope expansion;
- coupling-law adoption;
- matter-semantics adoption;
- detector-semantics adoption;
- matter-coupling derivation or adoption;
- stress-energy semantics;
- stress-energy tensor;
- matter action;
- Einstein equations;
- exact-GR benchmark promotion;
- Gate Chair verdict;
- completed derivation;
- future source-extension impossibility; or
- program-wide no-go conclusion.

## Required Director Check

Before routing any task whose objective can be read as deriving or selecting an
Einstein-equation route, the Director should record:

```yaml
einstein_equation_route_moratorium_check:
  direct_einstein_equation_route_attempted: false
  direct_efe_route_from_scoped_evidence_precondition_alone_attempted: false
  all_prerequisites_established_or_routed_by_tracked_authority: false
  missing_or_scoped_prerequisites: []
  selected_lowest_authority_route: ""
  bounded_prerequisite_work_allowed: true
  forbidden_overreads_preserved: []
  gate_chair_authority_needed_for_benchmark_promotion: true
  conclusion: "direct EFE route blocked; bounded prerequisite work allowed"
```

If a direct Einstein-equation route is requested and all prerequisites are not
established or explicitly routed by tracked authority, `conclusion` must remain
equivalent to `direct EFE route blocked; bounded prerequisite work allowed`.

## Validator Guidance

Validators may check that a task records this moratorium check, names missing
or scoped prerequisites, and preserves forbidden overreads. A validator PASS
confirms structure and claim-boundary hygiene only. It does not establish
matter semantics, detector semantics, coupling law, matter coupling,
stress-energy semantics, a stress-energy tensor, a matter action, a variation
principle, Einstein equations, benchmark recovery, or a completed derivation.
