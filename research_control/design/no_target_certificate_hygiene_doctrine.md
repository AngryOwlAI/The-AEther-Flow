# No-Target Certificate Hygiene Doctrine

## Scope

This control note separates negative import-prevention certificates from
positive source-side matter theory. It is a project-control guard for future
matter-sector, detector, stress-energy, matter-action, benchmark, and proof
packets.

## Required Doctrine

Negative certificates prevent illegal imports. Positive source-side objects
must still construct semantics.

Negative certificates are hygiene guards. They may block target-side topology,
target atlas, target metric, proper time, detector semantics, empirical
calibration, stress-energy, matter action, GR benchmark, or process-authority
imports. They do not supply positive matter theory, detector theory,
stress-energy theory, matter dynamics, benchmark recovery, coupling laws,
Einstein equations, proof authority, or completed derivation.

## Certificate Classes

The certificate classes governed by this note are:

- no-target-topology certificate;
- no-target-atlas certificate;
- no-target-metric certificate;
- no-proper-time certificate;
- no-detector-import certificate;
- no-empirical-calibration certificate;
- no-stress-energy-import certificate;
- no-matter-action-import certificate;
- no-GR-benchmark-import certificate;
- no-process-authority certificate.

## Forbidden Overreads

The following inferences are forbidden without separate positive source-side
construction and the relevant authorization gates:

- forbidden phrase: no-target certificate supplies matter semantics;
- forbidden phrase: no-detector certificate supplies detector semantics;
- forbidden phrase: no-stress-energy certificate supplies stress-energy semantics;
- forbidden phrase: no-action certificate supplies matter action;
- forbidden phrase: no-benchmark certificate supplies benchmark recovery;
- forbidden phrase: no-process-authority certificate supplies mathematical proof.

## Positive-Object Requirement

A future positive source-side matter or detector semantics packet must provide
its own source-side objects, domains, relations, admissibility conditions,
stability checks, fail-closed obstruction branches, and explicit non-import
receipts. A negative certificate may be cited as a guardrail for what the
packet does not import. It is not evidence that the missing positive object has
been constructed.

## Adoption And Promotion Boundary

This doctrine does not adopt `PositiveMSProfile_v1`,
`RR_ETransportCompletenessOrInvarianceLaw_v1`, source-side matter semantics,
detector semantics, a coupling law, matter coupling, stress-energy semantics,
a stress-energy tensor, matter action, Einstein equations, benchmark recovery,
or completed derivation.

This doctrine does not reject future positive source-side construction. It
only prevents a negative certificate from being overread as that construction.
