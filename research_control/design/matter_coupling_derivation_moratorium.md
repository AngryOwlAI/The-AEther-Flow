# Matter-Coupling Derivation Moratorium

## Purpose

This control note implements the v14 P11-T01 matter-coupling derivation
moratorium. It blocks direct universal matter-coupling derivation attempts
until tracked authority establishes the required source-side prerequisites.

The moratorium is a routing and claim-boundary control. It is not a physics
theorem, not a no-go result, not a claim that matter coupling is impossible,
and not authority to reject future source-side matter-coupling construction.

## Authority Status

This note is project-control guidance only. It does not alter canonical
ontology, source-law status, `MetricData(E)` status, `g_eff` status,
matter-semantics status, detector-semantics status, coupling-law status,
stress-energy status, matter-action status, Einstein-equation status,
benchmark status, or completed-derivation status.

It may be cited by Directors, selectors, auditors, validators, and future task
overlays as a route-control source. It must not be cited as proof authority.

## Direct Route Moratorium

Direct universal matter-coupling derivation is blocked unless a separate
tracked authority record establishes, at minimum, all of the following:

1. adopted or explicitly authorized matter semantics;
2. detector semantics or an explicitly source-side replacement for detector
   semantics;
3. a coupling-law target and candidate under source-side assumptions;
4. stress-energy semantics or an explicit reason stress-energy semantics are
   not needed;
5. a matter action or an explicit alternative dynamics path;
6. `RR_E` separation, transport, and invariance handling for the declared
   object;
7. no-target certificate hygiene showing that negative import-prevention
   certificates are not being read as positive matter theory;
8. protected Gate Chair or selector routing when adoption is requested.

If any listed prerequisite is absent, unresolved, or only available as scoped
evidence/precondition, the direct route must fail closed into a narrower
theorem, precondition, audit, selector, or control packet.

## Allowed Narrower Routes

This moratorium allows lower-authority work that does not claim matter-coupling
derivation or adoption. Lawful narrower routes include:

- source-side matter-semantics equivalence-class theorem under explicit
  certificates;
- `PositiveMSProfile_v1` stability theorem under finite/local variation
  assumptions;
- `RR_E` separation-preservation theorem under explicit source transport or
  source invariance certificates;
- no-target certificate hygiene theorem or linter/example hardening;
- coupling-law target formalization only, with no adoption;
- detector-semantics alternative target formalization only, with no adoption;
- pre-adoption checklist, red-team review, smuggling audit, Refuter stress, or
  phase-validation control packet;
- scoped obstruction or freeze evaluation when prerequisites are insufficient.

Allowed narrower routes must state their exact object, assumptions, source
certificates, forbidden downstream overreads, and adoption status after the
packet. They must preserve all blocked downstream claims unless a separate
tracked protected authority changes the boundary.

## Forbidden Overreads

No future packet may treat this moratorium, or any scoped evidence/precondition
referenced by it, as establishing any of the following:

- source-law adoption;
- `SourceMatterSemanticsAdoptionReadinessLaw_v1` law adoption;
- `PositiveMSProfile_v1` adoption as matter semantics;
- `RR_ETransportCompletenessOrInvarianceLaw_v1` adoption as a source law;
- `MetricData(E)` adoption;
- `g_eff` scope expansion;
- coupling-law adoption;
- matter-semantics adoption;
- detector-semantics adoption;
- matter-coupling derivation or adoption;
- stress-energy semantics, stress-energy tensor, or matter action;
- Einstein equations;
- benchmark promotion;
- completed derivation;
- future source-extension impossibility; or
- broad rejection of the theory.

## Required Director Check

Before routing any task whose objective can be read as matter-coupling
derivation, the Director should record:

```yaml
matter_coupling_moratorium_check:
  direct_universal_matter_coupling_derivation_attempted: false
  all_prerequisites_established_by_tracked_authority: false
  missing_or_scoped_prerequisites: []
  selected_lowest_authority_route: ""
  allowed_narrow_route: true
  forbidden_overreads_preserved: []
  gate_or_selector_authority_needed: false
  conclusion: "direct route blocked; narrow route allowed"
```

If the direct route is requested and all prerequisites are not established by
tracked authority, `conclusion` must remain equivalent to `direct route
blocked; narrow route allowed`.

## Validator Guidance

Validators may check that a task records this moratorium check, names missing
or scoped prerequisites, and preserves forbidden overreads. A validator PASS
confirms structure and claim-boundary hygiene only. It does not establish
matter semantics, detector semantics, coupling law, stress-energy semantics,
matter action, Einstein equations, benchmark recovery, or a completed
derivation.
