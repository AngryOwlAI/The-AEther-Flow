# Scoped Positive Claim Vocabulary

## Scope

This note defines allowed high-risk status language for current live
frontier reporting after `RT-20260701-031`. It is a project-control
claim-language surface only. It does not edit ontology, adopt a source law,
prove an unrestricted theorem, or promote any downstream GR claim.

Use this note when a status row or agent handoff must report a positive,
source-side result while preserving blocked downstream obligations.

## Layer Rule

High-risk status claims must state all four layers when the object is near a
GR-derivation boundary:

| Layer | Required question |
| --- | --- |
| Control status | What transaction or gate status exists? |
| Mathematical status | What formal object, witness, theorem target, or obstruction is actually established? |
| Physical status | What physical interpretation is not established? |
| Promotion status | What downstream route remains blocked or merely opened? |

Bare `accepted` is disallowed for high-risk rows unless it is immediately
qualified by scope, object, and blocked downstream claims. The safer default is
to replace bare `accepted` with a scoped-positive term from this note.

## Required Vocabulary

| Term | Allowed meaning | Forbidden overread |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `adopted_as_source_only_object` | A source-side object has been adopted only inside its declared source-side role. | Do not render as target manifold, metric, matter coupling, Einstein equations, or benchmark promotion. |
| `adopted_as_scoped_source_extension_object` | A source-extension object has been adopted only inside a declared bounded extension scope. | Do not render as canonical ontology edit, unscoped adoption, or downstream GR premise. |
| `accepted_as_scoped_evidence_precondition` | Gate or control authority accepts an object only as scoped evidence/precondition for a later non-promotional route. | Do not render as source-law adoption, theorem proof, matter coupling, or completed derivation. |
| `proposal_only_law_target` | A candidate law is named or formalized as a target only. | Do not render as an adopted law or proved source primitive. |
| `proposal_only_theorem_target` | A theorem target is named or formalized as a target only. | Do not render as a theorem proof. |
| `source_pure_pending_stress` | Smuggling/source-purity audit found no immediate violation as written, but stress and later gates remain pending. | Do not render audit success as adoption or proof authority. |
| `stress_survived_pending_selector` | Refuter stress did not kill the route, but a selector or gate still determines lawful next use. | Do not render stress survival as acceptance, adoption, or theorem proof. |
| `human_gated_evidence_review_required` | A protected human gate is required before evidence/precondition status can change. | Do not infer authorization from user intent, validator output, or registry presence. |
| `blocked_adoption_open_continuation` | Current adoption is blocked while same-milestone continuation remains open. | Do not render as global impossibility or global theory rejection. |
| `frozen_negative_no_global_rejection` | A scoped route is frozen or negatively classified locally. | Do not render as rejection of all future source-extension routes or of the full theory. |
| `scoped_obstruction_no_global_no_go` | A specific obstruction holds under stated premises. | Do not render as a universal no-go theorem unless such a theorem is separately proved. |
| `route_synchronization_required` | Control surfaces must be synchronized before downstream work proceeds. | Do not render as physics progress or new mathematical payload. |
| `source_hygiene_certificate_only` | A certificate or checker records source-hygiene evidence only. | Do not render as proof authority, source-law adoption, or physical prediction. |
| `positive_source_semantics_candidate_only` | A source-semantics candidate has positive scoped evidence but is not adopted as semantics. | Do not render as matter semantics, detector semantics, stress-energy, or action principle. |
| `rr_e_separation_preserved` | The current evidence still preserves separation from unrestricted `RR_E` theorem or detector-collapse claims. | Do not render as unrestricted `RR_E` irrelevance proof or detector semantics. |
| `certificate_indexed_transport_invariance_evidence_only` | Certificate-indexed RR_E transport-completeness or invariance material is evidence/precondition only. | Do not render as adopted transport law, unrestricted theorem, matter-sector bridge, or downstream GR premise. |

## Permitted Claim Templates

Use a two-part claim when space is limited:

> `X` is [scoped-positive term] for [declared source-side use]; it is not
> [blocked downstream claim list].

Use a four-layer claim when reporting high-risk rows:

> Control status: [gate/control term]. Mathematical status: [actual formal
> payload]. Physical status: [what is not established]. Promotion status:
> [blocked or next lawful route].

## Current RR_E Boundary

For the current live frontier, `RR_ETransportCompletenessOrInvarianceLaw_v1`
may be described only as scoped source-extension `RR_E`
transport-completeness or invariance evidence/precondition under the declared
source-side, certificate-indexed, fail-closed boundary. It must not be
described as an adopted source law, an adopted `RR_ETransportCompletenessOrInvarianceLaw_v1`
object, an unrestricted `RR_E` irrelevance theorem, detector semantics, matter
semantics, a coupling law, matter coupling, `MetricData(E)`, `g_eff`,
Einstein equations, benchmark promotion, or completed derivation.
