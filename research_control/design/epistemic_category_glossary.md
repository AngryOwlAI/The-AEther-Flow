## Scope

This glossary implements v15 P14-T02, carries the bounded v21 P4-T06
source-architecture vocabulary synchronization, and is aligned by v21 P14-T06
with the completed P14-T02 through P14-T05 evidence chain. It defines the
project-control categories that public and agent-facing surfaces must keep

a canonical ontology edit, not a Gate Chair verdict, and not authority to

## Required Distinctions

### Interpretation Versus Derivation

An interpretation is an explanatory frame or research ontology proposal. A
derivation is a source-backed argument that establishes a target statement from
declared assumptions. Public text may say AEther-flow is a proposed
interpretation. It must not say the interpretation itself derives GR.

### Research Architecture Versus Physical Ontology

A canonical mainline research architecture fixes which source assumptions and
open slots current work will use. It does not establish that those assumptions
describe reality. For the selected continuum-first mainline, the smooth
four-dimensional source arena is primitive debt rather than an emergent or
physical-spacetime result. `Phi_src` remains unresolved: no parameter domain,
algebra, invertibility, stochastic status, gauge status, source law, source
dynamics, or physical-time meaning is adopted. Public text may describe this
tracked architecture selection, but it must not call the selection an
established physical ontology or a derivation.

### Model Versus Physical Theory

A model may be mathematically precise while still lacking the semantics,
dynamics, observables, or empirical commitments needed for physical theory
status. Public text may describe source-side models as models. It must not
upgrade them to a physical theory unless tracked authority establishes the
missing physical layers.

### Exact Closure, Benchmark Compatibility, And First-Principles Recovery

Exact-GR operational closure adopts ordinary GR as the target-side effective
law. Benchmark compatibility is a comparison or obligation target.
First-principles recovery is the stronger derivation path from source-side
primitives to the GR target. Gate E currently records `NOT_READY`, denies
benchmark promotion, and says a completed derivation may not be claimed.
Public text must not collapse target-side closure, compatibility, benchmark
aspiration, or protected review into first-principles recovery.

### Formal Equivalence Versus Genuine Emergence

A formal equivalence relates declared mathematical objects under declared
assumptions. Genuine emergence additionally needs positive source provenance,
dynamics, uniqueness or quotient status, operational meaning, and robustness.
No-target purity is necessary but insufficient. A target-side redescription or
isomorphism may be mathematically useful while leaving every source-selection
and physical-emergence burden open.

### Interpretive Representative Versus Source-Derived Structure

The protected P14-T04 decision assigns target-side `u^mu` the status
`INTERPRETIVE_REPRESENTATIVE_TARGET_CONGRUENCE_ONLY`. It is admissible and
generally non-unique inside the adopted GR benchmark. It is not `Phi_src`, is
not selected by the current source ontology, is not established as pure gauge,
and is not an independent dynamical field. A stronger status needs separate
source-backed evidence and, where protected, fresh authority.

### Representative Independence Versus Observer-Relative Measurement

For a fixed target solution, fixed base observables, and fixed protocols, the
P14-T04 theorem says the stated predictions do not depend on which admissible
representative is chosen. That claim does not erase congruence-explicit or
observer-relative quantities. Measurements or decompositions that explicitly
change observer field or protocol can differ without contradicting the fixed-
protocol representative-independence result.

### Operational Meaning Versus Empirical Novelty

Operational meaning connects a formal object to adopted semantics, observers,
detectors, or protocols. Empirical novelty is stronger: it requires a distinct
observable claim and comparison evidence. The protected P7 matter postulate
creates operational and physical status only for its exact finite package; it
does not derive those meanings from the source ontology and does not create a
new low-energy prediction, target stress-energy, Einstein equations, or
benchmark promotion.

### Scoped Source Object Versus Physical Object

`M_src` and `g_eff` can be discussed as scoped source-side objects under their
tracked reviews. That does not make `M_src` a target manifold, `g_eff` an
unscoped Lorentzian metric, or either object a physical matter-coupling result.

### Evidence/Precondition Versus Adoption

Matter-sector and `RR_E` transport/invariance materials may be scoped
evidence/preconditions. Adoption requires an explicit tracked authority
decision and exact scope. The exact P7 matter package now has separate
protected postulate-level adoption within its declared finite domains; that
does not retrospectively turn its supporting evidence into a source
derivation. Public text must name which layer it means.

### Validator Receipt Versus Scientific Proof

A validator receipt confirms that specified files or records passed specified
operational checks. It does not prove a physics claim. It can support workflow
trust, freshness, and claim-language hygiene; it cannot adopt a source law,
derive matter coupling, derive Einstein equations, promote a benchmark, or
complete the derivation.

### Publication Surface Versus Authority Source

Publication surfaces communicate source-backed status. They do not outrank
registered TeX, registries, registered Markdown control sources, Gate Chair
artifacts, or the explicit project authority hierarchy. If a publication
surface and a tracked source conflict, the tracked source governs.

## Public-Overread Guard

Public text derived from this glossary must preserve these negative guards:

- GR is not derived.
- AEther-flow is a proposed research interpretation / explanatory frame, not an established physical ontology.
- Continuum-first is the canonical mainline research architecture, not proof
  of a true, unique, fundamental, empirical, or derived physical ontology.
- The smooth four-dimensional source arena is explicit primitive debt and is
  not identified with physical spacetime or the exact-GR target manifold.
- `Phi_src` is an unresolved abstract source-order or evolution slot, not an
  adopted map, action, generator, gauge structure, clock, source law, or
  source dynamics.
- Emergence-first remains a proposal-only noncanonical fallback under the
  tracked P4-T05 reopening conditions; it is not rejected or erased.
- Target-side `u^mu` is an admissible, generally non-unique interpretive
  representative; it is not `Phi_src`, a source-selected congruence, an
  established pure-gauge result, an independent field, or a completed bridge.
- Fixed-protocol representative independence does not erase
  congruence-explicit or observer-relative measurement differences.
- `M_src` is not a target manifold, metric, or GR derivation.
- `ScopedMetricStructureRecord_src` is not an unscoped Lorentzian metric,
  operational geometry, matter coupling, or Einstein equations;
  `g_eff^{GSC-cand}` is its exact legacy alias only.
- The exact P7 matter package is adopted by explicit protected human postulate
  only within its declared finite domains. Its supporting evidence is not a
  first-principles derivation, and the adoption does not supply target-side
  stress-energy, `g_eff`, Einstein equations, benchmark promotion, or
  empirical novelty.
- `RR_E` transport/invariance evidence is not source-law adoption or an
  unrestricted theorem.
- Exact-GR operational closure is target-side adoption, not source derivation
  or Gate E benchmark promotion.
- Formal equivalence is not genuine emergence; operational meaning is not
  empirical novelty.
- Validator receipts are not scientific proof.
- Publication surfaces are not independent authority sources.
- Benchmark promotion and completed derivation remain blocked unless tracked
  protected authority changes that status.
