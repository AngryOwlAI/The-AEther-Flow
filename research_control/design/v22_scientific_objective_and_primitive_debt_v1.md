## Metric-equivalence rule

If assumed data determine a Lorentzian conformal class without new physical
input, they are metric-bearing. If they also determine scale, they are
metric-completing. An outcome constructed from such assumed data is
postulation or reconstruction unless the data's own provenance is derived
from source fields and laws.

- A coframe together with a fixed internal Lorentzian form, or a nondegenerate
  Lorentzian quadratic form, is metric-completing.
- A single-cone principal polynomial or a nonbirefringent constitutive tensor
  is metric-bearing only under the explicit hypotheses that permit conformal
  metric recovery; normalization or volume data may then complete scale.
- A volume structure alone is not metric-equivalent. It becomes
  metric-completing when paired with a Lorentzian conformal class.

Ambiguous cases fail closed and receive no emergence credit.
