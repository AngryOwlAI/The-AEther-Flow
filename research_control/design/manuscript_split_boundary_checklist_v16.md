# Manuscript Split Boundary Checklist v16

Plan task: `P12-T03`

Role: `project-control-maintainer@0.2.0`

Output type: project-control checklist.

This checklist implements the v16 P12-T03 manuscript split boundary. It is a
control artifact, not an executable validator, not a manuscript submission,
not a publication action, not a physics proof, not an AI-system capability
claim, and not authority for claim promotion.

## Purpose

The physics-program manuscript and the AI research-agent methodology
manuscript may cite shared project-control evidence only under strict
authority separation:

- the physics manuscript may use workflow receipts as process provenance, but
  not as physics proof;
- the AI methodology manuscript may use scoped physics artifacts as case
  studies of controlled workflow, but not as autonomous-system success beyond
  the declared scope;
- neither manuscript may borrow stronger authority from the other manuscript.

When a future manuscript edit, outline refresh, abstract, introduction, public
summary, or reviewer packet touches both manuscript lanes, run this checklist
before treating the wording as compatible with project-control state.

## Required Check Matrix

| Check | Pass condition | Fail condition | Corrective action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Physics manuscript cannot cite AI validator PASS as physics proof. | Validator receipts, checkpoint receipts, memory preflight, dependency graphs, and role completions are described only as process provenance or integrity evidence. | The physics manuscript states or implies that a validator PASS, AgentJob completion, commit, graph, memory hit, or workflow success proves a theorem or physical claim. | Replace proof language with source-backed proof or evidence language. Cite the relevant registered TeX, Markdown control source, or registry row instead. |
| AI methodology manuscript cannot cite physics theorem as autonomous-system success beyond scoped result. | Physics artifacts are described as bounded case-study outputs governed by source authority, human gates, roles, and validators. | The methodology manuscript states or implies that the AI system independently established a theory, derived physics, or earned scientific authority because a theorem, audit, or Gate Chair result exists. | Restate the claim as workflow traceability or bounded support. Preserve the human and source authority surfaces. |
| Neither manuscript may claim GR derivation. | Both manuscripts state that first-principles GR derivation remains open unless a separate protected gate and source-backed derivation establish it. | Either manuscript says or implies that exact GR, Einstein equations, universal matter coupling, or benchmark recovery has been derived from the current ontology. | Replace with open-burden language and cite the current frontier or Distance-to-GR ledger as status navigation only. |
| Neither manuscript may convert scoped evidence into adoption. | Scoped evidence, preconditions, source-extension data, finite/local fixtures, and Gate Chair scoped results remain exact in scope. | Scoped evidence is shortened to adopted source law, adopted physical object, matter semantics, detector semantics, coupling law, benchmark promotion, or completed derivation. | Restore the exact status label and name the blocked downstream claim. |
| Public wording must preserve proposed ontology status. | AEther-flow ontology is described as proposed, source-first, or interpretive unless an exact tracked authority changes that status. | Public or manuscript wording presents the ontology as established physical ontology, canonical ontology adoption, or accepted physical theory. | Restore proposed-ontology language and cite the glossary boundary or current frontier. |
| Negative results must remain scoped. | Obstructions, countermodels, frozen routes, and fail-closed branches name their assumptions, route, object, and downstream consequence. | A local obstruction is generalized into program-wide rejection, future source-extension impossibility, or global no-go conclusion without a separate theorem and protected authority. | Add the route/object scope and state which continuations remain open. |

## Additional Boundary Checks

| Check | Pass condition | Fail condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source hierarchy preserved | Physics claims cite registered TeX or exact source-control artifacts. Control claims cite registries, handoffs, DDRs, AgentJobs, or registered Markdown control sources. | Generated wiki notes, PDFs, HTML, Obsidian notes, `.local/` caches, compact graphs, or dependency snapshots are treated as independent authority. |
| Gate authority exact | A manuscript names the exact protected gate, layer, and downstream block when a Gate Chair or human authorization is relevant. | Broad user authorization, role identity, or ordinary task completion is treated as a Gate Chair verdict or adoption authority. |
| Cross-citation discipline | A manuscript cites the other manuscript only for framing, comparative methodology, or compatible vocabulary. | The physics manuscript uses the methodology manuscript to strengthen physics status, or the methodology manuscript uses the physics manuscript to strengthen AI autonomy. |
| Compact-state discipline | `current_frontier.md` and dependency graph outputs are used only as synchronized navigation to tracked sources. | Compact frontier, graph output, renderer output, or freshness PASS is cited as proof or claim promotion. |
| Manuscript surface boundary | Control artifacts guide future manuscript drafting but are not themselves submitted manuscripts. | A control artifact is presented as submitted, peer-reviewed, public-facing, or publication-ready without separate authorization. |

## Application Procedure

1. Identify the manuscript lane: physics-program manuscript, AI methodology
   manuscript, shared glossary, public summary, or external review packet.
2. Identify the claim class: physics theorem, ontology status, evidence status,
   benchmark status, process-methodology claim, validator receipt, memory
   receipt, negative result, or public-facing wording.
3. Inspect the canonical source or registry row named by any memory hit or
   generated derivative.
4. Run the Required Check Matrix. Any fail condition blocks the wording until
   corrected.
5. Record either direct citation of this checklist or a task-local
   compatibility receipt.
6. If the wording would promote ontology, source law, matter coupling,
   Einstein equations, benchmark status, Gate Chair closure, autonomous
   scientific authority, or completed derivation, stop and route through the
   exact protected gate instead.

## Forbidden Conclusion Summary

- No validator PASS is physics proof.
- No AgentJob completion is autonomous scientific authority.
- No scoped theorem, audit, source-extension datum, or Gate Chair scoped result
  is unqualified adoption.
- No manuscript outline or status refresh is a manuscript submission.
- No current artifact derives GR from first principles.
- No local negative result is a program-wide no-go theorem unless a separate
  theorem and protected authority establish that stronger claim.

## No-Delta Receipt

- Scientific claims changed: false.
- Physics promotion authorized: false.
- Proof authority changed: false.
- Benchmark promotion authorized: false.
- Completed derivation authorized: false.
- Autonomous scientific authority authorized: false.
- Public publication action authorized: false.
