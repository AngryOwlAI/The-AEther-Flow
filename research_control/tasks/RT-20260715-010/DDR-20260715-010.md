## Scope-alarm review

The plan explicitly requires a new documentation-core gate, a scoped
synchronization path, mode summaries, fixture repositories, and durable scope
proof. That necessarily exceeds a trivial local branch change, but remains
inside the three planned implementation paths and introduces no dependency,
public schema, or physics-authority change. Make, CI, checkpoint, planner,
P3-T05, and final compatibility retirement remain outside this packet.

## Preserved boundaries

- Preserve the ordinary full bootstrap, `memory_validate_core`, explicit
  publication gate, local-retrieval gate, and legacy composite behavior.
- Documentation modes may select registered Markdown, HTML explainers, their
  wiki notes, and required mirrored registry rows only.
- Exclude TeX, physics PDFs, scientific validators, full control-history
  validation, and local-retrieval freshness from documentation-mode ownership.
- Preserve `handoff-0740` and `EqSrc_family_closure_repair_or_stress` as the
  ordinary research authority.
- Do not edit Make, CI, checkpoint, planner, canonical ontology, science draft,
  or generated derivatives by hand.

  evidence as physics proof.

corruption to remain blocking; unrelated corrupted TeX/PDF/local-retrieval
families to remain untraversed by documentation modes while the full gate still
detects them; selected and excluded family counts to be reported; tracked
documentation to name the canonical commands; and all current project-system
checkpoint obligations to pass. Otherwise retire the modes or issue
`REPAIR_REQUIRED`, `ROLLBACK_REQUIRED`, or `BLOCKED` without checkpointing
invalid state.
