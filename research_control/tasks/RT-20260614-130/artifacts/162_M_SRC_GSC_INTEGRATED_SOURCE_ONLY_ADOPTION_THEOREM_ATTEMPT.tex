\section{Theorem Statement}

\begin{theorem}[Conditional integrated source-only adoption theorem candidate]
Let \(E\) be a source-side input for which \(\Mgsccand(E)\) has the prior
bridge-candidate status recorded in \texttt{RT-117} through \texttt{RT-123}.
Assume the following hypothesis families.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{H\arabic*.}, leftmargin=*]
  \item Carrier and quotient support are source-defined and stable under the
  allowed source overlaps.
  \item \(\CovSrc(E)\) is the selected site-coverage topology analogue and is
  generated from source supports, overlap predicates, certificates, and
  \(\BottomSrc\) guards, not from target open sets.
  \item Chart-support tokens are regular only in the source-token sense
  required by the Phase 1 theorem envelope.
  \item Transition tokens satisfy source identity, inverse, composition, and
  cocycle conditions over the selected coverage overlaps.
  \item Separation, countability or size, and regularity analogues are stated
  and discharged as source-only conditions or else return their named
  obstruction labels.
  \item Any soldering use is conditional on \(\RegSold\)-style source data and
  does not adopt \(\RegSold\).
  \item Any finite-variation use is conditional on \(\FVR\)-style source data
  and does not adopt \(\FVR\).
  \item \(\BottomSrc\) is fail-closed for missing, ambiguous, noncanonical, or
  process-only premises.
  \item Every non-bottom proof step factors through \(\SourceData\) and is
  invariant under replacement of the diagnostic target context \(\TargetCtx\).
  \item Finite/local witnesses and finite checker outputs are used only as
  examples, counterexample surfaces, or stress data; they do not globalize to
  \(\Msrc\) adoption by themselves.
  \item Every Phase 6 counterexample family is either excluded by one of the
  hypotheses above or returns \(\BottomSrc\) or the corresponding obstruction
  label.
  \item No registry row, validator pass, role assignment, handoff status,
  generated derivative, local cache, commit status, or file order is used as a
  mathematical premise.
  \item The theorem candidate remains subject to later Smuggling Auditor
  review, Refuter stress, and explicit human Gate Chair adoption authority.
\end{enumerate}

Then \(\Pi_{\mathrm{int}}(E)\) is a conditional source-only adoption theorem
candidate for \(\Mgsccand(E)\): it is well-formed enough to be submitted to
Smuggling Auditor and Refuter review as the integrated Phase 7 theorem
candidate. The theorem does not adopt \(\Msrc\), does not construct full
\(\Msrc\), and does not unlock \(g_{\mathrm{eff}}\), matter coupling, Einstein
equations, or benchmark promotion.
\end{theorem}

\section{Proof Attempt}

The proof is an integration proof rather than an adoption proof.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item The Phase 1 envelope supplies the minimum theorem shape: carrier or
  quotient support, topology or topology analogue, chart support, transition
  compatibility, separation/countability/regularity analogues, conditional
  soldering and finite-variation burdens, bottom behavior, and no-target
  import. Hypotheses H1 through H13 restate these as source-only obligations.
  \item The Phase 2 packet selects \(\CovSrc(E)\) as the site-coverage route.
  H2 uses that route only as a source-only topology analogue. It does not
  identify \(\CovSrc(E)\) with target open sets or a target smooth manifold.
  \item The Phase 3 packet states source transition-token identity, inverse,
  composition, and cocycle conditions. H4 incorporates those conditions as
  assumptions with fail-closed transition obstruction branches.
  \item The Phase 4 packet proves the relevant non-implication: finite/local
  survival is not global \(\Msrc\) adoption. H10 therefore treats finite/local
  objects as theorem inputs or stress data only.
  \item The Phase 5 packet states the source-only factorization criterion. H9
  imports that criterion as an audit obligation on every non-bottom proof step.
  \item The Phase 6 catalog supplies finite/local counterexample pressure
  against separation, topology canonicity, transition inverse and cocycle
  laws, soldering uniqueness, finite-variation robustness, target import, and
  bottom behavior. H11 requires those branches to be excluded by explicit
  source hypotheses or to fail closed with named obstruction labels.
  \item H12 blocks process-authority laundering. Therefore validation,
  registry, handoff, role, generated-derivative, and local-cache states can
  track the packet but cannot serve as proof premises.
  \item Given H1 through H13, every non-bottom step of
  \(\Pi_{\mathrm{int}}(E)\) is source-factorized, every failed branch has a
  stable obstruction label or \(\BottomSrc\), and all downstream claim gates
  remain outside the theorem. Hence the theorem candidate is formed, but the
  only authorized conclusion is readiness for audit and stress.
\end{enumerate}

This proof attempt is conditional. If a later audit finds target-context
dependence, process-authority laundering, or an unexcluded Phase 6
counterexample family, the theorem candidate fails under the corresponding
obstruction label rather than becoming an adoption proof.
