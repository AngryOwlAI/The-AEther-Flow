\section*{Control Status}

This artifact is a human-approved Gate Chair decision for
\texttt{RT-20260614-087}. It records the explicit approval
\texttt{approval-20260622-001} for the \(\GSC\) source-extension-law
adoption question raised by \texttt{RT-20260614-086}.

The decision adopts \(\GSC\) only as an admissible source-extension law for
later \(\Msrc\) continuation. It is not a canonical ontology TeX edit, not a
canonical adoption of \(\MSL\), not adoption of \(\AtlasGlueDisc\), not
adoption of \(\AtlasGlue\), not adoption or full construction of \(\Msrc\),
not \(\geff\), not matter coupling, not Einstein equations, not benchmark
evidence, not a closure verdict, and not completed-derivation evidence.

\section{Question Decided}

The Candidate Constructor packet \texttt{RT-20260614-084} constructed
\(\GSC\) as a source-only fail-closed general source-cover candidate. The
Smuggling Auditor packet \texttt{RT-20260614-085} found a conditional
source-purity pass as written. The Refuter packet \texttt{RT-20260614-086}
found that \(\GSC\) survives the named stress cases as draft/control
source-extension data because profile collapse, selector ties, inverse
defects, and cocycle defects return \(\Bot\), while \(\Qsrc\) remains
conditional source quotient support and \(\Vargsc\) remains candidate-local.

The protected question is therefore narrow:
\[
\text{May }\GSC\text{ be adopted as an admissible source-extension law for}
\ \Msrc\text{ continuation?}
\]

\section{Decision}

\begin{decision}[General source-cover source-extension-law adoption]
Under tracked human authorization, \(\GSC\) is adopted as an admissible
source-extension law for subsequent bounded \(\Msrc\) continuation.
\end{decision}

The adopted law has the same fail-closed dependency basis recorded in
\texttt{RT-20260614-084}, audited in \texttt{RT-20260614-085}, and
stress-tested in \texttt{RT-20260614-086}. For a supplied source-cover package,
\(\GSC\) may return candidate source quotient-support data or \(\Bot\). This
decision does not prove that current ontology derives \(\GSC\), does not prove
that all source covers are non-bottom, and does not prove arbitrary
finite-variation robustness beyond \(\Vargsc\).

\section{Adoption Conditions}

\begin{condition}[Source-only inputs]
The inputs to \(\GSC\) must remain source carriers, source overlap relations,
source-token profiles, source transition candidates, source inverse and
cocycle checks, candidate-local admissible variations, and conditional source
quotient-support data.
\end{condition}

\begin{condition}[Fail-closed branches]
Profile collapse, selector nonuniqueness, inverse defects, cocycle defects,
and variations outside \(\Vargsc\) must continue to return \(\Bot\) or remain
unproved. They may not be repaired by target-atlas resemblance, benchmark
success, validation receipts, role identity, registry metadata, generated
derivatives, handoff status, or file order.
\end{condition}

\begin{condition}[No downstream shortcut]
The adoption of \(\GSC\) does not identify \(\Qsrc\) with adopted \(\Msrc\).
It does not define \(\geff\), matter coupling, Einstein equations, or
benchmark status. Those remain separate derivational burdens.
\end{condition}

\section{Claim Boundary}

Allowed by this decision:
\begin{itemize}
  \item use \(\GSC\) as adopted source-extension-law data in a later bounded
  \(\Msrc\) continuation packet;
  \item classify \(\GSC\) as adopted in the source-extension-law sense only;
  \item preserve \(\Qsrc\) as conditional source quotient support rather than
  adopted \(\Msrc\); and
  \item route the next packet toward a source-manifold candidate construction
  or the smallest remaining missing primitive.
\end{itemize}

Not allowed by this decision:
\begin{itemize}
  \item canonical ontology TeX edits;
  \item canonical adoption of \(\MSL\);
  \item adoption of \(\AtlasGlueDisc\), \(\AtlasGlue\), or \(\Msrc\);
  \item full \(\Msrc\) construction;
  \item \(\geff\), matter coupling, Einstein equations, benchmark promotion,
  completed-derivation language, future source-extension impossibility, or
  global theory rejection; or
  \item treating registry status, validation status, role identity, generated
  derivatives, or this approval as physics evidence beyond the scoped
  source-extension-law adoption decision.
\end{itemize}

\section{Next Route}

The logical next packet is one bounded Candidate Constructor bridge attempt
targeting \(\Msrc\). It may use the adopted \(\GSC\) source-extension-law
input. It must either construct a source-manifold candidate or name the
smallest remaining missing primitive. It must not promote \(\geff\), matter
coupling, Einstein equations, benchmark status, closure, completed
derivation, future source-extension impossibility, or global theory rejection.

\section{Conclusion}

\(\GSC\) is adopted as an admissible source-extension law for the
\(\Msrc\) continuation line. The adoption is deliberately narrow. It opens a
bounded source-manifold construction attempt while preserving all downstream
claim gates.
