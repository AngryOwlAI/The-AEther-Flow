\section{Canonical current-topology carrier}

Let \(U\) be an open submanifold of a current smooth source manifold and let
\(\Open(U)\) denote its topology. For an open embedding
\(j:V\hookrightarrow U\), define
\begin{equation}
  j^*: \Open(U)\longrightarrow\Open(V),\qquad j^*(O)=j^{-1}(O).
  \label{eq:restriction}
\end{equation}
The identity and composition laws for inverse image give
\begin{equation}
  (\operatorname{id}_U)^*=\operatorname{id}_{\Open(U)},\qquad
  (j\circ k)^*=k^*\circ j^*.
\end{equation}
Thus \(\Open(-)\) is a canonical contravariant carrier on the declared
source-open category. For a diffeomorphism \(f:M\to N\), direct image
\(O\mapsto f(O)\) and inverse image are mutually inverse frame
isomorphisms. Their variances are distinct and must not be conflated.

The category of elements packages pairs \((U,O)\) with
\(O\in\Open(U)\). This packaging is eliminable: every object and arrow
reduces to open-subset membership and inverse image in the already present
source topology. If a future construction adds a selector, measure,
orientation, law, or physical predicate not eliminated by those definitions,
it is no longer a conservative definitional extension.

\subsection{Unique open-cover descent}

Let \(\{U_i\}_{i\in I}\) be an open cover of \(U\), and choose
\(O_i\in\Open(U_i)\) satisfying the overlap equations
\begin{equation}
 O_i\cap U_j=U_i\cap O_j
 \quad\text{for all }i,j.
 \label{eq:compatibility}
\end{equation}
Then
\begin{equation}
 O=\bigcup_{i\in I}O_i
 \label{eq:glue}
\end{equation}
is open and restricts to every \(O_i\). If \(O'\) has the same
restrictions, the cover gives
\(O'=\bigcup_i(O'\cap U_i)=\bigcup_iO_i=O\). Hence the gluing is unique.

This is topology descent. It is not the predecessor's conditional descent of
an occurrence kernel, and it supplies no occurrence law.

\section{Symmetry and occurrence separation}

\subsection{Trivial full-symmetry natural sections}

Let \(M\) be connected, and suppose \(O_M\in\Open(M)\) is invariant under
every element of \(\Diff(M)\). If \(O_M\) is nonempty, choose
\(x\in O_M\). The diffeomorphism group of a connected smooth manifold is
transitive on points, so for every \(y\in M\) there is a diffeomorphism
\(f\) with \(f(x)=y\). Invariance gives \(y\in O_M\), hence \(O_M=M\).
The other possibility is \(O_M=\varnothing\).

Therefore, on each connected component of the full smooth-manifold audit
groupoid, a natural deterministic open selection is fiberwise empty or total.
This does not impose one common choice on components with no connecting arrow.
It also does not survive arbitrary added source markings: with a marked point
\(p\) and point-preserving diffeomorphisms, \(M\setminus\{p\}\) is a proper
invariant open. Full symmetry and bare-source typing are load-bearing.

\subsection{Carrier does not determine occurrence}

Conditionally equip \(\Open(M)\) with the full powerset measurable
structure. Both \(\varnothing\) and \(M\) are fixed by every diffeomorphism,
so the formal kernels
\begin{equation}
 \delta_{\varnothing},\qquad \delta_M,\qquad
 K_\lambda=\lambda\delta_{\varnothing}+(1-\lambda)\delta_M,
 \quad 0\leq\lambda\leq1,
 \label{eq:kernels}
\end{equation}
are normalized and invariant. Distinct \(\lambda\) give distinct kernels on
one unchanged carrier. Covariance and normalization therefore do not select a
unique occurrence law, physical probability, empirical frequency, or realized
token. The measurable structure in this control is conditional and is not
adopted as current-source ontology.
