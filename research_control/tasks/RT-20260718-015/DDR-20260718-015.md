## Decision

Select `ontology-formalizer@0.2.0` through one task overlay for a bounded
`ontology-law-research-packet`. The packet defines
`EqSrcClosureLaw_src^cand` as a proposal-only source-side law interface that
makes the prior family-closure assumptions explicit as typed certificate
operations.

## Basis

`research_control/handoffs/handoff-0740.yaml` authorizes one bounded
`EqSrc_family_closure_repair_or_stress` packet. The validated P3 theorem and
Refuter artifacts establish that the conditional theorem remains source-pure
when H1--H7 are supplied, while current ontology does not derive those closure
assumptions generally. Missing inverse and composition operations already have
finite source-side countermodels.

This is `derivation_critical_missing_source_law`, not an ordinary documentation
gap or workflow inconvenience. Another generic stress packet would repeat the
known obstruction. A constructive, reversible repair packet is available:
formalize explicit identity, inverse-provenance, composition, and
ledger-congruence operations as a proposal-only candidate for later audit.

objects and witness-certificate domains; define identity tokens, explicit
inverse-provenance tokens, composable certificate-step families, ledger
