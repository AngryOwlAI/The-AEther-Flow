## Claim Boundary

The packet may report observed parse counts, file reads, hashes, subprocesses,
cache hits and misses, bytes, wall times, storage, eviction, normalized
correctness parity, and whether the measured evidence supports or rejects
planner-authoritative cache activation. It may not claim general performance,
production gate eligibility, cache-backed checkpoint acceptance, scientific
proof, or any physics result.

## Validation

PASS requires shared-snapshot parse reduction, at least three warm repetitions
for each short plan shape, no-hit and cold measurements, storage and eviction
evidence, normalized correctness parity in every mode, comparable fingerprint
accounting, no false hit, legacy/planner affected blocking-gate parity,
focused and repository-wide tests, documentation impact, research-control
validation, whitespace validation, and one governed checkpoint. Insufficient
live eligibility or comparability evidence must reject activation rather than
inflate the measured result.

## Outcome

PASS with activation rejected. Shared snapshots reduce repeated registry parses
with row-count parity. Warm exact-tree reuse saves work against cold publication
with zero false hits, but empty-lookup and cold overhead are material, the short
affected warm shape remains slower than cache-disabled execution, the live
manifest has no eligible gates, and the production runner supplies no cache
context. Cache mode therefore remains off by default. No production or physics
source changed.
