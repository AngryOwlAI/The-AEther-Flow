\section{Audit Object}

The audited object is the RT-042 repair candidate
\[
  \MSStableRepair(E;\Bcur)=
  \bigl(\MSSig,\MSRepEq,\MSRepProj,\MSRepDisc\bigr),
\]
where \(\MSSig(C)\) contains the stable source core, the source-syntax
readout-response coordinate \(\mathrm{RR}_E(C)\), and an admissibility flag.
The same candidate is named \(\MSIrrelRepair\) when considered as a response
to the earlier \(\mathrm{MSSemanticsQuotient}_{v1}\) target.

The audit does not convert the candidate into an adopted source law. It asks
only whether the written construction uses forbidden target-side or
process-authority premises.

\section{Audit Criteria}

The audit checks whether \(\MSSig\), \(\MSRepEq\), \(\MSRepProj\), or
\(\MSRepDisc\) uses any of the following as a mathematical premise:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{target geometry} & \text{target atlas, target metric, Lorentzian signature, proper time},\\
\text{target matter} & \text{detector semantics, stress-energy tensor, matter action},\\
\text{target dynamics} & \text{conservation law by GR/QFT import, Einstein-equation premise},\\
\text{benchmark authority} & \text{benchmark success, exact-GR fit as proof},\\
\text{process authority} & \text{registry, validator, role, handoff, approval, commit, generated derivative}.
\end{array}
\]
It also checks whether \(\mathrm{RR}_E(C)\) is used as detector semantics,
physical measurement semantics, stress-energy semantics, a matter action, a
coupling law, or proof authority rather than as source syntax.

\section{Audit Findings}

\begin{proposition}[No target-GR import detected as written]
As written, \(\MSStableRepair\) does not use target atlas, target metric,
Lorentzian signature, proper time normalization, detector semantics,
stress-energy tensor, matter action, benchmark success, or Einstein-equation
premises.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Reason.}
The repaired signature is defined from source records in
\(\MSAdm(E;\Bcur)\): a stable core, no-target certificate classes, sector
labels, and a source-syntax readout-response coordinate. No step requires a
target metric, target manifold chart, stress-energy tensor, detector protocol,
matter action, benchmark success, or field equation.

\begin{proposition}[Readout-response coordinate is source syntax only]
The coordinate \(\mathrm{RR}_E(C)\) is not a detector readout, empirical
measurement response, stress-energy datum, matter-action term, or coupling
law as written. It may be used only as an uninterpreted source-syntax
equivalence class.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Reason.}
RT-042 explicitly defines \(\mathrm{RR}_E(C)=(R_E,Q_E)/{\equiv_{\mathrm{src}}}\)
under source relabelings and states that it is not detector semantics, not
stress-energy semantics, not a matter action, and not a target-side
measurement interpretation. The audit therefore detects no hidden target
import in the written definition. The guard is essential: if a later packet
uses \(\mathrm{RR}_E\) as physical detector response or matter semantics, that
later use would be a forbidden semantic import.

\begin{proposition}[No process-authority laundering detected as written]
As written, the repair candidate does not use registry rows, generated wiki
notes, semantic extracts, Obsidian cache state, validators, roles, handoffs,
approvals, commits, file order, or current-frontier rendering as mathematical
premises.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Reason.}
Those surfaces establish routing, provenance, and validation. The mathematical
candidate is the RT-042 TeX definition of \(\MSSig\), \(\MSRepEq\),
\(\MSRepProj\), and \(\MSRepDisc\). Validation does not prove the quotient,
source-law adoption, matter coupling, or any downstream GR claim.

\begin{proposition}[No-target certificates remain guards, not evidence of matter semantics]
The certificate classes \(\NoDetector\), \(\NoSE\), \(\NoAction\), and
\(\NoTarget\) are used as source-side admissibility guards. They do not prove
detector absence as a physical theory, derive matter semantics, establish a
coupling law, construct stress-energy, or derive Einstein equations.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Reason.}
A no-target certificate can restrict which source records enter the finite
repair basis, but a restriction against importing detector, stress-energy, or
matter-action structure does not itself supply a positive source-side matter
semantics theorem.

\begin{proposition}[Stable partition gain is not an irrelevance theorem]
The repaired candidate avoids the RT-040 degeneracy by retaining
\(\mathrm{RR}_E\). This is source-pure as written, but it makes the candidate
a stable discriminator-like partition rather than a proof that readout and
response-marker differences are irrelevant to matter semantics.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Reason.}
The pressure pair \(C_+\) and \(C_-\) is no longer collapsed when
\(\mathrm{RR}_E(C_+)\neq\mathrm{RR}_E(C_-)\). This repairs nondegeneracy but
does not discharge the original irrelevance burden. It preserves the same
milestone continuation: the current ontology does not derive a source-side
law making readout/response differences irrelevant to matter semantics.

\section{Verdict}

The audit verdict is
\[
  \AuditPass.
\]
This means the repair candidate is source-pure as written under the
finite/local proposal-only boundary, provided that \(\mathrm{RR}_E\) remains
uninterpreted source syntax. It may proceed to one bounded Refuter stress
packet. It does not mean the candidate is adopted, canonical, physically
interpreted, source-extension data, ready for matter-coupling, ready for
Einstein-equation work, ready for benchmark promotion, or ready for Gate Chair
closure.
