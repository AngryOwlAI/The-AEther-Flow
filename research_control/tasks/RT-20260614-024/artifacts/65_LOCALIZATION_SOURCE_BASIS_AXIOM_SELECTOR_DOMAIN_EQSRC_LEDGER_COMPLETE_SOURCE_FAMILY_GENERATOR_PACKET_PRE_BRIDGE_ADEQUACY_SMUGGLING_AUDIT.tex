\begin{abstract}
This draft/control Smuggling Auditor artifact audits the
\texttt{RT-20260614-023} pre-bridge adequacy packet for hidden target
import and authority-label laundering. The audit finds no local positive
use of target metric, target proper time, target locality, observer

separation is a protective control surface rather than a promotion

example, one finite boundary example, and one failed-certificate

\section{Audit Findings}

\begin{auditfinding}[No target primitive import detected locally]
The adequacy target is defined through source-ledger transport,
negative controls, boundary cases, failed certificates, and source-side
readout constraints. The audit does not detect target metric, target
proper time, target locality, observer protocol, benchmark rank, or
candidate behavior as a positive primitive in the packet.
\end{auditfinding}

\begin{auditfinding}[Obligations are not evidence]
The packet supplies criteria and obligations, not completed examples or
bridge theorems. Reading the obligations as fulfilled evidence would be
authority laundering and returns \(\Block\).
\end{auditfinding}

\begin{auditfinding}[Bridge relevance remains unsupplied]
No concrete \(\ReadSrc\) and no theorem
\[
    X\EqLC Y \Longrightarrow \ReadSrc(X)=\ReadSrc(Y)

are supplied. Even a later source-side invariance theorem would still
need a separate audited bridge-relevance argument before any use toward
\(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), causal structure, clock behavior, matter
coupling, candidate reconstruction, or benchmark recovery.
\end{auditfinding}

needed for a positive result. They do not authorize global rejection of
the research program.
