## Review Design

The blind arm receives only two deterministic context-stripped JSON packets.
The same-context arm receives two hash-bound context-rich packets and may read
only the sources and QA controls named there. Both arms are internal AI review
classes. Shared model family, provider, repository, and code base remain
explicit; neither role diversity nor blindness is relabeled as external human
review or independent replication.

## Claim Boundary

The selector review may judge the written conditional theorem but cannot
derive or adopt its proposal inputs. The `g_eff` review may judge source-record
assembly and issue a missing-assumption certificate for any physical reading,
but cannot reject the scoped source record, adopt a metric, or derive coupling
or field equations. Workflow, checker, proof-build, prior-review, and consensus
success are never scientific premises.

## Validation

Completion requires deterministic packet rebuilds with zero context leaks,
strict review-artifact validation, at least two honestly supported review
classes, explicit reviewer access records, a preserved disagreement and
leakage analysis, a positive-provenance before/after comparison, exact proof
scope, semantic-smuggling checks, reviewer metrics, all-finding disposition,
claim and authority gates, affected research-control validation, and a
governed checkpoint.

Stop if a source hash drifts, a packet leaks process metadata, any internal arm
claims external or independent standing, a receipt is treated as proof, a
review edits its source, a physical claim is promoted, an edit escapes the
allowlist, or a required validation or checkpoint fails.
