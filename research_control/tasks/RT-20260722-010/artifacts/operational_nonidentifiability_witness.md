# Operational nonidentifiability witness for the scoped source record

## Boundary

This is a finite draft/control missing-assumption witness for a stronger
physical reading of the scoped ten-slot source record

\[
T=(D_E,Q_E,S_E,\Theta_E,N_E,B_E,F_E,C_E,O_E,L_E).
\]

It is not a countermodel to the conditional claim that supplied typed slots
form a source-side record. It does not reject that record, prove that no
source extension can work, adopt a metric, derive matter coupling or field
equations, or authorize any protected conclusion.

## Two-extension construction

Fix any record `T` satisfying every declared source-record condition. Add an
operational-result set `Y = {0, 1}` and a one-point protocol set `P = {*} `. An
operational enrichment would need, at minimum, a readout map

\[
R_T:P\longrightarrow Y.
\]

Define two enrichments of exactly the same source reduct:

\[
R_T^{(0)}(*)=0,
\qquad
R_T^{(1)}(*)=1.
\]

They agree on all ten fields of `T`. The current candidate definition names no
protocol set, readout map, response law, or equation constraining either
enrichment, so its assumptions do not distinguish them. Equivalently, the
forgetful projection from such operationally enriched records to the ten-slot
source record is noninjective under the current assumption set.

## Exact result

The source record alone does not identify a unique operational meaning. A
physical effective-metric reading therefore needs additional source-side
structure such as a defined protocol/readout or response map, its typing and
naturality laws, and evidence connecting it to the proposed interpretation.
The witness establishes only `not_enough_assumptions` for that stronger
reading; it leaves the narrow record-assembly claim intact.
