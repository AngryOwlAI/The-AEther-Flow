\section{Fail-Closed Stress Lemma}

\textbf{Lemma.} For the audited theorem candidate \(\Pi_{\mathrm{int}}(E)\) as
written, each Phase 8B stress branch either satisfies an explicit source-side
non-bottom hypothesis or terminates in a named obstruction surface. None of
the stress branches authorizes \(\Msrc\) adoption, full \(\Msrc\)
construction, \(g_{\mathrm{eff}}\), matter coupling, Einstein equations, or
benchmark promotion.

\textbf{Reason.} The mathematical branches are guarded by H1--H8 and H11:
carrier, coverage, chart-support, transition, separation/countability/
regularity, conditional soldering, conditional finite-variation, and bottom
discipline are all explicit conditions. The authority branches are guarded by
H9, H10, H12, and H13: target repair, finite/local globalization, process
authority, and adoption-language collapse are forbidden. Therefore a malformed
branch cannot be converted into an adopted \(\Msrc\) conclusion without
changing the object under review.

The lemma is conditional. It does not prove that H1--H13 are canonically
available, and it does not decide the protected adoption question.
