\begin{abstract}
This packet turns the fixed-domain selector theorem and its stress record into
an actionable, machine-checkable taxonomy.  A complete burden record receives
exactly one primary class: added source structure, representative irrelevance,
dynamical symmetry breaking, probabilistic selection, or scoped obstruction.
The classification is by the scientific result actually established, not by a
label chosen after the fact.  Supporting mechanisms may overlap, but every
overlap must be explicit and every added assumption must be costed.  The packet
is proposal-only source-extension control data.  It changes no ontology or
scientific ledger and grants no physics, proof-adoption, publication, or global
no-go authority.
\end{abstract}

\section{Authority, fixed inputs, and purpose}

The fixed baseline is the proposal-only source groupoid and eligible-choice
functor used by P2-T01 through P2-T08.  On a component representative $r_i$,
the P2-T03 classification identifies deterministic natural selectors with
automorphism-fixed choices
\[
  F_i=S_\chi(r_i)^{\Aut_{\G}(r_i)},
  \qquad
  \Sel(\G)\cong\prod_i F_i.
\]
The P2-T07 stress packet established that added marks, quotient-level
observables, dynamical inputs, and invariant probability laws change the
question or its inputs; they do not refute the fixed-domain theorem.  P2-T08
found no scoped mathematical contradiction while keeping external human
review pending and every adoption boundary intact.

The present purpose is narrower than selecting a physical theory.  It defines
the evidence needed to say what kind of resolution a future packet has
actually established.  Current ontology does not derive or adopt $\G$,
$S_\chi$, the relation interface $K$, any selector-relevant source extension,
any probability law, or any selection dynamics.  Structural automorphisms are
not thereby physical gauge transformations.

\section{Typed burden records}

\begin{definition}[Complete selector-resolution burden]
A burden record $B$ consists of
\[
 B=(D_0,H,S,K,V,T,M,E,\Delta A,\mathcal U),
\]
where $D_0$ is the hash-bound baseline source domain; $H$ is its declared
morphism or symmetry scope; $S$ is the eligible-choice assignment; $K$ is the
downstream observable or relation interface when one is used; $V$ is the
variation class; $T$ is exactly one requested result kind; $M$ is the claimed
mechanism; $E$ is the evidence set; $\Delta A$ is the assumption delta; and
$\mathcal U$ records authority flags.  The allowed values of $T$ are:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
  \item downstream observable or relation;
  \item deterministic point or representative;
  \item probability law or Markov kernel;
  \item dynamically selected state or sector; or
  \item scoped disposition.
\end{enumerate}
Missing baseline hashes, ambiguous target type, incomplete evidence, or an
uncosted assumption delta makes the record unclassifiable rather than
silently defaulting it to a class.
\end{definition}

Every record separately reports definition, existence, uniqueness,
naturality, covariance, robustness, dynamics, operational meaning, empirical
status, and authority.  A result on one axis does not entail a result on any
other.  In particular, formal naturality does not establish physical
covariance; existence does not establish uniqueness; and validation does not
establish adoption.

\section{The five lawful primary classes}

\subsection{Added source structure (AS)}

AS applies when a prospectively declared, target-neutral static extension
$p:D_1\to D_0$ supplies new source data and a deterministic natural selector
exists on $D_1$ because of that datum.  Necessary evidence is:
\begin{enumerate}[label=AS\arabic*.]
  \item the baseline and extended signatures plus a reduct or forgetful map;
  \item independent source provenance for the added datum;
  \item the induced change in automorphisms, morphisms, or stabilizers;
  \item a selector construction and naturality proof on the extended domain;
  \item an assumption-strength and minimality cost; and
  \item a no-target check showing that the datum was not populated by reading
        the desired downstream answer.
\end{enumerate}
Writing the answer into a mark is mathematically sufficient on the marked
domain but scientifically circular unless the mark has independent source
meaning.  AS therefore records a conditional source extension, not a
derivation from the baseline ontology.

\subsection{Representative irrelevance (RI)}

RI applies when the downstream object is proved independent of every
admissible representative or selector.  If $K:S\Rightarrow E$ is the declared
relation interface, sufficient evidence is a natural factorization
\[
  S\longrightarrow S/{\sim}\xrightarrow{\bar K}E
\]
or an equivalent proof that $K\circ\sigma=K\circ\sigma'$ for all admissible
selectors $\sigma,\sigma'$.  The admissible selector domain must be nonempty
when a unique image is claimed.  Agreement of a sample, or merely naming the
symmetry ``gauge,'' is not evidence.  RI removes the downstream point-choice
burden; it does not prove that a point selector exists or that structural
symmetry is physical gauge.

\subsection{Dynamical symmetry breaking (DSB)}

DSB applies when an explicit source-side evolution law and declared state,
initial, boundary, or phase data select a state, orbit, phase, or sector.  A
qualifying packet supplies:
\begin{enumerate}[label=DSB\arabic*.]
  \item a source state space and symmetry action;
  \item an evolution law or action with a well-posedness regime;
  \item provenance for all initial, boundary, and state data;
  \item a selection or convergence theorem in an exact regime;
  \item stability and robustness information; and
  \item equivariance, or an explicit statement of how and where symmetry is
        broken.
\end{enumerate}
A static minimizer is not a dynamical selection result.  A branch hidden in
initial data is an added assumption and must be reported.  DSB does not show
that current ontology derives the dynamics or the state data.
When an equivariant law produces a non-invariant realized state, the stronger
symmetry-breaking label is appropriate on the declared state-data regime.  If
that condition is not shown, the packet must use the weaker description
``dynamics-mediated selection'' while retaining DSB only as the normalized
route class; the class label cannot strengthen the mathematical claim.

\subsection{Probabilistic selection (PS)}

PS applies when the established result is a normalized source-defined
probability measure or Markov kernel.  The evidence includes measurable
choice spaces, measure or kernel provenance, normalization, naturality or
invariance, uniqueness status, and sampling semantics.  An invariant measure
is a distributional result, not a canonical point.  A realized sample depends
on additional random input unless another source law is supplied.  Uniformity
is not free outside a declared finite-symmetry or other source-side measure
principle.

\subsection{Scoped obstruction (SO)}

SO applies when a theorem or exhaustive bounded countermodel establishes
failure for an exact domain, morphism or variation class, mechanism, and
result kind, while no positive class is established in the same packet.  It
must state the hypotheses, proof or exhaustive search, failed positive
predicates, and exact reopening criteria.  The lawful conclusion is
``no such resolution exists under this declared scope,'' not ``no future
source extension or theory can resolve the burden.''  Missing local data is
not an obstruction theorem.

\section{Normalization and overlap}

Define evidence predicates $P_{\rm RI}$, $P_{\rm PS}$, $P_{\rm DSB}$,
$P_{\rm AS}$, and $P_{\rm SO}$ by the necessary evidence above, each typed to
its corresponding requested result kind.  The primary-class normal form is:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
  \item choose RI when the downstream burden is eliminated without selecting
        a representative;
  \item otherwise choose PS when the established result is a probability law
        or kernel;
  \item otherwise choose DSB when explicit evolution establishes a selected
        state or sector;
  \item otherwise choose AS when a deterministic point selector depends on a
        declared static source extension;
  \item otherwise choose SO only for a proved scoped negative disposition;
  \item otherwise return \texttt{unclassified} and fail closed.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{proposition}[Unique primary classification]
For every complete burden record with exactly one requested result kind and
with the corresponding evidence predicate satisfied, the ordered normal form
returns exactly one primary class.  Conversely, an ambiguous target, an
unsupported predicate, or a record satisfying no typed branch is rejected.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Each step is guarded by a distinct requested-result type.  The first passing
step terminates, so at most one primary class is returned.  Completeness and
the stated evidence premise ensure that the branch for the record's unique
type passes, so one class is returned.  If the type is ambiguous or its
evidence predicate fails, the input contract requires rejection rather than a
fallback label.
\end{proof}

Mechanisms can still overlap.  A probability measure is often added source
structure, but PS is primary because the result is a law, not a point.
Evolution and initial data are additions, but DSB is primary when the claimed
selection is dynamically produced.  A deterministic point selector may be
obstructed while the downstream observable is representative-independent; RI
is then primary for that downstream burden and SO may be supporting evidence
about the unused point target.  Every supporting class requires an overlap
rationale and its own assumption cost.

\section{Assumption-cost ledger}

Every nonempty $\Delta A$ records an identifier, introduced structure, source
provenance, current-ontology derivation status, effect on the declared
symmetry, target-dependence check, necessity or minimality status, and a
reopening or reversal condition.  ``Natural,'' ``symmetric,'' ``minimal,''
and ``physical'' are conclusions to justify, not free labels.  The cost ledger
prevents a class name from laundering an ontology edit or target import.
Costs are also retained as a componentwise vector over source signature,
selector or readout law, state or boundary data, dynamics, probability,
variation class, set-theoretic choice, empirical calibration, target-import
risk, and human authority.  Unlike components are not collapsed to an
unsupported scalar ordering; incomparable extensions remain incomparable for
P3-T02 to analyze.

\section{Five worked controls}

The companion machine record contains one control per class.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{AS: marked two-choice swap.}  The unmarked $C_2$ action on
        $\{+,-\}$ has empty fixed locus.  Adding a prospectively declared mark
        $m$ and restricting morphisms to preserve it makes $\sigma(X,m)=m$
        natural on the extended domain.  The mark is not ontology-derived,
        and answer-recording remains disallowed.
  \item \textbf{RI: constant relation map.}  Two admissible selectors remain,
        but $K(a)=K(b)=R$.  The induced relation factors through a singleton
        quotient and needs no representative choice.
  \item \textbf{DSB: symmetric double well.}  For
        $\dot y=y-y^3$, nonzero initial data converge to the stable branch
        with matching sign; $y=0$ is unstable.  The equations are
        sign-equivariant, and the initial datum, dynamics, and stability
        regime are explicit toy inputs.
  \item \textbf{PS: transitive three-cycle.}  The $C_3$ action on three points
        has no invariant point but has the unique invariant probability law
        assigning mass $1/3$ to each point.  A sampled point is not a
        deterministic natural selector.
  \item \textbf{SO: fixed two-choice swap.}  The nontrivial $C_2$ swap has no
        fixed point, hence no deterministic invariant point selector on that
        exact domain.  Source extension, quotient irrelevance, dynamics, and
        probability-law targets are explicit reopening routes, not refutations
        of the scoped result.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Route consequences and limitations}

The taxonomy makes P3-T02 dependency-ready: candidate additions can now be
ordered by assumption strength and symmetry reduction using the AS, DSB, and
PS evidence fields.  It does not execute that ordering.  P3-T03 still owns the
actual representative-irrelevance determinations and depends on P3-T02.
P3-T04 still owns construction and testing of probability or dynamics
alternatives and also depends on P3-T02.

No class is adopted by label.  No class reopens or rejects a frozen family,
changes the Distance-to-GR or metric-use ledgers, edits canonical ontology,
establishes physical gauge, discharges general EqSrc, derives a metric or
Einstein dynamics, promotes the exact-GR benchmark, authorizes publication,
or establishes a global no-go.  The only new mathematical payload is the
typed five-branch normal form and its unique-primary proposition under the
declared completeness contract.
