## Basis

`RT-20260630-044` records a scoped obstruction to adopting the repaired object
as a readout/response irrelevance law. The repair retains `RR_E` as a separating
coordinate, so it cannot identify records that differ only in `RR_E`. That
retention blocks the irrelevance-law reading.

The same stress result also shows a narrower surviving reading: under explicit
`RR_E` source syntax and core-retention guards, the repair behaves as a
deterministic finite/local stable source partition or precondition candidate.
The lowest-authority constructive continuation is therefore not adoption and
not a repeat stress test. It is a formalization packet that states exactly what
the stable-partition/precondition target is, what it proves, and what it still
does not prove.
