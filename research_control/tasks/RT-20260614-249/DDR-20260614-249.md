## Reasoning

The recovery-bridge candidate evidence is now accepted only as scoped
source-extension evidence/precondition. That status is not a coupling law, not
matter coupling, and not stress-energy semantics. A broader protected adoption
review would be premature because the repository still lacks a draft/control
source-side criteria surface that says what a stress-energy-interface bridge
would have to satisfy without importing target stress-energy, matter action,
detector semantics, `MetricData(E)`, or Einstein equations.

bounded `ontology-formalizer@0.2.0` packet to formalize source-side
stress-energy-interface criteria. The criteria packet may use the accepted
coupling-law-candidate evidence and recovery-bridge-candidate evidence only as
scoped source-extension preconditions. It may not adopt stress-energy
semantics or derive matter coupling.
