\section{Fail-Closed Lemma}

\begin{lemma}[All eight examples fail closed]
Each listed negative attempt has a non-null fail-closed reason and blocks
declared source equivalence for that attempted record.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
SCI-NEG-MISSING-001 has no certificate payload. SCI-NEG-MALFORMED-001 leaves
the declared codomain. SCI-NEG-FACTOR-CHANGE-001 changes the middle factor
object after the witness is declared. SCI-NEG-TARGET-METRIC-001 imports target
metric data. SCI-NEG-DETECTOR-001 imports detector semantics.
SCI-NEG-STRESS-ENERGY-001 imports stress-energy data. SCI-NEG-VALIDATOR-PASS-001
uses process status as payload. SCI-NEG-SCOPED-EVIDENCE-001 expands scoped
finite evidence into adoption. These are precisely the defects forbidden by
the P4 schema, so each attempt is rejected before any declared equivalence,
source-law adoption, or downstream physics claim can be inferred.
\end{proof}

\section{Scope Boundary}

\begin{nonconclusion}[No global no-go claim]
These eight examples are bounded negative fixtures. They show that these
specific malformed or imported attempts fail under the P4 schema. They do not
show that all future source extensions fail, they do not reject the project
globally, and they do not close any derivation route by theorem.
\end{nonconclusion}

\begin{boundary}[No promotion]
The packet records only fail-closed examples. It does not adopt source laws,
matter semantics, detector semantics, coupling laws, stress-energy semantics,
matter actions, Einstein equations, benchmark behavior, Gate Chair verdicts,
or completed derivations.
\end{boundary}
