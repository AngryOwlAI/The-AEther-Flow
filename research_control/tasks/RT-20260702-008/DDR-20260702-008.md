# DDR-20260702-008: v14 P6-T01 Three-Tier Claim Convention Policy

## Decision

Route one bounded P6-T01 `claim_convention_policy_packet` to
`documentation-curator@2.0.0` through a task overlay.

## Basis

`handoff-0460` requires one bounded v14 P6-T01 three-tier claim convention
policy packet before downstream physics routes. The v14 plan requires a policy
for completions, handoffs, current-frontier summaries, public status surfaces,
and final audits that distinguishes:

- adopted objects;
- accepted evidence/preconditions;
- open or blocked physical targets.

The live resolver reports `ready`, active task `RT-20260702-007`, latest
handoff `handoff-0460`, and an execution boundary of one bounded AgentJob per
invocation. The packet is documentation-control policy work, not physics
derivation.

## Selected Role

`documentation-curator@2.0.0` is selected because the deliverable is a
canonical control policy for summary language and reader-facing claim
discipline. A one-job overlay narrows the role to policy authoring,
research-control state updates, registry updates, and generated-system
synchronization.

## Scope

The AgentJob creates and registers:

- `research_control/design/three_tier_claim_convention.md`;
- task-local P6-T01 receipts and completion records;
- handoff `handoff-0461`;
- live program state routing to P6-T02.

It must not update completion or handoff schemas, pilot current-frontier
three-tier rendering, rewrite public surfaces, or edit generated HTML. Those
remain separate downstream packets.
