# DDR-20260702-036: v14 P11 Narrow Theorem Selector

## Decision

Route one bounded P11-T03 selector packet to
`theoretical-continuation-selector@0.1.0` through a task overlay.

## Basis

`handoff-0488` requires a narrow theorem target selector before any direct
theorem or precondition execution. The P11-T01 moratorium blocks direct
universal matter-coupling derivation unless separate tracked authority supplies
the missing prerequisites. The P11-T02 checklist requires no-target hygiene and
`RR_E` separation checks before future adoption-facing matter-sector routes.

## Selected Role

The theoretical continuation selector is selected because the deliverable is a
route decision among narrow theoretical packets. It does not modify canonical
science sources and does not promote claims.

## Scope

Allowed:

- rank the P11-T03 candidate narrow routes;
- select one next theorem or precondition route;
- preserve the plan-required P11-T04 task-template step before theorem
  execution.

Forbidden:

- derive universal matter coupling;
- adopt source laws, matter semantics, detector semantics, coupling laws,
  stress-energy semantics, matter action, Einstein equations, benchmark status,
  or completed derivation;
- treat negative no-target certificates as positive matter theory;
- execute the selected theorem route inside this selector transaction.

## Route Decision

The selected route is the no-target certificate hygiene theorem. This is the
lowest-authority constructive precondition available under the current P11
state. It clarifies that no-target, no-detector, no-stress-energy, no-action,
no-benchmark, and no-process-authority certificates block imports only; they
do not supply positive matter semantics, detector semantics, stress-energy
semantics, matter action, or coupling laws.

The next bounded packet remains P11-T04 by `documentation-curator@2.0.0`,
because the plan requires a reusable narrow-theorem task template before P12
executes the hygiene doctrine.
