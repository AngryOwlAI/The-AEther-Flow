## Rationale

The P3-T02 theorem candidate is conditional on a declared closed family. In
that narrow setting, `RetainH` and `GenH` are not theorem premises: the family,
its witnesses, and its invariant-ledger stability are already supplied.

The same result exposes two extension pressures:

- an `H`-retention extension needs a source-side rule saying which record,
  certificate, invariant-ledger, forbidden-channel, proxy-edge, comparison, and
  negative-control entries are preserved by an `H`-indexed retention operation;
- an `H`-generated family extension needs a source-side rule saying which
  generated objects and morphisms are admitted into the source family and which
  ledger witnesses accompany them.

The current packet may classify those as candidate-definition boundaries, but
it may not adopt either primitive or convert the boundaries into source laws.
