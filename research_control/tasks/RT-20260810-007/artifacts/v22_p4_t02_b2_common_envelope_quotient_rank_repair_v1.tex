\section{Exact response-image repair}

Put
\[
 a=k(u),\qquad b=k(z).
\]
In the declared bookkeeping trivialization,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:response}
 \Resp(k)=(a+\epsilon b,\ a,\ a-\epsilon b).
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Typed image, rank, and kernel]\label{thm:image}
The relabelled response map has image
\[
 H=\{(r_R,r_S,r_D)\in\RR^3:r_R+r_D=2r_S\},
\]
rank two, and kernel
\[
 \ker(\Resp)=\ann(\operatorname{span}\{u,z\}).
\]
Because $\dim V=4$, the kernel has dimension two.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Equation~\eqref{eq:response} gives
$(a+\epsilon b)+(a-\epsilon b)=2a$, so the image lies in $H$.
The conditions $h_0(u)=1$ and $h_0(z)=0$ with $z\ne0$ imply that $u,z$ are
independent.  Consequently the evaluation map
$k\mapsto(k(u),k(z))$ is onto $\RR^2$.  The responses at $(a,b)=(1,0)$ and
$(0,1/\epsilon)$ are $(1,1,1)$ and $(1,0,-1)$, which form a basis of $H$.
Thus the image equals $H$ and the rank is two.  Equation~\eqref{eq:response}
vanishes exactly when $a=b=0$, which is precisely the annihilator stated.
Rank--nullity gives the final dimension.
\end{proof}

This theorem closes the RT006 repair burden.  The three written response
coordinates are linearly constrained: the exact candidate image cannot retain

\section{Positive-projective image: one coordinate and a sign sheet}

Let
\[
 D=\{k\in V^*: \bar p_R(k)\bar p_S(k)\bar p_D(k)\ne0\}.
\]
Define the fixed-representative positive-scale quotient
\[
 \Qray(k)=[\Resp(k)]_{\RR_{>0}}.
\]
On $D$, $a=r_S\ne0$.  Therefore the two quantities
\[
 \sigma=\sgn(a)\in\{+1,-1\},
 \qquad
 t=\epsilon\frac{b}{a}
   =\frac{r_R-r_D}{2r_S}
\]
are defined, and $t\ne\pm1$ because either value makes one outer response
zero.

\begin{theorem}[Exact positive-ray atlas]\label{thm:ray}
The image of $\Qray$ on $D$ is in bijection with
\[
 \{(\sigma,t):\sigma\in\{+1,-1\},\ t\in\RR\setminus\{-1,+1\}\}.
\]
After division by $|a|$, the corresponding ray has representative
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ray}
 [\Resp(k)]_+
   =[\sigma(1+t),\ \sigma,\ \sigma(1-t)]_+.
\end{equation}
Hence each sheet has one continuous dimension.  Positive projectivization does
not identify the two $\sigma$ sheets.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Equation~\eqref{eq:response} equals
$a(1+t,1,1-t)$.  Dividing by the positive number $|a|$ gives
Equation~\eqref{eq:ray}.  Conversely, for any admitted $(\sigma,t)$ choose
$a=\sigma$ and $b=ta/\epsilon$; independence of $u,z$ supplies a realizing
covector.  If two normalized representatives lie on the same positive ray,
their middle coordinates force the same $\sigma$ and scale one, after which
the outer coordinates force the same $t$.
\end{proof}

The often-written expression $[1+t,1,1-t]_+$ covers only the
$\sigma=+1$ sheet.  The repaired statement is one continuous projective
coordinate \emph{plus} the discrete sheet, not a globally sheet-free $t$.
The locus $a=0$ lies outside $D$ because $r_S=0$.  In the projective closure of
the full response plane it may be covered by a complementary $b\ne0$ chart,
where it gives the boundary direction $[1,0,-1]$ or its opposite.  That closure
record is not part of either quotient on $D$.

\section{Exact six-cell sign image}

The coarser quotient is
\[
 \Qsign(k)=[\Resp(k)]_{(\RR_{>0})^3}.
\]
It retains only the ordered signs under independent positive sector scaling.

\begin{proposition}[Six and only six nonzero sign cells]
On $D$, the image of $\Qsign$ is
\[
 \{---,\ --+,\ -++,\ +--,\ ++-,\ +++\}.
\]
The alternating cells $-+-$ and $+-+$ are not realized.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For $\sigma=+1$, Equation~\eqref{eq:ray} gives
\[
 t<-1:\ -++,\qquad -1<t<1:\ +++,\qquad t>1:\ ++-.
\]
For $\sigma=-1$, every sign reverses, giving respectively
$+--$, $---$, and $--+$.  Every interval is nonempty, so all six cells occur.
At $t=-1$ or $t=+1$ one outer response vanishes and the point leaves $D$.
The interval classification is exhaustive and contains neither alternating
pattern.  Equivalently, three nonzero entries satisfying
$r_R+r_D=2r_S$ cannot have their middle sign opposite to both outer signs.
\end{proof}
