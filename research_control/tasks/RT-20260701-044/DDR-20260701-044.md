## Rationale

The latest tracked handoff requires a P4-T03 renderer and handoff update before
latest-state validation backfill, public status propagation, or downstream
physics routes. P4-T02 introduced the schema split and a basic renderer table.
The logical next step is to make that display explicit enough that readers and
future handoff authors can distinguish validator receipts from protected
scoped authorization and downstream scientific-promotion authorization.

The user's standing goal authorizes human gates needed for the implementation
plan. This decision records that authorization only for the protected
project-control renderer and template guidance update. It does not authorize
ontology edits, source-law adoption, Gate Chair verdicts, benchmark promotion,
downstream physics promotion, or completed-derivation claims.
