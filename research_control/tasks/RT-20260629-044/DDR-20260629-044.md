# Director Decision Record: DDR-20260629-044

## Decision

Execute P4-T02 as one bounded `ontology-formalizer@0.2.0` task overlay.
The task formalizes the source-extension minimization target over the P4-T01
dependency stack.

## Basis

`handoff-0338` selected P4-T02 after P4-T01 extracted the dependency stack for
`Resp_lc`, `M_src`, scoped `g_eff`, and matter-coupling precondition evidence.
The P4 plan requires a target definition before any Candidate Constructor may
attempt compression or produce an obstruction. The target must remain
source-side and must not import target metric, Lorentzian signature,
stress-energy, matter action, detector semantics, Einstein equations, or
benchmark behavior.

## Milestone And Burden

Target derivation milestone: `matter_coupling`.

Milestone burden: formalize a no-target-import-preserving compression target
for the existing source-extension stack, including derivability,
conservativity, irreducibility, target-import risk, minimal source-law package
criteria, and scoped failure-obstruction conditions.

## Scope

Allowed:

- read the v12 plan, P4-T01 dependency extraction and table, current frontier,
  and GR burden map;
- define `SourceExtensionStack_v1`, `ExtensionDatum(E)`,
  `DerivableDatum(E)`, `ConservativeExtension(E)`,
  `IrreduciblePrimitiveCandidate(E)`, `TargetImportRisk(E)`,

  `NoTargetImportPreservingCompression_v1`, and
  `CompressionFailureObstruction_v1`;
- define allowed and non-allowed conclusions for P4-T03;
- preserve parent-child synthesis receipts;
- update normal research-control receipts, registries, handoff, and generated
  memory/dependency derivatives.

Blocked:

- constructing a compression candidate;
- proving a compression obstruction or minimal countermodel;
- source-law adoption or canonical ontology edit;
- `MetricData(E)` adoption;
- `g_eff` scope change or unscoped `g_eff` adoption;

  semantics;
- Einstein-equation derivation;

- completed-derivation claim;
- global source-extension impossibility or global theory rejection.
