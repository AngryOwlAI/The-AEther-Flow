\section{Status and exact question}

This artifact is \texttt{draft/control} benchmark-protocol data.  It defines
how later source-derived recovery cases must be run; it does not run any case.
The P8-T07 protected decision remains
\texttt{NOT\_READY\_EINSTEIN\_SECTOR\_DERIVATION\_REQUIREMENTS\_UNMET}.
No benchmark result, Gate E verdict, exact-GR recovery, or physics promotion is
created here.

The exact P9-T01 question is:
\begin{quote}
How can a benchmark compare a source-derived output with an adopted GR target
without letting the target atlas, metric, solution, action, equations,
boundary data, tuning information, or desired agreement create the source
output being tested?
\end{quote}

The answer is a two-stage contract: a source computation is executed and
cryptographically sealed first; only then is a read-only target oracle opened
for comparison.  A provenance graph makes every upstream dependency auditable.

\section{Typed benchmark record}

\begin{definition}[Benchmark case]
A benchmark case is the tuple
\[
 \mathfrak B =
 (I_{\rm src},I_{\rm ext},C,A,X,O_{\rm src},
  \Seal,\Oracle,O_{\rm tgt},E,\tau,R,\mathcal F).
\]
The fields have the following types.
\begin{longtable}{p{0.18\linewidth}p{0.74\linewidth}}
\toprule
Field & Required meaning\\
\midrule
\(I_{\rm src}\) &
Exact source-derived inputs, each with authority status, domain, units or
explicitly dimensionless status, repository path, and SHA-256.\\
\(I_{\rm ext}\) &
Explicit proposal-only source extensions or independent calibrations.  Each
must disclose that it is not derived from current ontology and must be chosen
without target tuning.\\
\(C\) &
Exact code and algorithm manifest, including executable version, environment,
randomness policy, and hashes.\\
\(A\) &
Declared approximation regime and order, with a source-side validity domain.
If no controlled regime is available, the case is inconclusive before target
comparison.\\
\(X\) &
Ordered derived-intermediate manifest.  Every intermediate has typed inputs,
an algorithm or theorem identity, output type, and provenance parents.\\
\(O_{\rm src}\) &
The source output and uncertainty or exact-arithmetic record.  Physical
meaning may be asserted only if a separately established model-to-world map
supplies it.\\
\(\mathsf M_{\rm world}\) &
A typed model-to-world map from source states and formal tokens to physical
preparations, quantities, units, interventions, measurement records,
uncertainties, and failure modes.  If absent, a structural comparison remains
formal or physically inconclusive.\\
\(\Seal\) &
An immutable manifest containing hashes of all source roots, code,
intermediates, outputs, approximations, tolerances, and preregistered
comparison rules.\\
\(\Oracle\) &
A read-only target-reference loader that is unavailable until the seal
verifies.  Canonical GR sources define comparison targets only.\\
\(O_{\rm tgt}\) &
The target observable or equivalence class loaded after sealing.\\
\(E\) &
An analytic equivalence relation or a dimensionless residual functional
defined before source execution.\\
\(\tau\) &
A preregistered numerical tolerance with units, error budget, calibration
basis, and uncertainty rule.  An unset or target-tuned tolerance makes the
case inconclusive or fail-closed.\\
\(R\) &
Declared residual corrections, truncation errors, finite-size effects, and
their bounds.  Uncontrolled corrections cannot be silently normalized away.\\
\(\mathcal F\) &
Outcome classifier with \texttt{PASS}, \texttt{FAIL\_COMPARISON},
\texttt{FAIL\_FIREWALL}, \texttt{FAIL\_SOURCE},
\texttt{INCONCLUSIVE}, and \texttt{NOT\_RUN} branches.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
\end{definition}

\section{Provenance graph and stage separation}

\begin{definition}[Provenance DAG]
Let \(G=(V,E)\) be a finite directed acyclic graph.  An edge \(u\to v\)
means that the value or selection of \(v\) depends on \(u\).  Partition the
vertices into:
\[
 V=V_{\rm adm}\sqcup V_{\rm forbidden}\sqcup V_{\rm src}
   \sqcup\{\Seal\}\sqcup V_{\rm target}\sqcup V_{\rm cmp}.
\]
\(V_{\rm adm}\) contains fixed source roots, disclosed proposal-only source
extensions, independently justified calibration roots, and hashed code.
\(V_{\rm forbidden}\) contains target atlas, metric, solution,
Einstein--Hilbert action, Einstein equations, target boundary data, target
residuals, desired benchmark result, target-tuned parameters, and target-based
branch or stopping choices.  \(V_{\rm src}\) contains source-stage
intermediates and outputs.  \(V_{\rm target}\) and \(V_{\rm cmp}\) exist only
after the seal.
\end{definition}

The firewall requires
\[
 \Reach_G(V_{\rm forbidden})\cap V_{\rm src}=\varnothing ,
 \qquad
 \Reach_G(V_{\rm target})\cap
 (V_{\rm src}\cup\{\Seal\})=\varnothing.
\]
Allowed cross-stage edges point only from sealed source output and the seal
into comparison nodes.  There is no comparison-to-source feedback edge.
Tolerances, equivalence relations, parameter ranges, stopping rules, and
failure classifiers are sealed before the target oracle opens.

\begin{theorem}[TargetImportFirewallSoundness\_v1]
Let \(G\) satisfy the partition and reachability conditions above.  Suppose:
\begin{enumerate}
\item every source-stage node is a deterministic function of its parents, or
      uses randomness whose distribution and seed policy are sealed;
\item every parent edge is complete, so undeclared side channels are absent;
\item source outputs and the full source manifest are immutable after
      \(\Seal\);
\item the target oracle is unavailable before the seal verifies; and
\item comparison nodes cannot alter any source-stage value, branch, tolerance,
      or stopping rule.
\end{enumerate}
Then \(O_{\rm src}\) is invariant under arbitrary changes to target-reference
roots that preserve the source-stage roots.  Consequently, a later agreement
between \(O_{\rm src}\) and \(O_{\rm tgt}\) cannot be caused by a declared
upstream target import.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Choose a topological ordering of \(G\).  Every source-stage root lies in
\(V_{\rm adm}\), and no forbidden or target root reaches a source-stage node.
Induct along the ordering.  If all parents of a source node are invariant
under a change of target roots, its deterministic value is invariant; the
same conclusion holds for a randomized node because its distribution and
seed policy are fixed by invariant sealed parents.  Thus every source-stage
node, including \(O_{\rm src}\), is invariant.  Immutability at \(\Seal\)
prevents later target or comparison nodes from revising that result.  The
agreement classifier may change when the target changes, but the tested source
output cannot.  Hence declared target data cannot be the upstream cause of the
source output or its agreement.
\end{proof}

This theorem is necessary protocol discipline, not a recovery theorem.  It
does not prove that the edge manifest is complete in an untrusted
implementation, that a source-extension premise follows from canonical
ontology, that an operational token has physical meaning, or that any case
will agree with GR.  Code, lookup tables, training data, priors, boundary
records, or calibration objects containing target information must be
reclassified as forbidden roots even if their filenames say ``source'' or
``code.''  The theorem also cannot erase target knowledge already held by a
human designer; every such exposure belongs in a target-exposure ledger and
limits any claim of blindness.

\begin{proposition}[Minimal rejected counterexample]
Suppose a target metric or target solution fixes a source boundary value,
initial condition, free coefficient, branch, tolerance, or stopping rule.
Then the provenance graph contains a path from a forbidden target root to a
source-stage node.  The firewall rejects the case before comparison, even if
the resulting output agrees exactly with the target.
\end{proposition}

\section{Comparison rules}

\subsection{Analytic equivalence}

When an exact relation is available, a case must preregister the quotient or
equivalence relation---for example, equality after a declared source-derived
presentation map.  Analytic equivalence passes only if the independently
produced source output belongs to the target equivalence class and every
domain, regularity, unit, gauge, and boundary obligation is satisfied.
Renaming a source object with a target label is not analytic equivalence.

\subsection{Numerical comparison}

When a numerical comparison is necessary, the default normalized residual is
\[
 \rho =
 \frac{\|O_{\rm src}-O_{\rm tgt}\|_W}
 {\max(s_{\rm prereg},\|O_{\rm tgt}\|_W,\epsilon_{\rm floor})}.
\]
The norm \(W\), preregistered scale \(s_{\rm prereg}\), floor,
sampling domain, uncertainty propagation, and threshold \(\tau\) are case
fields.  P9-T01 assigns no physical numerical tolerance.  A later case must
justify its value independently of the observed target mismatch and seal it
before execution.  Missing, altered, or post-hoc tolerance data yield
\texttt{INCONCLUSIVE} or \texttt{FAIL\_FIREWALL}, never a pass.

\subsection{Residual corrections}

Every finite-size, coarse-graining, discretization, truncation, boundary,
calibration, or stochastic correction is recorded separately.  A comparison
passes only if the residual plus its uncertainty lies within the preregistered
criterion and every correction has a source-side bound.  Uncontrolled
corrections remain visible and block a decisive pass.

\section{Independent reproduction}

A qualifying reproduction uses a fresh executor or team, a fresh working
environment, independently established code or method provenance, exact
immutable source hashes, and no copied intermediate or output files.  It
receives the sealed protocol, not the primary run's internal state.  It must
independently reconstruct the provenance graph, verify the firewall, execute
the source stage, seal its output, and open the same target oracle only
afterward.  Agreement between primary and reproduction source outputs is
distinct from agreement with the target.  Same-context role separation or a
rerun of identical code in the same environment is not independent
replication.

\section{Suite calibration, target exposure, and semantic status}

Calibration inputs, validation inputs, and held-out benchmark inputs are
separate sets.  A quantity fitted to one target case is labeled calibrated,
frozen suite-wide before any later held-out oracle opens, and is not counted
as an independent prediction in its calibration case.  Any later retuning
creates a new suite version and invalidates the earlier blind-test label.
Proposal-only source-extension ancestry and target exposure propagate to every
descendant and to the final classification.

Known target structures may legitimately determine which questions are
tested, but that cognitive exposure is recorded.  An oracle-informed repair or
rerun is a new nonblind case revision; it cannot replace the immutable
original result.  Formal \texttt{Clock\_src}, \texttt{Rod\_src},
\texttt{Signal\_src}, \texttt{Detector\_src}, or \texttt{FreeFall\_src}
tokens remain formal analogies until a typed model-to-world map licenses
physical time, distance, propagation, measurement, or free-fall claims.

\section{Planned benchmark cases}

The case schema creates six unexecuted contracts aligned to later v21 tasks:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{P9-T02 vacuum and Minkowski effective limit.}
      Requires a source-defined vacuum or stationary state, a lawful
      source-to-effective carrier, and perturbative behavior.  The target
      Minkowski metric is comparison-only.
\item \textbf{P9-T03 Newtonian, weak-field, redshift, and clock behavior.}
      Requires source-derived potentials or observables and lawful
      \texttt{Clock\_src}/\texttt{Signal\_src} physical semantics.  Target
      potentials and clock formulas cannot be boundary data.
\item \textbf{P9-T04 null propagation, local inertial behavior, and universal
      free fall.} Requires multiple physically interpreted source matter
      sectors and a derived comparison map.  Target null cones and geodesics
      are comparison-only.
\item \textbf{P9-T05 FLRW and cosmological observables.}
      Requires a source-defined homogeneous/isotropic state and derived
      expansion observable.  Target FLRW equations and \(\Lambda\) cannot
      select a branch.
\item \textbf{P9-T06 radiative degrees of freedom.}
      Requires an admitted background, physical dynamical operator,
      characteristics, polarizations, and detector semantics.  GR tensor
      modes are comparison-only.
\item \textbf{P9-T07 conservation, constraint closure, and a compact source.}
      Requires a nonlinear source-defined configuration, dynamical constraint
      propagation, and a physical observable map.  A GR compact-source
      solution cannot seed the source solution.
\end{enumerate}

All six cases are \texttt{NOT\_RUN}.  Their current source records expose
missing prerequisites rather than physical results.

\section{Outcome semantics}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.24\linewidth}p{0.67\linewidth}}
\toprule
Outcome & Meaning\\
\midrule
\texttt{NOT\_RUN} & The protocol exists but execution has not begun.\\
\texttt{FAIL\_FIREWALL} & A forbidden target path, post-hoc tolerance,
undeclared side channel, altered seal, or comparison feedback is detected.\\
\texttt{FAIL\_SOURCE} & The source computation is ill-typed, fails its own
equations or controls, or violates its declared validity regime.\\
\texttt{INCONCLUSIVE} & Required source inputs, physical semantics, tolerance,
uncertainty control, corrections, or independent reproduction are absent.\\
\texttt{CONDITIONAL} & The case is otherwise valid but its result depends on
an underived proposal-only source extension or an exposed calibration; it is
not recovery from unchanged ontology.\\
\texttt{FORMAL\_ANALOGY} & Only uncalibrated source syntax or operational
tokens are compared with physical target observables.\\
\texttt{FAIL\_COMPARISON} & A valid sealed source output is decisively outside
the preregistered target criterion.  The deviation remains recorded.\\
\texttt{PASS} & The firewall, source validity, primary and independent
reproduction, and preregistered comparison all pass in the exact case scope.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

A case cannot pass merely because it reuses adopted GR dynamics, repeats the
target equations, or is defined to emit the target output.  A local case pass
would not by itself authorize benchmark promotion.  Failed, rejected,
conditional, formal, and inconclusive cases remain individually visible; a
weighted average or majority vote cannot erase them.

\section{Current result and authority boundary}

P9-T01 defines a lawful benchmark program and the protocol-level theorem
\texttt{TargetImportFirewallSoundness\_v1}.  It narrows the
\texttt{benchmark\_promotion} burden by making circular cases machine
rejectable, but it changes no physical or Distance-to-GR status.  Gate E
remains not ready because no case is executed, no physical model-to-world
bridge is established, and no independent reproduction exists.

No canonical ontology or reviewed P5--P8 source is modified.  No effective
metric, matter-coupling law, Einstein equation, exact-GR recovery theorem,
benchmark result, Gate E verdict, physics promotion, proof authority,
publication, push, global no-go, future-extension impossibility, or completed
derivation is authorized.
