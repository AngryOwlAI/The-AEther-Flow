\section{Conditional mathematics remains correct}

On overlaps the chain rule gives
\[
 dq_i=\gamma'_{ij}(q_j)dq_j.
\]
Since $\gamma'_{ij}$ is nonzero, the two covectors span the same real line.
The cocycle makes this equality coherent, so the descent, restriction,
refinement, and gluing proofs stand.  The line stabilizer in an adapted basis
has matrices whose first row is $(a,0,0,0)$ with $a\ne0$; therefore
\[
 \dim \GL(4)=16,
 \qquad \dim H=13,
 \qquad \operatorname{codim}_{\GL(4)}H=3.
\]
The adapted frames form the claimed principal $H$-reduction.

The same-$\mathbb R^4$ fixture also stands.  The presentations with lines
$\Span(dx^0)$ and $\Span(dx^0+dx^1)$ have the same underlying smooth source
arena, hence the same frozen $K_\star$, but unequal bridge images.  This proves
that the enriched bridge does not factor through unchanged $K_\star$ as a
strict embedded statement over the identity of $M$.  A determinant-one
diffeomorphism relates the two presentations, so the fixture does not prove
nonisomorphism after quotienting by proposal arrows, physical inequivalence,

\section{New exact audit payloads}

\begin{theorem}[Bare-source natural-section obstruction]
There is no assignment sending every bare smooth four-manifold $M$ to a
section of $\mathbb P(T^*M)$ that is natural under all source
diffeomorphisms.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Suppose such an assignment exists.  On $M=\mathbb R^4$, evaluate it at the
origin.  Every $A\in\GL^+(4,\mathbb R)$ is the derivative at the origin of a
source diffeomorphism fixing the origin; global linear diffeomorphisms already
suffice.  Naturality would force the selected projective covector line to be
fixed by every $A^*$.  But $\GL^+(4)$ acts transitively on projective
covector lines.  For example, the determinant-one quarter rotation in the
$(x^0,x^1)$ plane maps $\Span(dx^0)$ to $\Span(dx^1)$.  No fixed line exists,
a contradiction.
\end{proof}

This is a scoped current-reduct theorem, not a global no-go.  Additional
source structure can select a line; RT005 does exactly that by stipulation.
The theorem proves that the added foliation atlas is not derived naturally
from the bare smooth source arena.

\begin{lemma}[Reduction carries no extra selector]
For $H$ the stabilizer of a reference covector line, smooth projective
cotangent-line sections and principal $H$-reductions of the frame bundle are
equivalent data.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The homogeneous space $\GL(4)/H$ is projective covector space.  The standard
section--reduction correspondence sends a line $L_x$ to the frames carrying
it to the reference line.  Conversely, an adapted frame recovers $L_x$ by
pulling back the reference line; this is independent of the frame because
$H$ stabilizes that line.  The two constructions are inverse.  Thus
$P_H(q)$ repackages $L_q$ and does not independently select it.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Output-relative naturality]
If an admitted proposal arrow $f$ is defined by
$f^*L_{\mathrm{target}}=L_{\mathrm{source}}$, then naturality of the
$L$-component of $\mathrm{Bridge}_{\rm PCR}$ follows directly from the arrow
premise and supplies no independent admissibility or $\mathrm{EqSrc}$
evidence.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The required naturality equation is the displayed arrow predicate itself;
transport of adapted frames follows functorially from the same equality.
The proposal functor is therefore mathematically valid on its declared
category, while the category cannot be justified by citing that same
naturality theorem.  An independently motivated pre-bridge arrow law would
be additional source data and is absent here.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[No equivariant conformal lift]
Let $V$ be four-dimensional and let $H\subset\GL(V)$ stabilize a projective
covector line.  There is no $\GL(V)$-equivariant map from
$\mathbb P(V^*)\cong\GL(V)/H$ to the space of Lorentzian conformal classes
$\GL(V)/\CO(1,3)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
A $G$-equivariant map $G/H\to G/K$ requires the stabilizer $H$ of the source
basepoint to lie in a conjugate of the target stabilizer $K$.  Here
$\dim H=13$, while $\dim\CO(1,3)=7$: six Lorentz generators plus one conformal
scale.  Such an inclusion is impossible.  Therefore no equivariant map
exists.
\end{proof}

The pointwise example is equally direct.  The two Lorentzian forms
\[
 g_1=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1,1),\qquad
 g_2=\operatorname{diag}(-1,2,3,5)
\]
share the distinguished covector line $\Span(dx^0)$ but are not conformally
related.  The line supplies three pointwise homogeneous-space coordinates;
a Lorentzian conformal class supplies nine.  A projective conormal is not a
conformal geometry.

\begin{lemma}[Coorientation erasure]
The RT005 quotient selects neither a positive representative ray nor a time
orientation.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The admitted label change $q\mapsto-q$ sends $dq$ to $-dq$ while preserving
$L_q$.  Even inside determinant-positive linear transformations, the matrix
$\operatorname{diag}(-1,-1,1,1)$ preserves $\Span(dx^0)$ and reverses its
representative ray.  Hence the quotient contains no invariant sign choice.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Domain-relative robustness]
The RT005 coherent-label robustness statement is correct, but it does not
imply robustness under arbitrary small line-field variations.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For $\epsilon\ne0$, let
\[
 \alpha_\epsilon=dx^0+\epsilon x^1dx^2.
\]
Then
\[
 \alpha_\epsilon\wedge d\alpha_\epsilon
 =\epsilon\,dx^0\wedge dx^1\wedge dx^2\ne0.
\]
Thus the associated codimension-one distribution is not Frobenius
integrable, even for arbitrarily small $\epsilon$ on a fixed compact chart.
By contrast, any coherent perturbation represented as $dq_\epsilon$ remains
closed and integrable.  RT005 explicitly limits its claim to the latter
domain, so its theorem survives while universal finite-variation credit does
not follow.
\end{proof}
