\section{Obstruction}

\begin{obstruction}[Family-domain gap]
The current object domain \(\Obj\) contains a manifest-bound positive
relabeling pair and local negative controls. It does not contain a
declared source-object family \(\ObjFam\) with nontrivial objects
\[
    Y_{1},\dots,Y_{n}\in\ObjFam
\]
whose admissibility, provenance, and comparison scope are registered.
Without \(\ObjFam\), a family-level \(\EqSrc\) claim has no domain.
\end{obstruction}

\begin{obstruction}[Witness-ledger gap]
The current datum does not provide a witness ledger containing intended
positive, negative, and blocked source-family pairs
\[
    (Y_{i},Y_{j})\mapsto
    \{\EqSrc(Y_{i},Y_{j}),\neg\EqSrc(Y_{i},Y_{j}),\Block\}.
\]
The local pair \((X_{0},X_{1})\) cannot be promoted into such a ledger by
analogy.
\end{obstruction}

\begin{obstruction}[Relation-property gap]
The stress test leaves reflexivity and symmetry local to the relabeling
orbit at most. Transitivity outside relabeling copies, quotient descent,
representative selection, and invariant preservation under quotienting
remain unregistered. Therefore the current datum does not establish an
equivalence relation over source-family objects.
\end{obstruction}

\begin{obstruction}[Negative-control retention gap]
The annotation-weakening and unmapped-proxy negative controls remain local
guards. They do not prove that a family-level comparison rule preserves
negative controls under source-object variation, quotienting, retention,
or generator output.
\end{obstruction}

\begin{obstruction}[Downstream-use gap]
The current datum supplies no condition under which an adverse source
output is retained after equivalence decisions, and no source-side
generator with validated output conditions. Thus \(\RetainH\) and
\(\GenH\) remain untouched.
\end{obstruction}
