## Selected Repair

Define two distinct transformation predicates on \(Z_1(E)\):

1. pointwise label stability,
   \(\chi_E(Vz)=\chi_E(z)\) for every \(z\); and
2. kernel-pair relation preservation and reflection,
   \(z\sim_E z'\iff Vz\sim_E Vz'\).

Prove that a translation \(S_c\) has the first property exactly when
\([c]=0\), while every translation has the second. Reclassify \(S_{e_2}\) as
a non-pointwise codomain-gauge translation. If a stronger out-of-class
relation-changing witness is retained, use the displayed invertible linear
map \(L\) on the finite source-state space and state explicitly that it is
neither an admitted boundary move nor a chain map of the displayed chain
package.

This repair does not derive the chain package, its differential, homology as a
physical discriminator, or boundary moves as physically admissible
variations. Those remain proposal-only source-extension burdens.
