## Decision Rationale

The Phase 11 selector found that scoped source-only `M_src` adoption satisfies
the hard prerequisite for a `g_eff` preflight, but no source-to-effective-metric
law exists yet. The missing-law inventory already records
`MSL-MSRC-METRIC-RESPONSE-LAW`, whose older evidence states that source
observer/readout data alone do not determine metric signature, interval scale,
causal cones, metric response, or coupling hooks. With scoped `M_src` adoption
available, the narrow legal next step is not immediate construction but a
proposal-only law interface or a precise obstruction.
