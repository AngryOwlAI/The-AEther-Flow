## Major Scientific Vulnerabilities

1. Certificate-gap dependence remains the central scientific vulnerability. The positive P2 theorem branch requires explicit valid source transport, invariance, or factorization certificates. Existing scoped evidence/preconditions cannot substitute for concrete certificates.
2. The matter-coupling route still lacks detector semantics or an authorized source-side replacement, coupling-law adoption authority, stress-energy semantics, stress-energy tensor construction, matter action or alternative dynamics path, universal matter-coupling derivation, Einstein-equation derivation, and benchmark promotion authority.
3. Source-extension evidence is not derivation support by itself. If a future route treats `M_src`, `g_eff`, `RR_ETransportCompletenessOrInvarianceLaw_v1`, `NarrowMSCertEq_v1`, or `PositiveMSProfile_v1` as downstream adoption rather than scoped evidence/precondition, the route fails closed.
4. The proof-assistant pilot covers only one local fail-closed kernel. It does not formalize the full certificate algebra, matter semantics, detector semantics, matter coupling, stress-energy, matter action, Einstein equations, or benchmark recovery.

## Major Process Vulnerabilities

1. A reviewer or later writer could overread the existence of a red-team template as readiness for external authority.
2. Public status summaries are only safe when the exists and does-not-exist columns remain coupled.
3. Claim-graph and dependency-graph artifacts are useful navigation, but their edges cannot become proof steps.
4. The support-only proof-assistant lane is valuable as validator support, but expanding it into proof authority would be premature after one narrow pilot.
5. Historical registry rows can still trigger linter warnings. These are historical/provenance-class warnings in current checks, not a new P16-T02 hard failure, but they remain a review hazard if copied into new public prose without scoped wording.
