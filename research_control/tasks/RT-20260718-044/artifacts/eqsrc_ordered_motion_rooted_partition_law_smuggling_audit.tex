\section{Universal ordered-partition realization}

\begin{theorem}[Universal ordered-partition realization]
Let \(X=P\sqcup Q\) be any finite ordered partition with \(P,Q\neq\varnothing\).
Choose \(r\in P\) and \(q_0\in Q\). Define a directed graph by
\[
 r\longrightarrow q\quad(q\in Q),\qquad
 q_0\longrightarrow p\quad(p\in P\setminus\{r\}).
\]
Take \(r\) as the singleton root certificate, \(E=X\), and
\(\pi=\mathrm{id}_X\). Then every token is root reachable, every root-to-token
path has a unique parity, the even block is exactly \(P\), and the odd block
is exactly \(Q\). Thus every nontrivial ordered finite partition can be
realized by the candidate syntax.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Every \(q\in Q\) is reached directly from \(r\), hence has odd parity. Every
\(p\in P\setminus\{r\}\) is reached by the unique length-two path
\(r\to q_0\to p\), while \(r\) has length zero. The graph has no other
outgoing edges, so no alternate-parity path exists. Both blocks are nonempty
by hypothesis.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The theorem is not candidate repair and does not show a forbidden target
premise in the displayed text. It shows non-discrimination: a desired
partition may be encoded in \(D_\Phi\) and \(C_{\rm root}\), with
\(\pi=\mathrm{id}\), unless independent source provenance constrains those
data. Declaring the data before \(B_U\) does not prove an acquisition history.
\end{remark}

Exact enumeration confirms the construction for all nontrivial ordered
partitions on sets of sizes \(2,3,4,5\): respectively \(2,6,14,30\) records,
with one distinct boundary line for every selected odd block.
