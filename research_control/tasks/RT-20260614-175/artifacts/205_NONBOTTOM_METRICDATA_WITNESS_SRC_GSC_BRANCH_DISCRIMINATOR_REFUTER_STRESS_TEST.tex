\section{Marked-Package Domain Lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Marked-package domain separation]
Let \(E\) be an unmarked finite source package and let \(\beta_1,\beta_2\) be
two incompatible branch-origin markings on \(E\). Then
\(\BranchDisc(E,\beta_1)\) and \(\BranchDisc(E,\beta_2)\) are not two values
of a unique unmarked discriminator on \(E\). They are values on two distinct
marked source packages.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The proposal-only domain is not \(E\) alone; it is the marked presentation
\((E,\beta)\). If \(\beta_1\) and \(\beta_2\) are incompatible, the data
\((E,\beta_1)\) and \((E,\beta_2)\) differ as source presentations. The
candidate therefore does not choose between source-automorphic branches of
unmarked \(E\). It evaluates only the supplied marked package. Forgetting
\(\beta\) removes the domain condition and returns \(\BottomSrc\).
\end{proof}

\section{Transition-Coherence Stress}

\begin{stresscase}[Inverse-defect pressure]
Suppose an admitted transition \(\tau:(E,\beta)\to(E',\beta')\) collapses two
marked branch labels or lacks a source-side inverse on the label transport.
Then \(\tau_\ast\beta\) cannot support two-sided preservation of
\(\BranchDisc\).
\end{stresscase}

\begin{proof}
Two-sided preservation would require source-side branch labels to be
transported without losing the distinction that \(\BranchDisc\) records. If
\(\tau_\ast\) collapses labels or lacks the required inverse on the marked
label data, the equation for marked transition preservation becomes
ill-defined or nonrecoverable. The candidate is not repaired by target
coordinates, target metric, benchmark success, role status, validator status,
or approval state. The route returns \(\BottomSrc\).
\end{proof}

\begin{stresscase}[Cocycle-defect pressure]
Suppose admitted transitions \(\tau_1,\tau_2\) satisfy
\[
  (\tau_2\tau_1)_\ast\beta\ne \tau_{2\ast}(\tau_{1\ast}\beta).
\]
Then branch-origin labels depend on the chosen transition factorization.
\end{stresscase}

\begin{proof}
If the transported marking depends on path or factorization, then
\(\BranchDisc\) is not functorial over the admitted marked transition class.
The candidate survives only when the admitted transition class is restricted
to coherent source-side transports. Otherwise the branch label is not a
well-defined source-side datum for the route and the result is
\(\BottomSrc\), not an adopted law or metric-data witness.
\end{proof}
