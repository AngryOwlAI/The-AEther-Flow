\section{Audit Findings}

\begin{proposition}[No target-GR import detected as written]
As written, \(\MSCertQuot\) does not use target atlas, target metric,
Lorentzian signature, proper time normalization, detector semantics,
stress-energy tensor, matter action, benchmark success, or Einstein-equation
premises.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Reason.}
The quotient relation is stated only in terms of source support \(S_E\),
probe-token family \(P_E\), and no-target certificate bundle \(\Gamma_E\)
agreement or explicit identity transport. The proof checks in RT-038 use
equality and identity transport inside the finite certificate fiber. They do
not invoke target geometric, matter, or dynamical objects.

\begin{proposition}[No process-authority laundering detected as written]
As written, the candidate does not use registry rows, generated wiki notes,
semantic extracts, Obsidian cache state, validators, roles, handoffs,
approvals, commits, or file order as mathematical premises.

\paragraph{Reason.}
The process artifacts establish routing and provenance only. The mathematical
definition of the quotient is contained in the RT-038 TeX artifact and remains
proposal-only. Validation and registry status do not prove the quotient,
matter coupling, or any downstream GR claim.

\begin{proposition}[Certificate bundle use is guarded but not decisive]
The no-target certificate bundle is used as a finite source-side domain guard
and equivalence criterion. It is not used as proof that matter semantics,
coupling law, detector semantics, stress-energy semantics, matter action, or
Einstein equations have been derived.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Reason.}
RT-038 explicitly preserves \(\NoDetector\), \(\NoSE\), \(\NoAction\), and
\(\NoTarget\). Those certificates constrain what may not be imported. They do
not supply a physical matter interpretation or a source-law adoption.

\begin{proposition}[Overcollapse risk is bounded and pending stress]
The quotient deliberately identifies records whose source readout values and
response-marker syntax differ inside one finite certificate fiber. This is a
bounded source-side overcollapse risk, not a hidden target import as written.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Reason.}
The candidate explicitly treats \(R_E\) and \(Q_E\) as uninterpreted source
bookkeeping labels only inside \(\mathcal F_{\Gamma}(C_+;E,\Bcur)\), and maps
non-fiber records to \(\bot\). That bounded definition prevents global

source relabeling, certificate deletion, finite variation, sector deletion,
or degeneracy stress. Those are Refuter obligations.
