# DDR-20260702-030: v14 P10 Literature Source Acquisition

## Decision

Route one bounded P10-T02 `literature_source_acquisition_packet` to
`documentation-curator@2.0.0` through a task overlay.

## Basis

`handoff-0482` requires one bounded P10-T02 literature source acquisition
packet for the selected
`operational_causal_metric_universal_coupling_constraints_scope`. The v14 plan
requires bibliographic metadata, source relevance, burden mapping, access
notes, APA 7 citation candidates, and an explicit no-claim-promotion statement.

This packet acquires sources only. It does not compare the sources against the
project, evaluate scientific adequacy, or route downstream physics.

## Selected Role

`documentation-curator@2.0.0` is selected because the deliverable is a
source-backed bibliography/control artifact. The role is narrowed to source
acquisition and may not modify public documentation, canonical ontology,
science drafts, role contracts, schemas, or routing behavior.

## Scope

Allowed:

- gather primary literature where possible;
- record source metadata, source-access notes, burden relevance, and APA 7
  citation candidates;
- record review anchors only when primary sources alone do not provide a
  compact boundary reference;
- update control state, registries, handoff, and generated derivatives.

Forbidden:

- compare or adjudicate the sources;
- treat external resemblance as validation;
- promote matter coupling, stress-energy semantics, matter action, Einstein
  equations, benchmark status, or completed derivation.
