\section{Stress Lemmas}

\begin{lemma}[Non-bottom instantiation obstruction]
Under current authority, a non-bottom split in \(L_E\) is adoption-ready only
if \(D_E:T_E\to L_E\) is induced by source-side structure and not merely by
labels attached to the payload.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The RT-055 candidate-law schema lists non-bottom source discrimination as a
future obligation. The RT-056 audit shows that the written schema avoids
hidden target imports, but it does not construct the promised source-side
non-bottom discriminator. If the non-bottom labels are not induced by a
source-side law, then replacing them by arbitrary names changes no source
fact. The packet therefore has no adoption-ready matter semantics under
\(\Bcur\). The fail-closed result is \(\Bottom\) or a scoped obstruction.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Relabeling transport obstruction]
Relabeling naturality for \(\SemMatterSrc(E)\) requires a source-defined
transport \(\rho_L\) on labels. It cannot be supplied by preserving target
matter interpretation.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The required equation \(D_{E'}(\rho(t))=\rho_L(D_E(t))\) is meaningful only
when both \(\rho\) and \(\rho_L\) are source-side maps. If \(\rho_L\) is
defined by keeping target matter names fixed, by detector outcomes, or by
stress-energy/action variables, then the stress surface imports the target
semantics the candidate was designed to avoid. Such an import is outside the
claim boundary. Without source-defined \(\rho_L\), adoption-readiness fails.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Support-only erasure lemma]
If support-only outputs are marked \(\ProofAuthFalse\), then erasing
non-source labels leaves no proof of \(\MatSrcDiscLaw\) or \(\SemMatterSrc\)
adoption.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The audit and control records allow support outputs only as retrieval or
process support. Once non-source labels, generated derivatives, registry
metadata, validator status, role status, handoff status, approval status,
commit status, and local caches are excluded as proof authority, no remaining
object in the audited payload proves source-law adoption or matter coupling.
\end{proof}
