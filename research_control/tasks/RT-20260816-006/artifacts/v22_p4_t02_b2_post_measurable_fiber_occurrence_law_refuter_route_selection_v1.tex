\section{Exact factorization theorem schema}

Let $X$ be a set of proposed occurrence/admissibility extensions, $D$ a
current-source reduct space, $U:X\to D$ the forgetful map, and $R:X\to Y$ a
typed relevance map. Let $\bar U:X\to\im(U)$ be the corestriction.

\paragraph{Image-factorization criterion.}
There exists a unique map $f_{\rm im}:\im(U)\to Y$ satisfying
\begin{equation}
  R=f_{\rm im}\circ\bar U
  \label{eq:factorization}
\end{equation}
if and only if $R$ is constant on every $U$-fibre.

Indeed, any factorization forces fibre constancy. Conversely, fibre constancy
makes $f_{\rm im}(U(x)):=R(x)$ well-defined, and surjectivity of $\bar U$
gives uniqueness. This statement is set-theoretic. Structured natural
factorization additionally requires independently sourced arrow classes and
commuting transport squares.

A factor on all of $D$ requires an extension of $f_{\rm im}$ away from
$\im(U)$. Its existence and uniqueness are separate from
Eq.~\eqref{eq:factorization}. Surjectivity of $U$ is sufficient for global
uniqueness. A degenerate singleton codomain supplies an exception to any
unqualified converse, so the future audit must state its hypotheses exactly.
