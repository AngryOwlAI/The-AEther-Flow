\section{Pressure-Pair Lemmas}

\begin{lemma}[\(\mathrm{RR}_E\) pressure-pair non-collapse]
Let \(b_0,b_1\in\BridgeDom\) agree except for distinct
\(\mathrm{RR}_E\) components. A proof of readiness for \(b_0\) cannot be
transferred to \(b_1\) by identifying their \(\mathrm{RR}_E\) records. Either
each record satisfies the readiness obligations independently, or the
attempted transfer fails closed.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
O3 explicitly blocks identification of distinct \(\mathrm{RR}_E\) records to
manufacture readiness. The audit also classifies \(\mathrm{RR}_E\) separation
as source syntax rather than detector response, empirical measurement,
stress-energy semantics, matter action, or coupling-law data. Therefore
collapse is not an admissible proof operation.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Certificate deletion fail-closed branch]
If one of the no-target, no-detector, no-stress-energy, or no-action
certificates is deleted from \(b\), then \(\Ready(b)\neq\AuditReady\) for the
deleted-certificate use.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
O6 requires explicit certificates blocking target atlas, target metric,
proper-time normalization, detector semantics, empirical calibration,
stress-energy, matter action, conservation law, coupling-law adoption,
\(\MetricData(E)\), \(\geff\), Einstein equations, and benchmark behavior.
Deleting any required certificate removes the admissibility guard. The
fail-closed clause then returns \(\Bottom\) or \(\Obstruction\).
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Process-authority immunity]
Changing registry metadata, validator results, handoff text, approval state,
role identity, generated derivatives, commit state, file order, or local cache
state cannot change \(\Ready(b)\) unless the mathematical source-side record
itself changes.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
O2 forbids process authority as a mathematical premise, and the
process-authority fail-closed condition sends any such dependence to
\(\Obstruction\). Thus process surfaces may document routing or provenance but
cannot prove readiness.
\end{proof}
