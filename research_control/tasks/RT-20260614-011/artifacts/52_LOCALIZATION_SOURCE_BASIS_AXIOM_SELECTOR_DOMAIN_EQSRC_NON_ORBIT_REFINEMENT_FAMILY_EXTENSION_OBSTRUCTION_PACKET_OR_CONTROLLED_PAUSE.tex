\section{Extension Obstruction Ledger}

\begin{obstruction}[Branch-family domain is not supplied]
A branch-family domain would require declared objects
\(\ObjBranch\), source provenance, annotation ledgers, proxy and
forbidden-channel guards, boundary anchors, and an admissibility rule for
incomparable internal carriers. The current record contains a branch case
only as a blocked control and stress pressure. It is not a positive
domain member and does not supply branch-family \(\EqSrc\) evidence.
\end{obstruction}

\begin{obstruction}[Non-chain relation properties are missing]
For a larger finite partial-order domain \(\ObjNonChain\), the record
does not supply a comparison relation \(\CmpExt\) with source-side
receipts for reflexivity, symmetry, transitivity, or representative
independence. The local proof by common contraction to \(Y_{1}\) works
only because the declared positive objects are chain refinements sharing
the same base chain. It does not prove relation properties for arbitrary
non-chain objects.
\end{obstruction}

\begin{obstruction}[Annotation-monoid stability is not proved]
The audit pass depends on a fixed free commutative monoid of source
annotation atoms and conservation of the total annotation mass. The record
does not prove stability under atom deletion, atom merging, weighting, or
success-selected atom choice. Those operations can erase source evidence
or become target-success proxies. Extension under a weakened annotation
monoid therefore returns \(\Block\).
\end{obstruction}

\begin{obstruction}[Contraction admissibility is chain-local]
The admissible contractions in the retained packet are constrained by the
declared chain domain and invariant ledger. A bare many-to-one map can
erase branch multiplicity, source-order shape, blocker state, and
annotation placement. The record does not supply an admissibility theorem
for contraction outside the chain-refinement family.
\end{obstruction}

\begin{obstruction}[Boundary-anchor invariance is not supplied]
The retained pass uses fixed source-chain input and output anchors.
Moving an anchor, splitting an endpoint, identifying an endpoint with a
target observer protocol, or selecting anchors because a downstream result
improves changes the source ledger. No boundary-anchor invariance receipt
for a larger family is present.
\end{obstruction}

\begin{obstruction}[Quotient descent is not supplied]
No tracked source packet proves that quotienting by admissible
contraction preserves annotation mass, proxy guard state,
forbidden-channel state, influence coverage, blocker status, and relation
properties for a larger family. A quotient rule may be proposed later,
but it must preserve adverse evidence. It cannot identify blocked objects
with positive witnesses by representative choice.
\end{obstruction}

\begin{obstruction}[Downstream-use evidence is absent]
No inspected artifact supplies the bridge from local \(\EqRef\) to
\(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), or candidate reconstruction. Downstream use would
require a source-side relation domain, invariant ledger, comparison rule,
audit result, and stress result strong enough for that downstream burden.
Those receipts are absent.
\end{obstruction}
