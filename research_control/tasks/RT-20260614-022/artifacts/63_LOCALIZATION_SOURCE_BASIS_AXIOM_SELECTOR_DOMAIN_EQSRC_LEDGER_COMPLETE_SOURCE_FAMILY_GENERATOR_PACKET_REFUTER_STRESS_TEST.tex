\begin{abstract}
This draft/control Refuter artifact stress-tests the audited
\texttt{RT-20260614-020} ledger-complete source-family generator packet
and the \texttt{RT-20260614-021} Smuggling Auditor local conditional
pass. The formal relation surface survives only as a declared
certificate relation \(\EqLC\) on the declared generated source-object
domain \(\ObjLC\), conditional on literal full-ledger preservation and
valid reversible certificates. Scientific adequacy does not survive:
generator coverage, certificate substance, inverse validity, composition
closure, quotient robustness, guard and blocker retention, influence
coverage, boundary-anchor stability, non-chain refinement, readout
invariance, and downstream bridge relevance remain unproved. General
\(\EqSrc\), \(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), downstream bridge claims,
candidate reconstruction, canonical ontology edit, benchmark promotion,
Gate Chair review, and completed-derivation language remain blocked.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope}

This artifact is the role output for task \texttt{RT-20260614-022}. It
does not repeat the source-family generator construction from
\texttt{RT-20260614-020} and does not repeat the target-import audit
from \texttt{RT-20260614-021}. The Refuter question is:
\[
    \text{Does the audited local }\EqLC\text{ packet survive pressure as
    a useful source-equivalence surface for later physics?}
\]
The answer is bifurcated. As a formal relation on the declared
certificate domain, the packet is locally retained. As scientific
adequacy evidence for downstream physics, it returns \(\Pause\) with
specific residual obligations.

\section{Two-Track Stress Method}

\begin{stressscreen}[Formal relation track]
The formal track asks only whether the declared relation \(\EqLC\)
remains reflexive, symmetric, and transitive on \(\ObjLC\) under the
packet's own certificate rules. This track is algebraic and local.
If \(\CertLC\) contains only reversible source-ledger steps and remains
closed under valid concatenation, the formal relation property is
retained.
\end{stressscreen}

\begin{stressscreen}[Scientific adequacy track]
The adequacy track asks whether the same relation preserves structures
needed for future readout, retention, generator, causal, clock,
matter-coupling, or benchmark-recovery burdens. This track is not
established by the equivalence-relation proof. It requires coverage,
nontrivial examples, failed examples, quotient robustness, certificate
substance, and eventually a source-side readout-invariance theorem.
\end{stressscreen}

\begin{controlrule}[No track collapse]
A pass on the formal relation track does not imply a pass on the
scientific adequacy track. Treating formal \(\EqLC\) status as evidence
for \(\EqSrc\), \(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), downstream bridge claims, or
candidate readiness returns \(\Block\).
\end{controlrule}

\section{Retained Local Surface}

\begin{stressfinding}[Formal relation retained inside the declared domain]
Inside \(\ObjLC\), the packet's formal relation proof remains locally
valid under its own assumptions: identity certificates give reflexivity,
valid reversible source-ledger certificates give symmetry, and valid
concatenation of certificates gives transitivity. The stress test does
not find a target metric, target proper time, target locality, observer
protocol, benchmark rank, candidate performance, validation status, or
authority label inside this formal proof.
\end{stressfinding}

\begin{controlrule}[Retention condition]
The retention condition is literal. Every positive certificate step must
preserve source branch tokens, source labels, annotation data, proxy
guards, forbidden-channel guards, blocker records, influence records,
quotient-fiber data, contraction data, and boundary anchors. A step that
weakens any listed coordinate exits \(\CertLC\).
\end{controlrule}

\section{Generator-Coverage Pressure}

\begin{counterexample}[Outside-domain object]
Let \(Y\) be a ledger-like source object with a new branch arity, a
cross-branch merge, or a boundary-anchor pattern not generated by the
declared \(\GenLC\) operations. Even if \(Y\) is source-side and
ledger-complete in an informal sense, the packet supplies no proof that
\(Y\in\ObjLC\). Therefore \(\EqLC(X,Y)\) is undefined or blocked for
\(X\in\ObjLC\) unless a later source-side generator extension proves
membership without target or benchmark selection.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{stressfinding}[Coverage remains a missing theorem]
The generator is a constructive domain definition, not an enumeration
theorem for all source objects relevant to the research program. The
Refuter therefore blocks any use of \(\GenLC\) as general source-family
coverage evidence.
\end{stressfinding}

\section{Certificate Pressure}

\begin{counterexample}[Inverse failure]
Consider a branch-refinement step that splits a branch while preserving
aggregate annotation totals but discards which sub-branch carries a
blocker record. The forward step may appear ledger-compatible at the
aggregate level, but its inverse cannot recover blocker provenance. The
step is not reversible in \(\CertLC\), so the certificate returns
\(\Block\).
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Composition failure]
Let \(c:X\to Y\) preserve all ledger coordinates except that it records a
quotient representative only up to a source-local fiber, and let
\(d:Y\to Z\) preserve quotient data only after moving a boundary anchor.
Each step may look locally repairable, but the concatenation does not
preserve the same boundary and quotient data. The composed certificate is
not admissible, so transitivity cannot be used for that chain.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{stressfinding}[Certificate substance remains open]
The proof that \(\EqLC\) is an equivalence relation is only as strong as
the certificate grammar. The current packet states reversible and
composable certificates but does not yet provide a machine-checkable
certificate algebra, inverse checker, or composition checker. Formal
status is retained; scientific adequacy remains blocked.
\end{stressfinding}

\section{Quotient, Guard, Blocker, Influence, and Boundary Pressure}

\begin{counterexample}[Quotient representative variation]
If a quotient representative is chosen because a later comparison,
readout, or candidate behavior improves, then representative choice is
downstream-selected. The result returns \(\Block\), even if the final
object still looks ledger-complete.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Guard weakening and blocker erasure]
If a proxy guard is relaxed, a forbidden-channel guard is deleted, or a
blocker record is erased before comparison, the positive relation can be
created by removing adverse source evidence. The result returns
\(\Block\).
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Influence deletion]
If influence records are collapsed to carrier order or deleted after
refinement, then source influence coverage no longer witnesses the same
object. The result returns \(\Block\) unless a later packet supplies a
new source-only influence-preservation proof.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Boundary-anchor perturbation]
If boundary anchors \(i,o\) move, split, or acquire observer-like
interpretation, a certificate that was valid for the old anchors is not
valid for the new object. Boundary perturbation returns \(\Block\) until
new source-side anchor data are supplied.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Non-chain refinement]
If a refinement introduces a branching partial order that cannot be
represented as a chain of permitted generator steps, the current
certificate-concatenation proof does not apply. The result is a new
obligation, not a positive \(\EqLC\) relation.
\end{counterexample}

\section{Tri-Family, Validation, and Downstream Pressure}

\begin{stressfinding}[Tri-family embedding does not generalize]
Embedding \(T_{1},T_{2},T_{3}\) into \(\ObjLC\) is a consistency check.
It does not convert \(\EqTri\) into general \(\EqSrc\), and it does not
prove generator coverage beyond the declared family.
\end{stressfinding}

\begin{stressfinding}[Validation labels are not physics evidence]
Registry rows, PASS validators, generated wiki visibility, and handoff
status are evidence of transaction hygiene. They are not evidence that
\(\EqLC\) is scientifically adequate, bridge-ready, or promotable.
\end{stressfinding}

\begin{stressfinding}[Downstream bridge remains absent]
No source-side readout map \(\Read\) is defined here, and no theorem of
the form
\[
    X\EqLC Y \Longrightarrow \Read(X)=\Read(Y)
\]
is supplied for any bridge-relevant readout. Therefore \(\RetainH\),
\(\GenH\), causal structure, clock behavior, matter coupling, candidate
reconstruction, and benchmark recovery remain outside the current
result.
\end{stressfinding}

\section{Stress Verdicts}

\begin{verdict}[Local formal retention]
The audited packet survives as a formal local certificate relation on
the declared \(\ObjLC\) domain under strict full-ledger and reversible
certificate assumptions.
\end{verdict}

\begin{verdict}[Scientific adequacy controlled pause]
The packet does not yet supply scientific adequacy evidence. Generator
coverage, certificate substance, quotient robustness, nontrivial
examples, failed examples, readout invariance, and bridge relevance
remain open obligations. The correct status is controlled pause before
downstream bridge use.
\end{verdict}

\begin{verdict}[Blocked overreads]
General \(\EqSrc\), \(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), downstream bridge claims,
candidate reconstruction, canonical ontology edit, benchmark promotion,
Gate Chair review, completed-derivation language, and global theory
rejection remain blocked.
\end{verdict}

\section{Kill Criteria and Next Burden}

\begin{nextburden}[Pre-bridge adequacy packet]
The logical next role is Ontology Formalizer. The next packet should not
attempt a downstream bridge. It should define a pre-bridge adequacy
target: a formal-relation track, a scientific-adequacy track, a finite
positive example, a finite negative example, a boundary example, a
failed-certificate example, quotient-variation and guard/blocker
perturbation tests, a source-side readout-invariance ladder, and kill
criteria for closing, repairing, or promoting the line.
\end{nextburden}

\begin{nextburden}[Minimal promotion bar remains closed]
Promotion cannot begin until a later audited and refuted packet supplies
at least one source-side readout map, a nonvacuous preservation theorem
under \(\EqLC\), negative examples that remain blocked, and explicit
failure criteria. Even then, Gate Chair review remains human-gated.
\end{nextburden}
