## Section-Level Refresh Guidance

### Director-led Continue Research

The methodology manuscript should now describe Continue Research as an
explicit Director-led protocol. The Director does not merely execute the next
instruction; it resolves tracked state, inspects authority surfaces, records a
Director Decision Record, and creates or reuses exactly one bounded AgentJob
when continuation is lawful.

The methodology claim is operational: the protocol reduces scope drift by
forcing one next route to be justified from tracked state. The manuscript must
not infer that a routed packet is scientifically correct because it was routed.

### AgentJob contracts

AgentJob contracts should be presented as the central unit of bounded agentic
work. A valid contract names its objective, execution role, allowed reads,
allowed writes, expected outputs, validators, stop conditions, claim boundary,
and completion receipt.

The manuscript may claim traceability: future readers can reconstruct what was
authorized, what was produced, and what was forbidden. It must not claim that
contract completion is proof, gate acceptance, or autonomous scientific
authority.

### Claim-boundary registries

V16 makes claim-boundary discipline more explicit. The methodology manuscript
should explain that the project records blocked readings such as source-law
adoption, matter-coupling derivation, Einstein-equation derivation, benchmark
promotion, Gate Chair verdict, and completed derivation.

The methodological value is anti-laundering: later summaries can cite exact
forbidden overreads rather than relying on informal caution. Registry rows are
control evidence and provenance, not claim promotion.

### Distance-to-GR ledger

The Distance-to-GR ledger should be described as a burden-state map with
layered status fields. It helps readers distinguish source-side object status,
evidence or precondition status, open physical targets, and blocked downstream
promotion.

The manuscript must preserve the key limitation: a ledger row communicates
tracked status. It is not a derivation, not a proof, and not an independent
source of physics authority.

### Smuggling audit

The smuggling-audit section should now name target import, detector import,
stress-energy shortcut, and process-authority overread as methodological risk
families. V16 model-zoo and future attack-suite work are relevant here because
they provide finite/local fixtures for adversarial checks.

An audit can expose hidden assumptions and force a narrower route. It cannot
prove the desired theory or certify the absence of all future hidden imports.

### Refuter stress

Refuter stress should remain a method for finding local failures and scoped
obstructions. V16 reinforces that negative results must preserve exact scope:
a countermodel or obstruction can block a stronger claim under stated
assumptions without rejecting the entire research program.

The manuscript should use the vocabulary of scoped obstruction, frozen local
route, and open continuation. It must avoid converting a local failure into a
global no-go theorem.

### Gate Chair boundaries

The methodology manuscript should describe Gate Chair authority as protected,
human-gated, and exact. Even when the user grants broad human authorization
for a planning run, a given packet consumes gate authority only when the
tracked task explicitly routes to that gate and records the exact layer.

This refresh does not consume Gate Chair authority. It adds a manuscript
instruction: distinguish scoped evidence-status acceptance from source-law
adoption, matter-semantics adoption, coupling-law adoption, benchmark
promotion, or completed derivation.

### Route-orbit gating

V16 route-orbit gating belongs in the methodology manuscript because it
addresses long-horizon repetition risk. The section should explain the narrow
hard-gate rule: three consecutive same-burden process-only tasks require a
freeze review, repair selector, protected gate, or burden narrowing.

The manuscript should also state the exemptions. Legitimate theorem
decomposition, finite/local construction, countermodel work, certificate work,
attack fixtures, red-team integration, and validator repair remain lawful
when they add tracked payload or required repair evidence.

### Minimum payload gate

The minimum payload gate clarifies that physics tasks must not advance by
process receipts alone. The manuscript should list examples of payload-bearing
work: definitions, theorem statements, proof attempts, finite witnesses,
countermodels, obstructions, source models, certificate instances, executable
support specifications, and attack fixtures.

Documentation and publication tasks must classify as non-physics project
tasks. That classification is important for this P12 packet: the present
artifact refreshes methodology guidance and creates no physics payload or
Distance-to-GR delta.

### Source-first memory

The methodology manuscript should make memory preflight a named control
practice: run status checks, query memory for navigation, then inspect the
canonical source file or registry row. Memory is a retrieval layer that helps
reduce forgotten-context risk.

The manuscript must state the authority boundary plainly. Obsidian notes,
semantic extracts, SQLite memory, generated wiki notes, vault mirrors, and
`.local/` caches do not override tracked source files, registries, handoffs,
or program state.

### Negative-result preservation

Negative-result preservation remains a central methodological claim. V16 adds
fresh evidence that negative results can also guide future review selection:
P11 selected a certificate-gap obstruction for later red-team reuse while
keeping the obstruction scoped.

The manuscript may claim that this improves auditability and prevents
rediscovering the same failed route. It must not claim that a negative-result
inventory is itself a global rejection of the theory.

### Target-import attack suite

The target-import attack suite should be introduced as future adversarial
methodology. P10 validated finite/local source model fixtures and selected
reuse for attack-suite construction. The point is to make hidden target data,
detector semantics, stress-energy shortcuts, and process-authority overreads
inspectable.

This is methodology and support tooling. It does not prove matter coupling,
derive Einstein equations, promote the benchmark, or establish completed
derivation.

### Compact frontier state

The compact frontier state section should describe `current_frontier.md` and
the dependency graph as generated snapshots from tracked authority surfaces.
They are useful because they compress active state, next route, open burdens,
and claim boundaries into reader-facing form.

The manuscript must state that compact snapshots do not govern when they
conflict with `program_state.yaml`, latest handoff, registries, or canonical
source artifacts.

### Limitations and human accountability

The methodology manuscript should close with explicit limits. Language models
are probabilistic systems that need grounding, source inspection, validation,
and human gates. The major risks are hallucination, stale retrieval,
mis-scoped tools, validator gaps, authority laundering, overfitting to local
workflow constraints, and treating generated text as scientific substance.

Human accountability remains non-transferable. Protected scientific status
changes require exact tracked authority and cannot be supplied by agent role,
validator PASS, memory hit, handoff completion, or commit status.
