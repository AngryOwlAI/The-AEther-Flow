\begin{abstract}
This draft/control artifact audits the relabeling-orbit source-family
packet supplied in task \texttt{RT-20260614-004}. The audit conditionally
passes the packet only as orbit-local source-presentation invariance:
admissible manifest-preserving relabelings preserve source token
incidence, forbidden-channel orientation, proxy-class coverage,
annotation content, influence-edge incidence, no-closed-world discipline,
and the local adjudicator shape. Annotation weakening, unmapped proxy use,
and non-orbit source-object variation remain \(\Block\). The result does
not discharge general \(\EqSrc\), \(\RetainH\), or \(\GenH\); it does not
prove semantic \(\NoTargetH\); and it does not authorize candidate
reconstruction, canonical ontology edit, benchmark promotion, Gate Chair
review, or completed-derivation language.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope}

This artifact is the role output for task \texttt{RT-20260614-005}. It
audits only the packet
\[
    \mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{orb}}=(\ObjOrb,\Adm,\InvOrb,\CmpOrb)
\]
from \texttt{RT-20260614-004}. The audit target is not whether general
\(\EqSrc\) is established. The target is narrower: whether the
orbit-local packet launders target success through relabeling-family
choice, notation, proxy handling, authority labels, or overreading an
identifier-presentation orbit as a nontrivial source-object family.

\section{Audit Method}

\begin{auditscreen}[Five-channel target-import audit]
The Smuggling Auditor applies five checks:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Relabeling-family laundering}: whether the family
    \(\ObjOrb\) is treated as a nontrivial source-object family rather
    than an admissible presentation orbit of one registered object.
    \item \textbf{Notation laundering}: whether symbol similarity,
    identifier naming, or quotient notation supplies positive evidence.
    \item \textbf{Proxy laundering}: whether proxy coverage, validation
    passage, generated visibility, ranking, or favorable omission selects
    the positive result.
    \item \textbf{Authority-label laundering}: whether role identity,
    registration, validation, handoff text, or wiki generation is treated
    as scientific promotion.
    \item \textbf{Orbit-overread}: whether the local relation
    \(\EqOrb\) is silently promoted to general \(\EqSrc\),
    \(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), or candidate-ready evidence.
\end{enumerate}
\end{auditscreen}

\section{Relabeling-Family Screen}

\begin{auditfinding}[The declared family is explicit and weak]
The packet declares
\[
    \ObjOrb=\{v(X_{0})\mid v\in\Adm\},
\]
where \(\Adm\) consists of manifest-preserving identifier bijections.
This is a legitimate local source-family only in the weak presentation
sense: each member is the same registered source object under an
admissible relabeling. The packet states that it is not a family of
distinct source hypotheses, source dynamics, or physical configurations.
\end{auditfinding}

\begin{residualrisk}[The pass cannot carry non-orbit variation]
The strongest available positive result is identity-presentation
invariance under source-preserving bijections. A later step that changes
source content, proxy inventory, annotation evidence, influence-edge
coverage, manifest scope, or object domain must return \(\Block\) or open
a superseding source-family datum.
\end{residualrisk}

\begin{controlrule}[No family inflation]
The phrase source-family may refer here only to the relabeling orbit
\(\ObjOrb\). It cannot be used as shorthand for a nontrivial family of
source objects or for general \(\EqSrc\).
\end{controlrule}

\section{Notation and Quotient Screen}

\begin{auditfinding}[Relation receipts are source-structural]
The positive receipts use identity, inverse, and composition of
admissible bijections preserving the seven declared source-only
structures. They do not use literal token names, target metric behavior,
target proper time, target locality, target observer protocols,
benchmark-success rank, or target-favorable proxy choice.
\end{auditfinding}

\begin{auditfinding}[Quotient notation is presentation-local]
The quotient \(\ObjOrb/{\EqOrb}\) identifies only source-neutral
presentations of the same registered manifest object. The packet blocks
annotation weakening, unmapped proxy use, influence-edge perturbation,
manifest-scope expansion, and non-orbit source-object variation from the
quotient.
\end{auditfinding}

\begin{controlrule}[Do not rename \(\EqOrb\) into \(\EqSrc\)]
The notation \(\EqOrb\) must remain the orbit-local relabeling judgment.
It is not canonical \(\EqSrc\), not a nontrivial source-equivalence
relation, and not evidence for \(\RetainH\) or \(\GenH\).
\end{controlrule}

\section{Proxy and Negative-Control Screen}

\begin{auditfinding}[Proxy classes remain guarded]
The positive rule asks whether an admissible manifest-preserving
bijection exists. It does not ask whether a target result succeeds, a
benchmark rank improves, validation passes, generated artifacts are
visible, or a role label is favorable.
\end{auditfinding}

\begin{auditfinding}[Negative controls are retained]
The packet retains the following cases as \(\Block\): annotation content
weakening or erasure, unmapped proxy addition or proxy-class alteration,
and non-orbit source-object variation. These retained failures prevent
the comparison rule from collapsing into a label-agreement test or a
target-success proxy.
\end{auditfinding}

\begin{residualrisk}[Negative controls are not systematic enough for general use]
The retained controls are sufficient for the orbit-local audit, but not
for general \(\EqSrc\). A broader theorem would require an independently
declared non-orbit object family, witness-pair inventory, invariant
ledger, comparison rule, and negative-control retention across that
family.
\end{residualrisk}
