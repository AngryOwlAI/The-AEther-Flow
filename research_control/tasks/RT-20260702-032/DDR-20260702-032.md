# DDR-20260702-032: v14 P10 Literature Findings Selector

## Decision

Route one bounded P10-T04 selector packet to
`theoretical-continuation-selector@0.1.0` through a task overlay.

## Basis

`handoff-0484` requires a findings selector before any repair, red-team,
theorem, obstruction, public-boundary, or downstream physics route. P10-T03
identified useful route risks but no immediate claim-promotion authority.

## Selected Role

The theoretical continuation selector is selected because the deliverable is a
route classification over science-facing findings. The role does not modify
canonical science sources and does not promote claims.

## Scope

Allowed:

- classify P10-T03 findings into route classes;
- select the lowest-authority next bounded route;
- preserve deferred findings for P11, P12, and P13.

Forbidden:

- execute a repair, theorem, red-team, obstruction, or public-boundary packet
  in the selector transaction;
- adopt source laws or external assumptions;
- derive matter coupling, stress-energy semantics, matter action, Einstein
  equations, benchmark status, or completed derivation.

## Route Decision

The selected next route is P10-T05 public-boundary review by
`documentation-curator@2.0.0`. This is the lowest-authority route because the
comparison artifact itself is complete, but public-facing language must not
turn literature resemblance into validation or promotion. Science-facing risks
are preserved for P11 through P13 rather than executed out of order.
