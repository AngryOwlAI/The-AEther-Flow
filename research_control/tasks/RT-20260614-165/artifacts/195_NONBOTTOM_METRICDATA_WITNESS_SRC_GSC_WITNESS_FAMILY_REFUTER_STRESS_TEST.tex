\section{Finite Chain-Family Stress Lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Finite directed chain-family stress survival]
For every finite \(n\ge 2\), under the source package \(\En\), admitted
chain-preserving source transition tokens, and repair-or-bottom finite
variation rule from \texttt{RT-20260614-163}, the audited
\(\Witness(\En)\) candidate survives the required Refuter stress dimensions
only as finite directed chain-family draft/control candidate data.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Collapse stress is handled by the response-profile condition. For the finite
chain \(\En\), omitting identities, the source profile of \(s_i\) is
\[
  p_n(s_i)=(n-i,i).
\]
If \(p_n(s_i)=p_n(s_j)\), then \(n-i=n-j\) and \(i=j\). Thus the declared
chain-family profiles distinguish sites.

Nonuniqueness stress is handled by the deterministic source fields: oriented
chain class, finite counting-measure class, reachability preorder,
transition-token action, response-profile distinguishability, and
repair-or-bottom variation behavior. Inside the stated source-gauge boundary
there is no independent valuation choice. If a broader equivalence policy is
demanded without a source-side definition, the packet cannot use target
geometry, file order, role identity, approval status, or validation status to
choose a record; the route must fail closed or be selected for a separate
source-side construction.

Source-transition covariance stress is handled by limiting \(K_{\tau}\) to
admitted source transition tokens preserving the directed chain package.
Orientation reversals that do not preserve the marked directed chain class
and non-chain automorphism pressures are outside the admitted transition
class. They cannot be repaired by target coordinates or a target metric.

Finite-variation stress is handled by the declared behavior: source relabeling
preserves the record, equivalent edge replacement repairs inside the source
package, and chain deletion, incidence breakage, profile collapse, target
contamination, process-authority laundering, or source-domain exit returns a
named bottom or \(\OBGEFF\) branch. Refinement and coarsening are not admitted
in this packet and therefore are not silent repairs.

Therefore every stress dimension either preserves finite directed
chain-family candidate status or fails closed. No branch yields adopted
\(\MetricData(E)\), an arbitrary source-package witness theorem, \(\geff\),
matter coupling, Einstein equations, benchmark promotion, completed
derivation, future source-extension impossibility, or global theory rejection.
\end{proof}
