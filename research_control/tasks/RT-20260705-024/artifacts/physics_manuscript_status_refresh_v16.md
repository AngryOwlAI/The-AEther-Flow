## Section-Level Refresh Guidance

### Exact-GR Benchmark Boundary

The manuscript should retain the v15 frame: exact-GR benchmark recovery is an
obligation target. Current v16 work improves control around source-side
certificate evidence and route selection, but it does not provide Einstein
equations, benchmark promotion, or completed GR derivation.

### Current Public-Safe Ontology Status

Use public-safe wording: AEther-flow is a proposed research ontology /
interpretation. It may be described as a source-first modeling program with
explicit burden tracking. It must not be described as an established physical
ontology or as an already completed derivation from first principles.

### Scoped `M_src` and Scoped `g_eff`

`M_src` and `g_eff` may be summarized as scoped source-side achievements under
their ledger qualifiers. The manuscript must keep the exact distinction:
`M_src` is not target spacetime and `g_eff` is not an unscoped Lorentzian
metric premise for matter coupling or Einstein equations.

### Matter-Sector Evidence and Preconditions

The matter-sector section should remain a status map. It may explain that the
project has scoped matter-sector evidence/preconditions and certificate
machinery. It must also state that matter semantics, detector semantics,
coupling-law adoption, stress-energy semantics, matter action, and universal
matter coupling remain open burdens.

### `NarrowMSCertEq_v1`, Certificate Algebra, and P4 Library

The manuscript may now cite v16 P4 and P5 progress:

- a concrete source certificate-instance library exists;
- the library contains 11 finite/local instances, including 3 positive
  instances and 8 negative fail-closed instances;

  supplied valid compatible composition certificate;

  transitivity claim without that supplied composition certificate.

This is meaningful manuscript material because it gives readers concrete
source-side controls. It is not downstream physical semantics and not a GR
