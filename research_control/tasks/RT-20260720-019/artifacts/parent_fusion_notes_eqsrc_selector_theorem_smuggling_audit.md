# Parent fusion: P2-T06 EqSrc selector-theorem audit

Both required perspectives support a source-purity pass for the exact written
theorem and its explicitly bounded Lean kernel. No target atlas, metric,
benchmark result, observer choice, physical time orientation, physical-gauge
premise, or repository-process state enters the audited mathematics.

The pass does not certify the proposal inputs. P2-T01 declares the signature,
admission predicate, arrow profiles, inverse certificates, candidate universe,
eligibility predicate, and relation map. P2-T04 likewise declares finite
structural actions and eligible-choice sets. Their definitions are
candidate-blind as written, but current ontology does not derive or adopt them,
and this audit does not show that the arrow class exhausts independently
motivated physical transformations.

The certified-isomorphism core is not a hidden restriction: it is the stated
domain of the product classification. P2-T03 separately records the
full-category equalizer conditions and warns that core fixedness may be
insufficient. The parent therefore classifies the issue as an explicit scope
boundary and provenance limitation rather than a theorem defect.

The Lean source is semantically faithful for the one-object fixed-point kernel:
empty fixed locus, transitive uniqueness, the distinct-choice obstruction,
multiple fixed choices, the sign-swap fixture, and the trivial-action control.
It does not mechanize the multi-component product, choice qualification,
relation-image theorem, or full-category equalizer. Because this limitation is
explicit in the source and receipt, the result is a bounded translation rather
than a false claim of general mechanization.

The mathematical child found a precise, theorem-nonfatal P2-T04 metric defect.
The atlas validator's `relation_image_sizes` field computes raw `K(D)` over all
eligible choices. P2-T03 relation sections instead arise from fixed choices
and global selectors, so an empty-fixed fixture has empty selector-induced
image even when raw `K(D)` is nonempty. The current
`relation_stabilizer_order` calculation is the kernel order of the induced
action on `K(D)`, not generally the stabilizer of an individual relation. The
parent preserves both findings and rejects any overread of those two metric
labels. The group, orbit, fixed-set, and no-selector conclusions remain valid.

The parent verdict is
`conditional_source_purity_pass_with_precise_p2_t04_k_metric_scope_defect`.
No P2-T03 theorem repair is required. A bounded P2-T04 metric-name and report
repair is required before those fields are reused, but repair is outside this
audit frame. Adoption, physical gauge, proof authority, general EqSrc,
scientific-ledger changes, and promotion remain blocked. The exact next v21
route is P2-T07 under `refuter@0.2.0`, which must stress the unchanged audited
theorem without relying on the two uncorrected metric labels and is not
executed here.
