# Director Decision Record — RT-20260809-021

## Decision

Bind one bounded `ontology-formalizer@0.2.0` task overlay to the exact inactive
B2 candidate `CAND-V22-B2-P7-COMMON-PRINCIPAL-LIFT-V1`. Preserve the protected
finite P7 package, the candidate family fingerprint, B1 termination, the exact
readiness vector `(1,1,1,1,0,0)`, and the source-only claim boundary.

The route is `ontology-law-research-packet`, the trigger is

`blocked_adoption_open_continuation`. Current ontology does not derive a
finite-P7-to-continuum lift, continuum sector equations, a source compatibility
selector, or a common reduced projective principal construction. This does not

## Mathematical burden

The formalizer must provide new payload rather than another generic checklist.
The selected packet therefore requires:

- a typed ten-component descriptor law;
- seven blocking families and 35 atomic obligations;
- a projective common-principal gluing theorem with hypotheses and proof;
- a finite-trace principal-class underdetermination theorem and explicit
  counterpair;
- the exact P2-T01 ten-field interface; and
- a concrete next-role consequence.

The common object must be defined projectively in source cotangent data, up to
nowhere-zero source units and a cocycle. No quadratic degree, Lorentzian
signature, time orientation, physical scale, target atlas, or desired GR cone
may be assumed.

## Exact result boundary

The packet may complete the descriptor specification while leaving the
descriptor instance incomplete. It may prove a scoped obstruction to selection
from finite sampled traces, but it may not claim that B2, nonlocal lifts, dense
refinement limits, or future source extensions are impossible. It may not
activate B2, reevaluate P2-T01, unlock P4-T03, adopt a source law, or create a
physical causal or metric claim.

## Next route

`smuggling-auditor@0.2.0` ontology-law packet to audit the descriptor law,
theorem premises, counterpair, projective gluing rule, and all 35 atomic
obligations. Candidate construction remains later and separate.
