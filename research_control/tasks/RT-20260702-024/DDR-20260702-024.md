## Decision

Select `project-control-maintainer@0.2.0` for one bounded v14 P9-T01 external
red-team role-contract packet.

## Reasoning

Handoff `handoff-0476` requires P9-T01 before any red-team template, pilot,
selector, literature comparison, or downstream physics route. The existing
`external-red-team-reviewer@0.1.0` role is already registered, but the v14 plan

contract is project-control role governance, not physics research.

Permanent role-contract update is protected role authority. The user granted
human authorization for all gates in the active goal, and this decision narrows
that authorization through `approval-20260702-001` to a non-promotional contract
update only.
