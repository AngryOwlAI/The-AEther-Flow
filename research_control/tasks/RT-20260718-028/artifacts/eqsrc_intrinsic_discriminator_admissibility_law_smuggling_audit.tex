\section{Pointwise Stability versus Relation Preservation}

\begin{theorem}[Translation label--relation separation]
Let \(A\) be an abelian group, \(B\leq A\), and
\(\chi:A\to A/B\) the quotient. For \(c\in A\), define
\(S_c(z)=z+c\). Then
\[
\chi(S_cz)=\chi(z)+[c].
\]
Thus \(S_c\) fixes every quotient value if and only if \(c\in B\). Nevertheless,
for every \(c\in A\),
\[
z-z'\in B
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
S_c(z)-S_c(z')\in B.
\]
Hence every translation preserves and reflects the kernel-pair relation.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Quotient linearity gives the first equation. The second statement follows
from
\[
(z+c)-(z'+c)=z-z'.
\]
\end{proof}

The candidate's admitted boundary translations are precisely the pointwise
quotient-value stabilizer among all translations. They are not the full
relation stabilizer: every fixed translation is a relation automorphism.
