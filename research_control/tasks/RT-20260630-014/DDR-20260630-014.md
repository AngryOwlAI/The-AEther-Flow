# Director Decision Record: DDR-20260630-014

## Decision

Execute the `handoff-0365` next action as one bounded
`theoretical-continuation-selector@0.1.0` task overlay. The selector must
choose the next non-promotional matter-coupling continuation route after
`RT-20260630-013` accepted `MCPA^cand_v1(E)` only as scoped
source-extension matter-coupling precondition evidence/precondition.

## Basis

`RT-20260630-013` resolved the protected Gate Chair question for the
proposal-only pre-coupling assembly candidate. The result is useful for
continuation, but it is not source-law adoption, not source-extension data
adoption beyond the exact gate result, not a coupling law, not matter
coupling, not stress-energy semantics, not detector semantics, not a matter
action, not `MetricData(E)`, not a `g_eff` scope expansion, and not Einstein
equations.

The immediate route question is therefore not construction, audit, stress, or
another Gate Chair verdict. It is a post-gate route decision: identify the
next bounded source-side target that can use the accepted MCPA
evidence/precondition without overreading it. The Theoretical Continuation
Selector is the narrowest role for that decision.

## Milestone And Burden

Target derivation milestone: `matter_coupling`.

Milestone burden: classify the post-Gate Chair route after scoped
`MCPA^cand_v1(E)` evidence/precondition acceptance before any source-law
adoption, coupling-law adoption, matter-coupling derivation, stress-energy
semantics, detector semantics, matter action, Einstein-equation route,
benchmark route, or completed-derivation claim.

## Result

The selector chooses one future `ontology-formalizer@0.2.0`
`ontology-law-research-packet`. The future packet should formalize a
proposal-only `SourceCouplingLawTarget_v1(E)` target that consumes

source-extension data beyond the exact RT-013 gate result, coupling law,
matter coupling, stress-energy semantics, detector semantics, matter action,
Einstein equations, benchmark status, or completed derivation.
