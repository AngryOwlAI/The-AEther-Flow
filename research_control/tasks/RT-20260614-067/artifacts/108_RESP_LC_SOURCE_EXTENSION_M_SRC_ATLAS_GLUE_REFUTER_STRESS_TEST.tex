\section{Stress Question}

\[

\Msrc\text{ construction, or does it remain underdetermined by transition and label data?}
\]

The stress target is not the conditional theorem itself. If an admissible

\section{Transition-Token Nonuniqueness}

\begin{countermodel}[Duplicate-compatible-token transitions]
Let
\[
  U_X=\{x\},\qquad U_Y=\{y_0,y_1\}.
\]
Let the source readout and response-label comparison rule classify both
\(y_0\) and \(y_1\) as compatible with \(x\):
\[
  \chi_X^+(x)\sim_{\mathrm{cmp}}\chi_Y^+(y_0),
  \qquad
  \chi_X^+(x)\sim_{\mathrm{cmp}}\chi_Y^+(y_1).
\]
Let both pairs lie in the declared source overlap relation:
\[
  (x,y_0)\in\Ov_{XY},\qquad (x,y_1)\in\Ov_{XY}.
\]
Then the partial bijection \(\tau_{XY,0}^+(x)=y_0\) and the partial
bijection \(\tau_{XY,1}^+(x)=y_1\) both satisfy local label compatibility.
They generate different quotient supports:
\[
  [(X,x)]=[(Y,y_0)]\quad\text{or}\quad
  [(X,x)]=[(Y,y_1)].
\]
Unless a further source-side discriminator is supplied, the local readout and
label-compatibility conditions do not select one transition-token family.
\end{countermodel}

This countermodel does not use target topology, target smooth charts, metric
scale, calibration, benchmark behavior, validation status, or role authority.
It is a source-internal nonuniqueness pressure on direct \(\Msrc\)
construction.

\section{Label-Compatibility Collapse}

\begin{obstruction}[Coarse comparison collapses distinct source sites]
If the label-comparison rule is too coarse, an admissible transition family can
identify distinct source sites that differ only by data ignored by the
comparison rule. In the duplicate-compatible-token model, a transition policy
that alternately chooses \(y_0\) and \(y_1\), or a transitive closure combining
both choices through additional patches, can force
\[
  [(Y,y_0)]=[(Y,y_1)]
\]
even when \(y_0\) and \(y_1\) are distinct source-site tokens. Thus label
compatibility alone is not enough. The comparison rule must carry explicit
source-side discriminants that prevent collapse when collapse is not intended.
\end{obstruction}

\begin{lemma}[Supplied datum versus derived datum]
The \(\AtlasGlue\) theorem proves a quotient support for a supplied admissible
datum. It does not derive the admissible transition family or the
label-comparison discriminator from the old \(\SX\) tuple or from the audit
pass.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The primitive lists \(\Cov\), \(\Ov\), \(\Tau\), and source-token comparison
rules as components or assumptions of the \(\AtlasGlue\) datum. The proof of
well-defined quotient support begins after those components are supplied.
Therefore the theorem cannot also be read as deriving those components from
the retained exact-branch tuple, from \(\XiR\), from validation status, or
from role authority. That would change the dependency basis.
\end{proof}

\section{Finite-Variation Fragility}

The formal primitive proves stability only under strict source-isomorphic
finite variation. The countermodel above gives a concrete non-isomorphic
finite perturbation: duplicate a compatible target-side source token, or
delete a discriminating comparison datum. Local germ syntax can remain
unchanged while the set of admissible transition-token families changes. Thus
arbitrary finite-variation robustness remains undischarged.

\section{Overread Screens}

\paragraph{Target-atlas side-door import.}
The countermodels do not require target topology or target smooth atlas data.
Their point is stronger: direct construction is already underdetermined inside
the source-token interface. Importing target charts would only mask the
missing source-side discriminator.

\paragraph{Quotient-support overread.}
\(\Qsrc\) is canonical only relative to a chosen admissible transition family
and comparison rule. The notation must not be read as a unique source
manifold support determined by local labels alone.

\paragraph{Source-authority laundering.}
Coverage, overlap, transition, and comparison data must be explicit
source-side data. They cannot be inferred from the word ``source'', registry
visibility, validation success, handoff status, generated derivatives, role
labels, target charts, or benchmark behavior.

\paragraph{Bridge-ladder collapse.}
The sequence ``formal source-only primitive, smuggling-audit pass, finite
witness'' does not by itself become \(\Msrc\). The missing rung is a source
rule or source datum that selects and stabilizes atlas transitions.
