\section{Candidate and family identity}

\paragraph{Candidate descriptor.}
A candidate packet $C$ must declare
\[
 C=(X_C,A_C,D_C,O_C,P_C,R_C,Q_C),
\]
where $X_C$ is the source field/state content, $A_C$ is the explicit
assumption multiset, $D_C$ is the source dynamics or transition law, $O_C$ is
the source-to-operational interface, $P_C$ is the proposed principal-structure
mechanism, $R_C$ is its continuum or refinement contract, and $Q_C$ is its
source-provenance and no-target-import receipt. Missing fields fail closed.

\paragraph{Prospective family fingerprint.}
Let $N$ normalize notation, field renamings, invertible parameter changes, and
presentation-only auxiliary variables. Define
\[
 \iota(C)=N(X_C,A_C,D_C,O_C,P_C,R_C,Q_C).
\]
Two packets are family-equivalent, $C\sim_{\rm fam}C'$, when their normalized
fingerprints have the same source primitives, assumption cost, dynamics,
operational interface, principal mechanism, refinement burden, and forbidden
target-provenance profile. A renamed route, reparameterized coefficients, a
new file layout, or additional validator does not create a new family.

\paragraph{Material distinction.}
Packets are materially distinct only when a prospective difference in source
field content, source law, principal mechanism, operational interface, or
continuum/refinement construction changes at least one falsifiable Gate B
burden without importing target data. The difference and its assumption delta
must be declared before results. Familiarity, implementation language, author,
role, or label is never material distinction.

\section{Sequential activation state machine}

Let the protocol state be
\[
 S=(a,U,H,E),
\]
where $a\in\Fam\cup\{\bot\}$ is the sole active primary family, $U$ is the
set of unused or reserved slots, $H$ is an append-only activation and
termination history, and $E$ is the immutable evidence map for every state
transition. The invariant is $|\{a\}|\leq 1$.

Allowed transitions are:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Select.} P2-T04 records one primary and one fallback; no
  candidate runs merely because it is selected.
  \item \textbf{Activate primary.} Activate the selected primary only after
  its complete descriptor, assumption ledger, family fingerprint, and test
  plan are sealed.
  \item \textbf{Terminate.} Record either qualifying-for-protected-review or
  a named hard failure. The record and all negative results are append-only.
  \item \textbf{Activate fallback.} Require a terminated primary, a preserved
  termination receipt, and a fresh selector decision that names the exact
  preregistered fallback.
  \item \textbf{Allocate slot 3.} Require both a qualifying independent-review
  finding of material distinctness and a new explicit program decision. The
  decision cannot erase prior failures or create a fourth slot.
  \item \textbf{Terminalize.} When all allocated slots are consumed without a
  qualifying candidate, use only the scoped insufficiency template below.
\end{enumerate}

Forbidden transitions include concurrent activation, fallback activation
before primary termination, deletion or weakening of a failure, post-result
family definition, renamed reuse of a frozen family, silent slot-3 allocation,
and budget growth.

\paragraph{Finite-budget termination theorem.}
Assume every activation terminates in finite control time as either
$\Qual$ or $\Term$, every overlap debits all implicated slots, and family
equivalence is enforced by $\iota$. Then the protocol admits at most three
primary activations and no infinite sequence of renamed fallbacks.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Each lawful activation consumes at least one previously unused slot from the
three-element set $\Fam$. The append-only ledger prevents consumption from
being reversed. A family-equivalent rename has the same fingerprint and hence
cannot enter $U$ as a new element. An overlapping candidate consumes rather
than creates its secondary slots. Therefore after at most three activations no
unused slot remains, so a fourth activation is impossible without a new
explicit program decision outside this protocol. $\square$

\paragraph{Failure-monotonicity lemma.}
If $H_t$ is the ledger after transition $t$, every lawful transition satisfies
$H_t\subseteq H_{t+1}$. Consequently a failed premise, countermodel, instability,
or target-import finding cannot be erased by later selection or renaming.

\section{Gate B eligibility predicate}

A candidate is eligible only for later protected Gate B review when every
criterion is supported by source provenance:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item target-atlas-free construction;
  \item gauge- and constraint-reduced hyperbolic principal polynomial;
  \item one stable Lorentzian cone or a physically derived equivalence class;
  \item universal compatibility across declared matter sectors;
  \item operational clock and rod calibration and physical scale;
  \item tensorial transformation and local-to-global gluing;
  \item finite-variation and coarse-graining robustness; and
  \item no hidden metric, tetrad, coframe, or equivalent constitutive primitive.
\end{enumerate}
It must also report regularity, time orientation, nondegeneracy, conditioning,
and second-clock-effect analysis. Passing this predicate means only
\emph{eligible for protected review}; it is not a positive Gate B verdict.

\section{Hard-fail predicate}

Terminate and preserve the candidate when any disjunct of $\Fail(C)$ holds:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item an underived metric, tetrad, coframe, Lorentzian constitutive tensor,
  or equivalent quadratic structure is inserted;
  \item source labels are identified with clocks, rods, locations, cones, or
  target observables by fiat;
  \item free coefficients are selected by fitting GR targets;
  \item universal propagation is abandoned or inequivalent physical cones are
  tolerated without a derived selector or quotient;
  \item ghosts, instability, nonhyperbolicity, degeneracy, or uncontrolled
  signature change are accepted;
  \item a controlled continuum or refinement limit is absent;
  \item the Einstein--Hilbert action or Einstein equations are postulated as a
  derivation; or
  \item an information-deficient frozen route is repeated under a new name.
\end{enumerate}
Hard failure is candidate-local. It cannot be converted into a global no-go
claim without a separate theorem and applicable protected authority.
