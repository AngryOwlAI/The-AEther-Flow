## Selected Mathematical Route

A prospective candidate is normalized to a source-side descriptor containing
its fields, domains, symmetries, locality class, dynamics, observables,
continuum or refinement rule, assumptions, and forbidden target imports. Two
descriptors share a family when source-side isomorphisms preserve these data
and their principal operational structure; renaming, auxiliary elimination,
or equivalent repackaging does not create a new slot.

Exactly three family classes are registered: local multi-field continuum
dynamics, matter-principal-polynomial reconstruction, and controlled
discrete-to-continuum refinement. At most one primary family may be active.
A lawful combined candidate debits every implicated slot but does not activate
multiple primaries or grow the budget. Failures are append-only. Therefore the
state machine admits at most three activation episodes and terminates after a
finite number of hard-fail transitions.

## Claim Boundary

All three entries are inactive proposal-only family classes, not source laws,
canonical-ontology candidates, or adopted extensions. P2-T04 retains the task
of selecting one primary, pre-registering one fallback, and leaving the third
slot reserved. Gate B remains NOT READY. Exhausting the budget may establish
only that the current ontology plus the preregistered family budget did not
supply an admissible candidate; it is not a global impossibility theorem.
