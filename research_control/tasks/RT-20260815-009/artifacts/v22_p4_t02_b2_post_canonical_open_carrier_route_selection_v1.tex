\section{Exact selector controls}

\subsection{Carrier-law nonidentifiability}

Hold fixed the two-element formal carrier
\(X=\{\varnothing,U\}\). For every \(p\in[0,1]\), define
\begin{equation}
  K_p(\varnothing)=p,\qquad K_p(U)=1-p.
  \label{eq:kernel-simplex}
\end{equation}
Every \(K_p\) is normalized, but \(K_p\neq K_q\) for \(p\neq q\). Thus the
Open-carrier, restriction, and descent axioms do not determine a kernel. These
are formal mathematical kernels, not physical probabilities or frequencies.

\subsection{Equivariance is not token selection}

Let a two-token fiber \(Y=\{L,R\}\) carry the swap \(s(L)=R\),
\(s(R)=L\). No deterministic choice \(y\in Y\) is fixed by \(s\), so there is
no deterministic equivariant section. The uniform kernel
\begin{equation}
  K_{1/2}=\tfrac12\delta_L+\tfrac12\delta_R
\end{equation}
is invariant. Probability-valued naturality therefore does not realize one
token. A constructive source law must either preserve this symmetric
nonselection or disclose an additional source-side asymmetry; it may not hide
that asymmetry in a desired output.

\subsection{Dependency order}

A Refuter can classify a typed admissibility subfunctor or disprove a typed
candidate. It cannot generate the absent proposal fiber, admissibility rule,
kernel, and occurrence generator from carrier nonselection alone. Conversely,
a distinct P4 irrelevance theorem is not formed until there is an independently
current-source-defined P4-relevant codomain \(C_{P4}\), a typed map into it,
and a new factorization or nonfactorization claim. Those data are absent. Under
the selector's constructive preference, a transparent proposal-only
construction-or-obstruction packet is therefore prior to candidate-specific
audit and stress.

\subsection{A new invariant-subsheaf control}

The assignment
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{Clop}(U)=\{A\in\Open(U):U\setminus A\in\Open(U)\}
\end{equation}
is nonempty, full-diffeomorphism invariant, and stable under inverse image.
Compatible local clopen subsets glue to a clopen global subset, so
\(\operatorname{Clop}(-)\) is a subsheaf of the Open carrier. It is proper on
every positive-dimensional source object and consists exactly of unions of
connected components; on connected objects it reduces to
\(\{\varnothing,U\}\).

This gives Route A genuinely new theorem content beyond RT007. It does not,
however, select a law: on connected objects the natural family
\begin{equation}
 \mu_{t,U}=(1-t)\delta_{\varnothing}+t\delta_U,
 \qquad 0\leq t\leq1,
\end{equation}
remains normalized and restriction-natural. Moreover, the endpoint assignment
\(E(U)=\{\varnothing,U\}\) is restriction-stable but can fail cover descent on
disconnected covers. These exact results are retained as the immediate future
Refuter control after a concrete proposal-only law exists.
