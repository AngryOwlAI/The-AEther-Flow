# Exact Operational Identity, Interpretive Ontology, and the Open Derivation Burden

## Internal publication-preparation manuscript

This is a task-local, noncanonical internal manuscript prepared for source and
claim review. It is not a public release, a submission, an ontology decision, a
benchmark-promotion decision, or a Gate Chair verdict. Registered TeX sources
and tracked claim-boundary records remain authoritative.

## Abstract

The Æther-Flow Interpretation of Relativity adopts ordinary general relativity
exactly as its effective observable-scale gravitational theory. With the matter
sector, initial data, and boundary conditions held fixed, its effective
observables are therefore identical to those of GR. The package uses one
operative Lorentzian metric, universal matter coupling, standard null and
proper-time relations, ordinary diffeomorphism structure, and the two tensor
gravitational degrees of freedom of GR. At this effective scope it offers no
independent low-energy non-GR prediction.

The Æther / Æther-flow vocabulary plays a different role: it is the project’s
proposed interpretive ontology for what the exact relativistic structure may
describe. Exact operational identity does not establish formal source
equivalence, genuine emergence, empirical novelty, or a first-principles
substrate-to-GR derivation. Current non-empirical ontology criteria remain
unmet or indeterminate, and source-purity alone is necessary but insufficient
for positive physical provenance. The manuscript consequently presents an
exact-GR interpretive benchmark and an open research program, not evidence that
the deeper ontology has been established.

## 1. The two-level statement

The research program separates an effective benchmark from a deeper
derivational ambition.

1. At the effective level, the active benchmark adopts GR with the same matter
   sector and one operative metric. This fixes observable-scale dynamics and
   measurement rules.
2. At the ontological level, Æther and Æther-flow are proposed as a deeper
   interpretation. Their source-side laws have not yet been shown to force the
   benchmark without importing target structure.

The word *adopts* is essential. It says what law the benchmark uses. It does
not say where that law came from. The word *derives* is reserved for a
source-to-target construction that discharges the tracked source ontology,
effective-metric, matter-coupling, and field-equation burdens.

In compact form: GR is the adopted effective law, and the first-principles substrate derivation remains open.

The current exact-GR package therefore has a strong but deliberately bounded
status: it is a definite effective theory and interpretive redescription. It
is not presently a distinct low-energy alternative to GR and does not supply
independent empirical novelty at that scale.

## 2. Exact effective dynamics

The benchmark effective action is the Einstein-Hilbert action with universally
coupled matter:

$$
S_{\mathrm{eff}}
=
\frac{c^3}{16\pi G}\int d^4x\,\sqrt{-g}\,R
+ S_{\mathrm{matter}}[g,\psi].
$$

This equation is copied in substance from the registered foundations,
dynamics, consistency, and relativistic-recovery TeX sources. It is an adopted
effective law, not the endpoint of a substrate derivation.

Varying that adopted action gives the benchmark field equations:

<!-- equation-trace: EQ-002 -->
$$
G_{\mu\nu}=\frac{8\pi G}{c^4}T_{\mu\nu}.
$$

Within this benchmark, all matter and light couple to the same metric
$g_{\mu\nu}$. The consistency source records the ordinary diffeomorphism gauge
redundancy, constraint structure, and two propagating tensor polarizations of
GR. It does not add an independent low-energy Æther-flow scalar, vector, or
preferred-frame field.

## 3. Exact operational identity

For fixed matter sector, initial data, and boundary conditions, the registered
relativistic-recovery source states the effective identity as

<!-- equation-trace: EQ-003 -->
$$
\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{theory}}=\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{GR}}
$$

for each observable defined entirely within the effective metric-matter
sector. This is stronger than an approximate weak-field match. It follows
because the benchmark adopts the same effective action, coupling rule, and
boundary-value problem as GR.

It is also narrower than a derivation claim. The equality says that two
effective descriptions return the same observables under matched inputs. It
does not by itself identify a source-side construction, prove a quotient
equivalence, select a unique source law, or show how Lorentzian geometry and
matter coupling emerge from substrate variables.

The standard measurement relations make the same operational point. Light
follows the null structure of the one operative metric,

<!-- equation-trace: EQ-004 -->
$$
0=g_{\mu\nu}\,dx^{\mu}dx^{\nu},
$$

and ideal-clock proper time is

<!-- equation-trace: EQ-005 -->
$$
d\tau^2=-\frac{1}{c^2}g_{\mu\nu}\,dx^{\mu}dx^{\nu}.
$$

Redshift, causal order, local Lorentz behavior, and the standard observer-facing
relativistic tests are consequently those of GR at the benchmark scope.

## 4. What the flow interpretation contributes

The flow-geometry source uses ordinary GR congruence kinematics as a disciplined
interpretive dictionary. Given a chosen unit timelike observer congruence
$u^\mu$, its rest-space projector is

<!-- equation-trace: EQ-006 -->
$$
h_{\mu\nu}=g_{\mu\nu}+\frac{1}{c^2}u_\mu u_\nu.
$$

Expansion, shear, vorticity, and acceleration of a congruence then organize
different relativistic situations without replacing the Einstein equation.
This avoids reducing all gravity to a literal three-dimensional wind or vortex.

At current scope, however, the congruence is an admissible observer choice
inside the adopted metric geometry. It is not a uniquely selected field
already derived from substrate microphysics. The interpretive dictionary is
therefore useful and mathematically disciplined without becoming a second
low-energy dynamics or evidence that the proposed ontology is physically
established.

## 5. Four success categories must remain separate

The P14-T01 source separates four propositions that are often blurred:

| Category | Question | Current exact-GR package |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interpretive redescription | Does the ontology organize an exact-GR description coherently? | Met at the bounded benchmark scope. |
| Formal equivalence | Is there a proved structure-preserving source/target equivalence? | Indeterminate; no such general proof follows from operational identity. |
| Genuine emergence | Are GR structures derived from more primitive source variables without target import? | Not met. |
| Empirical novelty | Is there a distinct confirmed prediction or constraint beyond GR? | Not met at the exact effective scope. |

No row entails another by definition. In particular, exact observable
agreement supports interpretive redescription, but it cannot be used as
evidence that the ontology caused, selected, or derived the GR structure.

## 6. Non-empirical ontology value and positive provenance

Empirical equivalence does not make every ontology equally successful, but it
also does not license an aesthetic or workflow score as a scientific verdict.
P14-T02 supplies seven non-compensatory evidence coordinates. Its current
assessment records zero met coordinates, six not-met coordinates, and one
indeterminate coordinate. A strong result on one coordinate cannot erase a
missing burden on another, and *not met* is not an impossibility theorem.

P14-T03 adds a second discipline. A construction that does not import target
metric or matter structure passes a necessary purity condition, but purity is
not sufficient for physical meaning. Positive provenance also requires, as
applicable, source-side dynamics, discriminating or uniqueness control,
operational meaning, robustness, and evidence appropriate to the protected
gate. Validator success and internal agreement are not substitutes for those
ingredients.

These two results explain how the project can study an empirically equivalent
interpretation without treating consistency, elegance, source purity, or
exact-GR agreement as automatic ontology adoption.

## 7. Open derivational burdens

The current tracked burden chain remains open upstream of benchmark promotion.
The project still needs source-side laws and evidence sufficient to establish,
without target import:

- a derivation-ready source ontology;
- a general source-equivalence or selector result rather than only scoped
  candidate facts and negative results;
- an effective Lorentzian metric with operational clock and causal meaning;
- universal matter coupling and stress-energy semantics;
- a source-side action or equivalent dynamics whose variation yields the
  Einstein equations; and
- protected review and human authorization for any stronger benchmark or
  publication claim.

Finite witnesses, proposal-only source extensions, scoped obstructions, and
internal validators can advance or close exact subquestions. They do not, by
themselves, discharge the whole chain. The appropriate current phrase is that
the exact-GR benchmark is adopted while its first-principles substrate
derivation remains open.

## 8. Empirical status and falsifiability

At the exact effective scope, the benchmark deliberately has the same empirical
content as GR. It therefore has no independent low-energy non-GR signature to
claim. Any future deviation sector would be a separate extension and would
need its own source law, consistency analysis, observational comparison, and
claim gate. It cannot be inferred from the ontology vocabulary alone.

The deeper program remains scientifically assessable through its construction
burdens. A proposed source law can fail covariance, uniqueness, robustness,
matter-coupling, or no-target-import tests. A named route can be refuted or
frozen at exact scope. Such failures are informative, but a scoped failure is
not a global rejection of every possible extension unless a separate theorem
supports that quantifier.

## 10. Conclusion

The exact-GR interpretive package is definite about its effective theory and
equally definite about its limits. It adopts the Einstein-Hilbert action,
Einstein equations, one operative metric, universal matter coupling, and the
observable predictions of GR. The Æther / Æther-flow layer is a proposed
interpretive ontology expressed through a disciplined relativistic dictionary.

Operational identity establishes neither source equivalence nor emergence.
It supplies no independent low-energy novelty, does not settle the current
non-empirical ontology criteria, and does not close the tracked substrate
derivation. The scientifically honest publication posture is therefore:
exact-GR benchmark, interpretive proposal, open derivation burden, no physics
promotion, and no external release without review and human authorization.
