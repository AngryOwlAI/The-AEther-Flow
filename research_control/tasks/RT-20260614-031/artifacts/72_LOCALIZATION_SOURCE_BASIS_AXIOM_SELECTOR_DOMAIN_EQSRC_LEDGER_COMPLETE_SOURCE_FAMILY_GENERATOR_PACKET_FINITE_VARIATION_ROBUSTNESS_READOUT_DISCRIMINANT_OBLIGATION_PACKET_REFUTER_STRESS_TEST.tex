\begin{abstract}
This draft/control Refuter artifact stress-tests the audited
\texttt{RT-20260614-029} finite-variation robustness and
readout-discriminant obligation packet and the \texttt{RT-20260614-030}
Smuggling Auditor local conditional pass. The packet survives only as
a source-side obligation interface: it gives useful names for what
must be supplied before any local finite-variation robustness claim can
be audited. It does not survive as robustness evidence. Counterexample
schemas show that representative equivalence, boundary equivalence,
quotient composition, inverse provenance, readout discriminating
power, readout minimality, and negative-witness coverage remain open or
repair-bound. General \(\EqSrc\), \(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), downstream
bridge claims, candidate reconstruction, canonical ontology edit,
benchmark promotion, Gate Chair review, completed-derivation language,
and global theory rejection remain blocked.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and Stress Question}

This stress test responds to \texttt{handoff-0072}. The stress target
is not a claimed theorem of finite-variation robustness. The stress
target is an audited obligation suite whose local value depends on
whether it cleanly exposes failure modes without silently converting
open burdens into evidence.

The stress question is:
\[
    \text{Does the audited obligation packet withstand counterexample
    pressure as robustness or readout-discriminant evidence?}
\]

The answer is no. The packet is retained as a source-side test
interface, but its named obligations remain undischarged.

\section{Stress Method}

\begin{stressscreen}[Obligation interface versus evidence]
Each named burden \(\RepLC,\BndLC,\CompLC,\InvLC,\MinLC,\DiscLC\),
and \(\NegLC\) is tested for overread. A stress case succeeds if it
shows that a future proof must supply additional source-side data
before the obligation can be treated as discharged.
\end{stressscreen}

\begin{stressscreen}[Source-side pressure only]
The stress cases vary quotient representatives, boundary anchors,
finite certificate steps, inverse histories, readout coordinates, and
negative witnesses. They do not use target metric, target proper time,
target locality, observer protocol, candidate behavior, benchmark
rank, validation status, registry status, generated visibility,
handoff status, or authority label as evidence.
\end{stressscreen}

\section{Representative and Boundary Counterexamples}

\begin{counterexample}[Representative-equivalence underdefinition]
Let \(q\) and \(q'\) be two quotient representatives that a future
source-side relation \(\Theta_Q\) may identify. The current packet
requires a definition of \(\Theta_Q\), a lifted readout relation or
explicit exact-equality policy, certificate-composition preservation,
and inverse-provenance preservation. If \(q\) is replaced by \(q'\)
before those four data are supplied, two failures are possible:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item exact \(\ReadLC\) equality rejects a variation that the
    future quotient relation would accept; or
    \item quotienting \(\ReadLC\) accepts a variation while losing the
    certificate history needed to reconstruct the source ledger.
\end{enumerate}
Thus \(\RepLC\) cannot be counted as \(\Pass\). The correct local
status is \(\Pause\) until source-only representative data are
supplied, or \(\Repair\) if a proposed representative relation loses
certificate history.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Boundary-equivalence transport gap]
Let boundary anchors \((i,o)\) and \((i',o')\) be proposed as
equivalent by a future \(\Theta_{\partial}\). Exact boundary anchors
are conservative but not robust. Relaxed boundary equality is not
licensed unless a source-only boundary transport law proves that
\(\CertLC\) certificates, inverse recovery, and readout treatment are
preserved under the relaxation.

The stress result is local \(\Pause\): the obligation packet correctly
names \(\BndLC\), but no source-only transport law has been supplied.
Any future boundary relaxation chosen because it improves downstream
target behavior or bridge convenience returns \(\Block\).
\end{counterexample}

\section{Composition and Inverse Provenance}

\begin{counterexample}[Noncomposing quotient representatives]
Suppose two certificate steps are locally plausible:
\[
    X \xrightarrow{s_1} Z \xrightarrow{s_2} Y .
\]
If \(s_1\) outputs representative \(q_Z\) while \(s_2\) consumes a
distinct representative \(q_Z'\), endpoint readout equality can still
hold after quotient relaxation even though composition has no
well-defined intermediate representative. A source-side composition
ledger must state identities, associativity, representative matching,
and compatibility with endpoint readout.

Without that ledger, \(\CompLC\) remains \(\Pause\) or \(\Repair\).
Endpoint readout agreement is not a composition proof.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Endpoint-equal inverse-provenance loss]
Construct two refinements with the same endpoint multisets of blockers,
influence records, quotient labels, and boundary anchors, but with the
blocker and influence provenance attached to different refined
branches. If \(\ReadLC\) sees only endpoint multisets, then
\(\ReadLC(X)=\ReadLC(Y)\) can hold while the inverse data needed to
recover branch-level history fail.

This blocks any converse use of \(\ReadLC\) and any robustness claim
that omits inverse provenance. \(\InvLC\) is not discharged by readout
equality.
\end{counterexample}

\section{Readout Discriminant Stress}

\begin{counterexample}[Readout under-discrimination]
Let two source objects share the same recorded readout coordinates but
differ in certificate history, branch provenance, or intermediate
representative matching. If those differences matter to \(\CertLC\),
then \(\ReadLC\) equality under-discriminates. A finite discriminant
table must specify which variation families each coordinate detects
and which histories are deliberately ignored with a source-side proof
of irrelevance.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Readout overcompletion]
Conversely, \(\ReadLC\) may retain coordinates that are redundant for
the declared local certificate relation. Overcomplete readout is not a
failure for local one-way invariance, but it blocks minimality claims.
Keeping a coordinate because it helps downstream target behavior,
benchmark rank, candidate behavior, or bridge convenience would be
target import. Keeping it because it protects a source-side negative
family is admissible only if the finite table says so.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{stressfinding}[Minimality and discriminating power remain split]
The obligation packet correctly separates \(\MinLC\) and \(\DiscLC\).
The stress result preserves that split. A readout can be nonminimal and
still discriminate the declared source relation; it can also be small
and fail to discriminate inverse provenance. Neither burden is
discharged here.
\end{stressfinding}

\section{Negative-Witness Coverage}

\begin{counterexample}[Missing negative families]
The current near-miss negative witness covers proxy weakening and
blocker erasure. It does not cover influence provenance swaps,
representative substitution, noncomposing quotient refinement,
boundary perturbation, readout-coordinate relaxation,
readout-coordinate deletion, or endpoint-equal provenance shifts.
Therefore \(\NegLC\) remains \(\Repair\).

A later robustness claim must either supply at least one source-side
negative member for each missing family or prove by source-side
criteria that the family is irrelevant to the declared local
certificate relation.
\end{counterexample}

\section{Failure Conditions and Authority Boundaries}

\begin{stressfinding}[Failure-condition sharpness]
The packet's \(\Pass\), \(\Pause\), \(\Repair\), and \(\Kill\)
calculus is useful, but \(\Kill\) requires care. A local \(\Kill\) is
permitted only after the named obligation is shown necessary for the
declared source objective and all source-only repairs are exhausted.
It is not a global theory rejection and it is not a Gate Chair verdict.
\end{stressfinding}

\begin{controlrule}[No authority-label overread]
The Smuggling Auditor pass, validator success, registry rows, generated
wiki note, task closure, handoff status, and role labels remain
process receipts. They do not supply representative equivalence,
boundary equivalence, quotient composition, inverse provenance, readout
minimality, discriminating power, negative-family coverage, bridge
relevance, \(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), candidate reconstruction,
benchmark status, or Gate Chair authority.
\end{controlrule}

\begin{controlrule}[No bridge-ladder collapse]
The following ladder remains blocked:
\[
\text{obligation interface}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{finite-variation robustness}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{bridge relevance}
\not\Rightarrow
\RetainH \text{ or } \GenH .
\]
Each implication requires a separate source-side or bridge-side burden
and later audit under the appropriate gate.
\end{controlrule}

\section{Stress Verdicts}

\begin{verdict}[Obligation interface retained]
The audited packet survives as a useful source-side obligation
interface. It names the right local burdens and prevents a single
finite witness from being read as source-family robustness.
\end{verdict}

\begin{verdict}[Robustness and discriminants not discharged]
The packet does not survive as finite-variation robustness evidence or
readout-discriminant evidence. \(\RepLC\), \(\BndLC\), \(\CompLC\),
\(\InvLC\), \(\MinLC\), and \(\DiscLC\) remain in controlled
\(\Pause\) unless a future repair attempt fails, and \(\NegLC\)
remains in \(\Repair\).
\end{verdict}

\begin{verdict}[Promotion and bridge blocks preserved]
General \(\EqSrc\), \(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), downstream bridge claims,
candidate reconstruction, canonical ontology edit, benchmark
promotion, Gate Chair review, completed-derivation language, and
global theory rejection remain blocked.
\end{verdict}
