\section{Candidate definition}

Transport the ordinary topology and smooth structure of \(\mathbb R\) to
\(\Qamp\) through \(a\mapsto q_a\).  Write
\(e(\theta)=\sin\theta_1\).  For one proposal parameter \(\gamma>0\), define
\[
 X_\gamma:\Qamp\longrightarrow T\Qamp,\qquad
 X_\gamma(q_a)=-\gamma a^3e .
\]
The assignment is well typed because
\[
 X_\gamma(q_a)\in
 \operatorname{span}\{e\}=\Var(q_a)=T_{q_a}\Qamp .
\]
The autonomous source-order update rule is
\begin{equation}
 \frac{d^+q}{d\lambda}=X_\gamma(q),
 \qquad
 q(0)=q_{a_0},
 \qquad
 \lambda\in\mathbb R_{\geq0}.
 \label{eq:update}
\end{equation}
The superscript \(+\) records the one-sided derivative at the parameter
boundary.  The source base \(\mathbb T^4\) is compact and boundaryless, so the
only data selected here are \(a_0\), \(\gamma\), and the forward parameter
domain.  No target atlas, metric, volume form, connection, congruence, clock,
or benchmark datum enters.

\section{Equation and explicit nontrivial solution}

Writing \(q(\lambda)=q_{a(\lambda)}\), equation~\eqref{eq:update} becomes
\begin{equation}
 \frac{da}{d\lambda}=-\gamma a^3,
 \qquad a(0)=a_0 .
 \label{eq:amplitude}
\end{equation}
The right-hand side is smooth and locally Lipschitz.  Separation gives the
unique global forward solution
\begin{equation}
 a(\lambda)
 =
 \frac{a_0}{\sqrt{1+2\gamma a_0^2\lambda}},
 \label{eq:solution}
\end{equation}
with the \(a_0=0\) case included directly.  The denominator is positive for
every \(\lambda\geq0\), so the solution remains in \(\Qamp\).

For \(a_0\neq0\), the orbit is not constant.  For example, with
\(\gamma=1\), \(a_0=1\), and \(\lambda=1\),
\[
 a(1)=\frac1{\sqrt3}\neq1 .
\]
This is the positive control required by P5-T03; the fixed point \(a_0=0\) is
only an identity control.

\section{Compatibility with the P5-T02 semiflow}

\[

 \Phi^\gamma_\lambda(q_a)\right|_{\lambda=0}
 =-\gamma a^3e=X_\gamma(q_a).

\section{Genuinely forward-only behavior}

For \(\gamma,\lambda>0\),
\[
 \frac{\partial f^\gamma_\lambda}{\partial a}
 =
 (1+2\gamma\lambda a^2)^{-3/2}>0,
\]
so the map is injective.  Its exact range is
\[
 \operatorname{ran}f^\gamma_\lambda
 =
 \left(
 -\frac1{\sqrt{2\gamma\lambda}},
  \frac1{\sqrt{2\gamma\lambda}}
 \right),
\]
which is a proper subset of \(\mathbb R\).  Hence no positive-parameter map
has an inverse self-map on all of \(\Qamp\).  Equivalently, a nonzero backward
solution reaches a zero denominator at
\[
 \lambda_*=-\frac1{2\gamma a_0^2}.
\]
Thus this candidate has no global \(\mathbb R\)-action extension on
\(\Qamp\).  The forward maps remain injective, so this result is not
information loss.  It is also not physical irreversibility, entropy
production, causal orientation, a physical beginning, or a spacetime
singularity.
