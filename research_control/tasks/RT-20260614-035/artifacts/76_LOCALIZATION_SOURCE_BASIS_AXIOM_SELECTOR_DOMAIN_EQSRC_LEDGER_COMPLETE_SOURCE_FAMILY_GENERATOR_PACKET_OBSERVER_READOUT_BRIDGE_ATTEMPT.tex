\begin{abstract}
This draft/control Candidate Constructor artifact executes the
human-authorized bridge-or-fail successor to \texttt{handoff-0076}.
It attempts to construct a source-only bridge from \(\ObjLC\),
\(\CertLC\), and \(\ReadLC\) control data to an observer normal/readout
orbit \(([n]_{U},\PhiU)\). The attempt does not produce a candidate
map. The minimal missing primitive is a source-only
observer-localizing soldering rule \(\ObsLocLC\) that assigns a
source neighborhood \(U_X\), a normal orbit \([n]_{U_X}\), a readout
\(\Phi_{U_X}\), and certificate transport laws from ledger data alone.
Exact-interface \(\EqLC\) control status, \(\ReadAudit\), validation
receipts, and role authority are not bridge evidence. General
\(\EqSrc\), \(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), downstream bridge claims,
candidate reconstruction, canonical ontology edit, benchmark
promotion, Gate Chair review, completed-derivation language, and
global theory rejection remain blocked.
\end{abstract}

\section{Bridge Target}

The intended bridge target is a source-only assignment
\[
    \mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{obs}}:
    (X,\CertLC,\ReadLC)
    \longmapsto
    ([n]_{U_X},\Phi_{U_X})
\]
for \(X\in\ObjLC\). A stronger target would continue from
\(([n]_{U_X},\Phi_{U_X})\) to an effective metric candidate \(\geff\).
This job tests only the first target. It does not claim an effective
metric, matter coupling, Einstein equations, benchmark recovery, or a
Gate Chair-ready result.

\begin{criterion}[Source-only admissibility]
The bridge assignment is admissible only if all of the following are
defined from source data:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item a localization domain \(U_X\) chosen from source boundary
    anchors, ledger atoms, and certificate data;
    \item a normal representative orbit \([n]_{U_X}\) invariant under
    accepted \(\CertLC\) transport;
    \item a readout \(\Phi_{U_X}\) derived from canonical \(\ReadLC\),
    not from \(\ReadAudit\) or target observables;
    \item a transport rule for \(([n]_{U_X},\Phi_{U_X})\) along
    certificates \(c:X\to Y\);
    \item negative controls showing that forbidden target metric,
    target proper time, target locality, benchmark rank, and observer
    protocol do not select the assignment.
\end{enumerate}
\end{criterion}

\section{Available Source Data}

The ledger-complete source-family packet supplies finite objects
\[
    X=(B_X,i_X,o_X,A_X,E_X,C_X)
\]
with branch tokens, annotation aggregation, guards, blockers,
influence records, quotient-fiber data, contraction certificates, and
boundary anchors. The local certificate relation \(\CertLC\) preserves
the declared ledger coordinates and rejects target-metric or
observer-protocol selection. The finite-variation repair packet adds
exact representative and boundary baselines, a partial exact
composition ledger, inverse-provenance requirements, a diagnostic
\(\ReadAudit\), and schematic negative rows.

Those ingredients are useful controls, but they do not define how a
source object becomes an observer-local neighborhood or normal orbit.
They also do not prove that \(\ReadLC\) is minimal, discriminating, or
transported under nontrivial quotient and boundary variation.

\section{Attempted Construction}

\begin{attempt}[Naive exact-interface assignment]
One can try to set \(U_X=(i_X,o_X,B_X)\), let \([n]_{U_X}\) be the
orbit of the exact representative and boundary tuple, and define
\(\Phi_{U_X}\) from the available source readout coordinates.
\end{attempt}

\begin{finding}[Why the naive assignment fails]
The assignment is not a bridge map. It repackages exact source-control
coordinates without a source-side law saying why those coordinates are
observer-localizing normals or why their readout is the observer
readout. It also inherits the known burdens:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item exact representative equality does not define nontrivial
    quotient invariance;
    \item exact boundary equality does not define boundary transport;
    \item partial exact composition does not define transport for
    nonmatching intermediate representatives;
    \item inverse-provenance tokens remain requirements rather than
    concrete witnesses;
    \item \(\ReadAudit\) is diagnostic and cannot replace canonical
    \(\ReadLC\); and
    \item schematic negative rows are not finite witness coverage.
\end{enumerate}
Thus the attempted map collapses to bookkeeping unless a new
source-only observer-localizing soldering rule is supplied.
\end{finding}

\section{Minimal Missing Primitive}

\begin{finding}[Missing primitive]
The minimal missing primitive is a source-only observer-localizing
soldering rule
\[
    \ObsLocLC(X,c)
    =
    \bigl(U_X,[n]_{U_X},\Phi_{U_X},
    T_c^{n},T_c^{\Phi}\bigr),
\]
where \(T_c^{n}\) and \(T_c^{\Phi}\) transport the normal orbit and
readout along certificates \(c:X\to Y\). The rule must be defined from
ledger atoms, boundary anchors, quotient data, influence coverage,
contraction certificates, and canonical \(\ReadLC\) only.
\end{finding}

\begin{criterion}[What \(\ObsLocLC\) must prove before bridge use]
A future construction can use \(\ObsLocLC\) only if it supplies:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item finite concrete \(\ObjLC\) witnesses for at least one
    nontrivial source-local neighborhood;
    \item a certificate-transport law for \([n]_{U}\) and \(\Phi_U\);
    \item an invariance or controlled-variation theorem for
    representative and boundary changes;
    \item explicit inverse-provenance tokens or a source-side
    irrelevance proof;
    \item coordinate-removal tests separating \(\ReadLC\) from
    \(\ReadAudit\); and
    \item negative controls against target metric, target proper time,
    target locality, observer protocol, benchmark rank, validation
    status, and authority-label import.
\end{enumerate}
\end{criterion}
