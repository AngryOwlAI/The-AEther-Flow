baseline-authority snapshot packet.

## Basis

`research_control/program_state.yaml` names `RT-20260629-029` and
`handoff-0324`. The latest handoff explicitly routes the next packet to P0-T02:
create the post-v11 baseline authority snapshot before P1 work. The v12 plan's

for the post-v11 closure and the current post-P0-T01 state. It does not change
the Distance-to-GR ledger, science artifacts, canonical ontology, benchmark
status, or Gate Chair authority.
