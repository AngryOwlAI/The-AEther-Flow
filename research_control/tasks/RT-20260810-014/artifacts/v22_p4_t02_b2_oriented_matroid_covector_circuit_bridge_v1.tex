\section{Exact domain and objects}

Let $F$ be an ordered exact field, or an exact representation with decidable
sign, matrix rank, homogeneous linear feasibility, and root isolation for the
admitted path class.  The executable fixtures use $F=\Q$.  Let $S$ be a finite
nonempty sector set and
\[
  A:F^S\longrightarrow V,\qquad A e_s=v_s,
\]
where each $v_s\ne0$ is a predeclared oriented ray representative with explicit
proposal-only provenance.  The declared rank is $1\le r=\operatorname{rank}A
\le4$.  No claim is made that $S$ exhausts the P7 matter sectors.

For $k\in V^*$ define
\[
 \sigma_A(k)=\operatorname{sgn}(A^*k)\in\{-,0,+\}^{S},\qquad
 \Cstar(A)=\{\sigma_A(k):k\in V^*\},
\]
and the strict chamber
\[
 \GammaA=\{k\in V^*:k(v_s)>0\text{ for every }s\in S\}.
\]
A positive circuit is an inclusion-minimal support $C\subseteq S$ admitting
$\alpha_C\in F^C_{>0}$ with $A_C\alpha_C=0$; the complete set is
$\Cirp(A)$.  The total certificate is tagged either
$\operatorname{Feasible}(k)$ with $k\in\GammaA$ or
$\operatorname{Obstructed}(C,\alpha_C)$ with
$C\in\Cirp(A)$.

\section{Complete construction}

\subsection{Covectors and circuits}

Because the sign cube is finite, complete covector construction reduces each
$\sigma\in\{-,0,+\}^S$ to exact homogeneous feasibility:
\[
 k(v_s)=0\ (\sigma_s=0),\qquad
 \sigma_s k(v_s)>0\ (\sigma_s\ne0).
\]
The algorithm retains exactly the feasible cells and an exact witness for each.
It does not select one preferred covector or stop at the all-positive cell.

Every support-minimal positive dependence has at most $r+1$ columns.  The
constructor therefore enumerates supports $C$ with $|C|\le r+1$, computes the
exact kernel of $A_C$, and retains precisely those for which
\[
 \operatorname{rank}(A_C)=|C|-1,\qquad
 \dim\ker A_C=1,
\]
and the kernel generator has one strict positive sign after a global
rescaling.  Proper supports are tested, so the returned inventory is complete
and support-minimal.

\subsection{Strict total alternative}

Exactly one of the following holds:
\begin{align*}
 &\exists k\in V^*\quad k(v_s)>0\quad\forall s\in S,\\
 &\exists \alpha\in F^S_{\ge0}\setminus\{0\}\quad A\alpha=0.
\end{align*}
They cannot both hold because pairing $A\alpha=0$ with a strict feasible $k$
would give $0=\sum_s\alpha_s k(v_s)>0$.  If the first system is infeasible,
finite-dimensional separation supplies the second; minimizing its nonzero
support gives a positive circuit.  Thus $\operatorname{Tot}(A)$ is exhaustive
and exclusive.  Either branch is a valid construction result, so no
goal-selected success filtering occurs.

\section{Presentation typing and transport}

The natural presentation arrows are
\[
 A\longmapsto T A D P^{-1},
\]
where $T\in\operatorname{GL}(V)$, $D$ is positive diagonal, and $P$ relabels
sectors.  Covectors, ranks, circuit supports, coefficient rays, total branches,
and refinement structure transport naturally.  Negative column scaling is not
an arrow: it is a source reorientation and may change the total branch.

The raw feasible witness and raw obstruction coefficients transform as
\[
 k\longmapsto T^{-\mathsf T}k,
 \qquad
 \alpha\longmapsto P D^{-1}\alpha.
\]
Consequently a literal map from the presentation quotient to a raw $k$ or raw
$\alpha$ is ill-typed.  The corrected construction first assigns equivariant
data to the configuration groupoid.  Its quotient value is an isomorphism
class of $\Bridge$ data together with the appropriate certificate orbit.  This
is mathematical presentation naturality, not a claim of physical gauge
equivalence.

\section{Finite refinements}

For a declared inclusion $i:S\hookrightarrow T$ preserving every old oriented
ray up to positive scaling, restriction gives
\[
 \rho_i:\Cstar(A_T)\longrightarrow\Cstar(A_S).
\]
It is surjective: a dual witness for an old covector also evaluates all newly
adjoined columns and therefore supplies an extending covector.  The strict
chambers obey
\[
 \Gamma(A_T)\subseteq\Gamma(A_S),
\]
because adjoining inequalities can only shrink the feasible region.  A
positive circuit supported entirely on $S$ persists after adjoining sectors;
a smaller positive dependence inside its old support would already have
contradicted its old minimality.  Restrictions compose for nested inclusions.
These finite statements do not establish a continuum limit or all-sector
robustness.

\section{Finite-complexity path stratification}

crossed infinitely often.  The finite wall claim is therefore restricted to a
predeclared finite semialgebraic or piecewise-rational exact path family for
which all zero-vector, rank, and relevant determinant-minor roots can be
isolated.  On this domain the isolated roots form an ordered finite event
record; the oriented-matroid data are constant on each wall-free stratum.
