# P4-T01 source-assumption unresolved ambiguities

## Result boundary

The comprehensive ledger makes nine unresolved assumption clusters explicit.
It does not settle them. “Current ontology does not derive X” below means only
that the inspected canonical sources do not supply the required derivation; it
does not mean X is impossible. Conservative source extension and later
same-milestone continuation remain open. Canonical-ontology candidacy,
adoption, rejection, and ontology modification remain human-gated.

## High-impact fork

The current source language is hybrid. It explicitly posits a
four-dimensional differentiable substrate and ordered motion, while other
passages describe metric and spacetime structure as eventual derivational
targets. Two coherent proposal branches therefore remain:

1. `P4-T02`, continuum-first: declare dimension, topology, and
   differentiability primitive and owe derivations only for physical metric,
   clocks, matter, coupling, and gravitational dynamics.
2. `P4-T03`, emergence-first: start from genuinely weaker order, process,
   category, algebra, or discrete dynamics and owe reconstruction of dimension,
   topology, differentiability, and continuum structure in addition to the
   later physical burdens.

P4-T01 does not choose between these branches. The protected comparison and
regime decision remains downstream.
