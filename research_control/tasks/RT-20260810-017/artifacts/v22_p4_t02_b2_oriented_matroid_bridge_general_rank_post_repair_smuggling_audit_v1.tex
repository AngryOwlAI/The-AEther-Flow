\section{New exact mathematical payloads}

\subsection{Effective-span target-dimension nonselection}

Let $A:F^S\to V$ be a finite configuration and set
$W_A=\operatorname{span}\{A_s:s\in S\}$.  Let
$i:W_A\hookrightarrow W_A\oplus U$ be the canonical injection and define
$A'=i\circ A$.  Then restriction
$(W_A\oplus U)^*\to W_A^*$ is surjective.  Consequently every sign covector
of $A$ is realized by $A'$, and every sign covector of $A'$ is the restriction
of one on $W_A$.  Hence
\[
 \Cstar(A')=\Cstar(A).
\]
Because $\ker A'=\ker A$, positive circuits and Gordan's two total branches
also agree.  The supplied refinement arrows and tame paths transport through
the same injection, so their bridge records agree up to the already fixed
presentation transport.  Thus
\[
 \Bridge(A')\cong\Bridge(A).
\]
Unused ambient dimensions add no bridge information.  The quantity
$r=\dim W_A$ is intrinsic algebraic rank, not a selected ambient dimension,
spacetime dimension, or physical sector census.

\subsection{Finite-fixture cap nonidentifiability}

Let $T\subset\mathbb{N}_{>0}$ be the finite set of ranks exercised by a
conformance suite and let $m=\max T$.  For every $N\ge m$, define
$D_N=\{r:1\le r\le N\}$, and let $D_\infty$ denote all finite positive
ranks.  Then
\[
 T\cap D_N=T=T\cap D_\infty
 \qquad\text{for every }N\ge m.
\]
Therefore no result restricted to $T$ can distinguish the arbitrary-finite
domain from any cap at or above $m$.  In particular, the exact ranks-one-
through-eight model agrees with caps $8,9,\ldots,16$, as well as with the
rank-generic theorem, on every tested admission.  The rank-five and rank-six
controls decisively refute the necessity of the former cap four, but they do
not select a replacement cap or a physical dimension.

\subsection{Sharp controls and their limit}

The support-minimality argument itself uses only finite ordered-field
algebra.  If a positive dependence had kernel dimension greater than one,
an independent kernel vector could be scaled by the minimum of finitely many
positive coordinate ratios until at least one coefficient vanished.  The
remaining nonzero nonnegative dependence would have proper support, a
contradiction.  No Archimedean, completeness, target-dimensional, or
metric premise enters this proof.  At full column rank the positive-circuit
set is empty, so the same bound is correctly vacuous.

For
\[
 A_r^+=\bigl[e_1\ \cdots\ e_r\ \ \mathbf{1}_r\bigr],\qquad
 A_r^-=\bigl[e_1\ \cdots\ e_r\ \ -\mathbf{1}_r\bigr],
\]
the positive and negative choices exercise the strict feasible and obstructed
branches.  The negative configuration has a support-minimal positive circuit
of size $r+1$, proving sharpness.  At ranks five and six the mandatory four
controls contain 5072 covectors and 376 topes in aggregate, with support-six
and support-seven circuits.  These calculations establish theorem
nonvacuity and conformance across the former cap.  They do not establish
source occurrence, representative coverage, continuum robustness, a physical
dimension, or P7 universality.

The legacy fixture suffixes in labels such as $A_{F4}$ and $A_{O6}$ count
columns in those controls; they do not assert intrinsic rank four or six.
Likewise, the quarantine label has a negative normative function: it blocks
an evidentiary overread.  The label supplies no theorem proof, source
occurrence, ontology derivation, physical interpretation, empirical support,
universality, or adoption authority of its own.
