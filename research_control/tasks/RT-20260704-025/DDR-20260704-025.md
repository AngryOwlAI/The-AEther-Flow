# DDR-20260704-025

## Decision

Select one bounded `ontology-formalizer@0.2.0` task overlay for v16 P4-T01:
`source_certificate_instance_library_schema_v16`.

## Evidence

- `handoff-0570` selects P4 concrete certificate-instance library as the next
  route and restricts the next packet to P4-T01 schema.
- The v16 plan requires the artifact
  `research_control/design/source_certificate_instance_library_schema_v1.md`.
- Existing certificate primitives, operation laws, and checklist controls
  already define missing, malformed, target-importing, detector-semantics,
  benchmark-behavior, and process-authority fail-closed discipline.

## Rationale

P4 cannot safely construct examples until the instance record grammar is fixed.
The Ontology Formalizer role is the narrowest role that can define source-side
objects, domains, codomains, guards, payload fields, and no-target-import
boundaries without creating candidate laws or promoting physics claims.
