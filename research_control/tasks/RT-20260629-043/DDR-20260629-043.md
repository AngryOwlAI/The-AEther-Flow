# Director Decision Record: DDR-20260629-043

## Decision

Execute P4-T01 as one bounded `ontology-formalizer@0.2.0` task overlay.
The task extracts a formal dependency map for the source-extension stack used
by current `Resp_lc`, `M_src`, scoped `g_eff`, and matter-coupling
precondition evidence.

## Basis

`handoff-0337` selected P4-T01 as the first post-v12 scientific objective.
The frontier theorem inventory, Distance-to-GR ledger, and the relevant Gate
Chair artifacts show accepted scoped objects and scoped evidence packages, but
their authority is narrow. The logical next step is to inventory dependencies
before compression, matter semantics, proof-support, Einstein-equation, or
benchmark routes attempt to reuse the stack.

## Milestone And Burden

Target derivation milestone: `matter_coupling`.

Milestone burden: extract and classify the source-extension dependency stack
used by `Resp_lc`, `M_src`, scoped `g_eff`, and matter-coupling precondition
evidence before compression or matter-semantics route selection.

## Scope

Allowed:

- read the P2 frontier theorem inventory, Distance-to-GR ledger, burden map,
  final Gate Chair artifacts, and directly cited precursor artifacts;
- classify each current dependency using the P4-T01 categories;
- produce `source_extension_dependency_extraction_v1.md`;
- produce a machine-readable dependency table;
- update normal research-control receipts, registries, handoff, and generated
  memory/dependency derivatives;
- preserve parent-child synthesis receipts.

Blocked:

- compression success or failure decision;
- canonical ontology edit;
- source-law adoption;
- `MetricData(E)` adoption;
- `g_eff` scope change or unscoped `g_eff` adoption;
- coupling-law adoption;
- matter-coupling derivation or adoption;
- stress-energy semantics, stress-energy tensor, matter action, or detector
  semantics;
- Einstein-equation derivation;
- benchmark promotion or benchmark Gate Chair closure;
- completed-derivation claim;
- future source-extension impossibility or global theory rejection.
