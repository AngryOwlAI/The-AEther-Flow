# DDR-20260627-002: APA 7 Ontology Title And References Source-Format Retrofit

## Current Objective

Implement the user's explicit source-format authorization for the 8 current
managed ontology documents under `ontology/tex` and their managed PDF
derivatives under `ontology/pdfs`.

This supersedes the no-op stop recorded in `DDR-20260627-001`. The new
authorization is bounded to formatting and provenance presentation only.

## Role-Fit Matrix

| Candidate role | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ontology-source-format-retrofit` | Accepted | A one-job provisional role is the least broad mechanism for the current-only source-format authority. It expires after `AJ-RT-20260627-002-001`. |
| Project-Control Maintainer | Rejected | It can maintain control records and registries but the previous packet already established that it cannot edit canonical ontology TeX or PDF derivatives directly. |
| Documentation Curator | Rejected | The packet is not a source-backed explanatory Markdown or HTML publication change. |
| Physics roles | Rejected | The work is formatting and attribution presentation only; no scientific derivation or claim-gate decision is being made. |

## Decision

Create and execute one human-gated provisional AgentJob:
`AJ-RT-20260627-002-001`.

The selected provisional role may:

- add shared TeX fragments under `tex_shared/` for title-page and references
  presentation;
- mechanically wire those fragments into the 8 current `ontology/tex/*.tex`
  sources;
- rebuild only the corresponding current `ontology/pdfs/*.pdf` derivatives
  through the managed PDF derivative script;
- refresh generated registries and wiki metadata through the normal bootstrap
  path.

The selected provisional role may not:

- edit article body prose beyond the mechanical title/reference plumbing;
- edit `legacy_ontology/**`;
- edit historical `research_control/tasks/**` TeX or PDF artifacts;
- change physics claims, ontology status, benchmark status, Gate Chair status,
  or derivational conclusions;
- register a permanent role or widen future source-format authority.
