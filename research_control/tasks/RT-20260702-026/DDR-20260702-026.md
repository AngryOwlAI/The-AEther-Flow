## Reasoning

Handoff `handoff-0478` requires a P9-T03 pilot review after the P9-T02 review
template update. The selected role is the narrowest fit because it may create
review outputs, may critique mathematical and interpretive boundaries, and may
recommend selector, repair, obstruction, freeze, or literature comparison while
remaining unable to promote claims or modify sources.

objects. Its target derivation milestone is `matter_coupling`, with burden
focused on response semantics and coupling derivation pressure. The output is
not a derivation step: it is an adversarial review matrix and routing
recommendation.

- review the seven P9-T03 required core frontier objects;

Forbidden:

- source-law adoption;
- `MetricData(E)` adoption;

  stress-energy, matter-action, Einstein-equation, benchmark, or completed
  derivation claims.
