\section{Finite/Local Stress Lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Finite/local stress survival]

and the repair-or-bottom finite-variation rule from \texttt{RT-20260614-159},
the audited \(\Witness(\Ezero)\) candidate survives the required Refuter
stress dimensions only as finite/local draft/control candidate data.

\begin{proof}
Collapse stress is handled by the response-profile condition. For the three
sites in \(\Ezero\), the profiles are distinct under the admitted chain. If a
variation collapses these profiles, the candidate does not repair by target
rank or Lorentzian signature; it returns the source-degeneracy branch.

Nonuniqueness stress is handled by the deterministic finite source fields:
the oriented chain, finite counting-measure class, reachability preorder,
transition-token action, response-profile distinguishability, and
repair-or-bottom variation rule. Inside the stated gauge boundary there is no
independent valuation choice. If a broader gauge is demanded without a
source-side equivalence proof, the packet cannot use target geometry, file
order, role identity, approval status, or validation status to choose a
record; it must fail closed.

Covariance stress is handled by limiting \(K_{\tau}\) to source transition
tokens preserving directed incidence and the marked chain class. Broader
unmarked reversals or target coordinate transitions are not admitted repairs.
They either leave the candidate boundary or trigger the noncovariance branch.

Finite-variation stress is handled by the explicit three-way behavior:
source relabeling preserves the record, equivalent single-edge replacement
repairs inside the source package, and chain removal, distinguishability
collapse, target contamination, or source-domain exit returns a named bottom
branch.

Thus every stress dimension either preserves finite/local candidate status or
fails closed. No branch yields adopted \(\MetricData(E)\), a general witness
theorem, \(\geff\), matter coupling, Einstein equations, benchmark promotion,
completed derivation, future source-extension impossibility, or global theory
rejection.
\end{proof}
