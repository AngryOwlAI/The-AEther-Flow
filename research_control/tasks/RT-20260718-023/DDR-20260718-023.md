## Basis

v3 is abstractly instantiable but leaves its primitive cross-fiber decisions
and finite-variation class unselected. The handoff explicitly routes one
Candidate Constructor packet and bars another generic signature repair or

does not derive their physical selection. That limitation remains visible and
prevents adoption-level overread.

## Claim Boundary

\(\mathbb Z_4\), one proper nontrivial subgroup, quotient-equality selection,
the corresponding exact-v3 primitive decisions, one nontrivial class of
objectwise subgroup-valued profile variations, and bounded proofs of
nondegeneracy, closure, stability, naturality, and sharpness.

profile or subgroup follows from current ontology; general `EqSrc` discharge;

process authority as proof; Distance-to-GR ledger change; matter coupling;

derivation; future source-extension impossibility; or any conclusion beyond this bounded packet.
