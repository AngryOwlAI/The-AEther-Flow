\section{Question Decided}

The Ontology Formalizer packet \texttt{RT-20260614-110} defined
proposal-only \(\GenCover\) as a fail-closed general source-cover law
interface. The Smuggling Auditor packet \texttt{RT-20260614-111} found a
conditional source-purity pass as written. The Refuter packet
\texttt{RT-20260614-112} found that audited \(\GenCover\) survives the named
stress cases as draft/control source-extension data because failed extension,
nonuniqueness, inverse-token, cocycle-token, soldering, bottom-leak,
finite-replay-overread, and process-authority branches return \(\Bot\) or do
not reach non-bottom output.

The protected question is therefore narrow:
\[
\text{May }\GenCover\text{ be adopted as admissible source-extension-law data}
\text{ for later }\Msrc\text{ continuation?}
\]

\section{Decision}

\begin{decision}[GenCover source-extension-law adoption]
Under tracked human authorization, \(\GenCover\) is adopted as an admissible
source-extension law for subsequent bounded \(\Msrc\) continuation.
\end{decision}

The adopted status is scoped to the law interface as written in
\texttt{RT-20260614-110}, audited in \texttt{RT-20260614-111}, and
stress-tested in \texttt{RT-20260614-112}. For a supplied source-side
extension package, \(\GenCover\) may return candidate source-cover and
quotient-support data or \(\Bot\). This decision does not prove that current
ontology derives \(\GenCover\), does not prove that all source covers are
non-bottom, and does not adopt \(\FVR\), \(\RegSold\), or \(\Msrc\).

\section{Adoption Conditions}

\begin{condition}[Source-only inputs]
Inputs to \(\GenCover\) must remain source carriers, source cover families,
source overlap predicates, source-token profiles, source transition tokens,
source inverse and cocycle certificates, source-side soldering or regularity
certificates, bottom conditions, and conditional source quotient-support data.
\end{condition}

\begin{condition}[Fail-closed branches]
Unsupported extension, selector nonuniqueness, inverse-token degeneration,
cocycle-token degeneration, soldering nonuniqueness, bottom leaks, and
finite-replay overread must continue to return \(\Bot\) or remain unproved.
They may not be repaired by target topology, target atlas data, target metric
data, empirical detector semantics, benchmark success, validation receipts,
role identity, registry metadata, generated derivatives, handoff status, or
file order.
\end{condition}

\begin{condition}[No downstream shortcut]
Adoption of \(\GenCover\) does not identify \(\Qsrc\) with adopted \(\Msrc\).
It does not adopt \(\FVR\), \(\RegSold\), or arbitrary finite-variation
robustness. It does not define \(\geff\), matter coupling, Einstein equations,
or benchmark status. Those remain separate derivational burdens.
\end{condition}
