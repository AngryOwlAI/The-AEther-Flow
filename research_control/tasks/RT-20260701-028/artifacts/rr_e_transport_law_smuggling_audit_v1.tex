a source-side \(\RRE\) variation pair \(\VarRR(r,r')\), identification of
records for \(F\) may occur only through one of four branches:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{transport branch} & \TrRR(r,r')\ \text{with source soundness for }F,\\
\text{invariance branch} & \InvRR(F;r,r'),\\

\section{Guards}

\begin{guard}[Transport branch]
The transport branch is source-pure only when an actual source transport
certificate \(\TrRR(r,r')\) and source soundness for \(F\) are supplied. It may
not be read as an assumed complete transport law.
\end{guard}

\begin{guard}[Invariance branch]
The invariance branch is source-pure only for the declared source object \(F\).
It may not be read as detector indifference, empirical equivalence, benchmark
success, or arbitrary object-independence.
\end{guard}

\begin{guard}[Factorization branch]
The factorization branch is the ordinary conditional factor-through argument:
if \(F=\bar F\circ\pi_{\RRE}\), then equality of quotient labels implies
equality of \(F\)-values. It remains conditional on a source quotient and a
source factorization certificate; it is not unrestricted \(\RRE\) irrelevance.
\end{guard}

\begin{guard}[Certificate deletion]
If the only identification route depends on \(\TrRR\), \(\InvRR\), or
\(\FactRR\), deleting that source certificate deletes the identification route.
The fail-closed result is separation or obstruction, not detector semantics,
\(\MetricData(E)\), \(\geff\), benchmark behavior, or process authority.
\end{guard}
