\begin{lemma}[Source-relabeling stress survival]
For every incidence-preserving source relabeling
\(\rho=(\rho_T,\rho_A):\mathcal{S}_E\to\mathcal{S}_{E'}\) whose no-target
certificates pass, the candidate satisfies
\[
  D_{E'}(\rho_T(t))=\rho_L(D_E(t))
\]
for every \(t\in T_E\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Incidence preservation gives
\[
  (t,a)\in R_E \Longleftrightarrow
  (\rho_T(t),\rho_A(a))\in R_{E'}.
\]
Therefore
\[
  \sigma_{E'}(\rho_T(t))=\rho_A[\sigma_E(t)].
\]
The definition of \(D_E\), \(D_{E'}\), and
\(\rho_L(\ell_\Sigma)=\ell_{\rho_A[\Sigma]}\) gives the equality. The proof
uses only source-token, source-interface, and source-incidence data.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Incidence-deletion fail-closed stress]
If a finite variation deletes or corrupts the source incidence needed to
compute \(\sigma_E(t)\), then the candidate maps the affected token to
\(\Bottom\) or to a named obstruction label. This failure mode is fail-closed
and does not supply positive matter semantics.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The non-bottom labels are generated only from occurring source-incidence
signatures. When the incidence data needed for a token is removed, the
candidate no longer has the source evidence required to assign
\(\ell_{\sigma_E(t)}\). The candidate's own guard therefore routes the token
to \(\Bottom\) or to an obstruction label such as
\texttt{MS-SIG-SOURCE-INCIDENCE-MISSING}. A failed source signature is not a
physical detector outcome, not stress energy, not a matter action, and not a
coupling law.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Explicit-tag collapse is blocked]
An attempted implementation that preserves \(\ell_\Sigma\)-style names while
removing the source-incidence relation \(R_E\) fails the candidate's support
condition and routes to \texttt{MS-SIG-EXPLICIT-TAG-COLLAPSE}.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
In the audited candidate, labels are not primitive matter-sector names. They
are generated from subsets \(\Sigma=\sigma_E(t)\subseteq A_E\) that occur by
\((t,a)\in R_E\). If the apparent label persists after the source-incidence
basis is erased, then the label is an explicit tag rather than a source
signature. The candidate's stated finite-variation guard marks that branch as
collapse rather than adoption-ready semantics.
\end{proof}
