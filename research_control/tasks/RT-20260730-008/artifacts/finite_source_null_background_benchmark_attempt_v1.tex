\begin{theorem}[Finite source null-background theorem]
\label{thm:null}
Let \(L_C\) be the symmetric conductance Laplacian of a finite source carrier,
let \(\alpha>0\), and let \(u_0\in\ker L_C\).  Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(a_C(u_0)=0\);
\item every stationary solution of \eqref{eq:action} satisfies
      \([h_0]=0\) in \(\Qspace\);
\item \([h_0]=0\) is the unique quotient minimum;
\item for a source perturbation \(\delta u\), the exact static quotient
      linearization is
      \[
        \delta[h]=\frac{\beta}{\alpha}\delta[u].

\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}

equation becomes \(\alpha L_Ch_0=0\).  Since \(\alpha\ne0\),

by \(\ker L_C\); therefore the quotient stationary point is the unique
minimum.  Linearizing the exact equation gives
\(\alpha L_C\delta h=\beta L_C\delta u\).  Applying the quotient inverse of
\(L_C\) yields
\(\delta[h]=(\beta/\alpha)\delta[u]\).
\end{proof}
