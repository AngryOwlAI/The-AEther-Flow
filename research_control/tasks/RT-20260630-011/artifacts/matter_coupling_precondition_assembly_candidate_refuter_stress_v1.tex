\section{Formal Stress Lemmas}

\begin{lemma}[Certificate deletion is fail-closed]
If the compatibility-certificate family \(\CompCert_E\) is deleted or made
unavailable, \(\MCPACand(E)\) cannot return a positive nonempty pre-assembly
record. It returns an obstruction, an empty family, or \(\BottomSrc\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Every positive record in the written candidate has the form
\[
  a=(w,s,c,U_a,\mathsf{pre\text{-}coupling\text{-}only})
\]
with \(c=(w,s,U_c,\mathsf{match},n_c)\in\CompCert_E\). If
\(\CompCert_E\) is absent, no such \(c\) exists. The candidate definition's
missing-domain and mismatch branches therefore activate. No target or process
premise can replace \(c\), because RT-010 audits those replacements as
forbidden. Hence deletion fails closed.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Witness-signature mismatch is fail-closed]
If a proposed certificate does not pair one declared witness-slot record with
one declared matter-signature record, then it cannot witness a positive
pre-assembly record.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The candidate domain requires \(w\in\WitSlot_E\), \(s\in\SigRec_E\), and
\(c=(w,s,U_c,\mathsf{match},n_c)\in\CompCert_E\). A mismatched certificate
violates this domain condition. The written candidate names this as the
\(\mathrm{OB\text{-}MCPA\text{-}WITNESS\text{-}SIGNATURE\text{-}MISMATCH}\)
branch rather than as a positive assembly branch.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Nonunique matching is noncanonical]
If several source-local certificates match the same witness slot and
signature record, then the candidate may enumerate several pre-assembly
records, but this multiplicity does not select a canonical source law or
physical coupling law.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The written candidate enumerates records certified by source-local
compatibility certificates. It does not define an ordering, quotient,
selection functional, or uniqueness theorem over \(\CompCert_E\). Therefore
nonunique matching is at most a noncanonical multiplicity in the
pre-coupling assembly ledger. Selector review is required before any stronger
status could be requested.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
