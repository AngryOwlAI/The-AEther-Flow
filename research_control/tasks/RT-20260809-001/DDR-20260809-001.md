## Selected Role

control sources, validator, fixtures, task records, registries, and approved

## Claim Boundary

The charter and validators are operational governance only. Track A or C
success cannot reduce a Distance-to-GR burden. Track B cannot use interpretive
coherence, workflow conformance, validator PASS, or Track C results as Gate
evidence. Typed cross-track links carry context or dependency information with
zero authority effect. The current repository decision remains `retain` unless
measured coupling, cadence, access, maintenance-cost, provenance, and human
authorization criteria support a later separately authorized split.

## Validation

Validate exact 40-package assignment, schema separation, prohibited promotion
fixtures, dashboard separation, budget non-double-counting, publication-lane
separation, deterministic process-integrity review, focused unit tests,
documentation impact, research-control state, generated freshness, memory
freshness, and the final staged checkpoint. A passing control validator is not
physics proof or independent external review.
