\begin{abstract}
This draft/control Refuter artifact stress-tests the audited
\texttt{RT-20260614-023} pre-bridge adequacy packet and the
\texttt{RT-20260614-024} Smuggling Auditor local conditional pass. The
adequacy packet survives only as a source-pure obligation ledger: it
states what finite examples, failed certificates, source-side readouts,
invariance checks, kill criteria, and repair criteria must later
supply. It does not yet supply those examples, does not define a
concrete \(\ReadSrc\), does not prove readout invariance, and does not
establish bridge relevance. Scientific adequacy therefore remains in
\(\Pause\). General \(\EqSrc\), \(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), downstream
bridge claims, candidate reconstruction, canonical ontology edit,
benchmark promotion, Gate Chair review, completed-derivation language,
and global theory rejection remain blocked.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and Stress Question}

This stress test responds to \texttt{handoff-0066}. It reads the
pre-bridge adequacy packet as an obligation object and the Smuggling
Auditor result as a local conditional pass for source purity, not as a
claim that the adequacy obligations have been discharged.

The stress question is:
\[
    \text{Does the audited }\AdeqLC\text{ packet survive pressure as
    completed scientific adequacy evidence?}
\]

The answer is no. It survives as a well-scoped control object, but
returns \(\Pause\) for scientific adequacy because the finite examples,
the failed-certificate witness, the concrete source-pure readout, and
the readout-invariance theorem remain absent.

\section{Stress Method}

\begin{stressscreen}[Obligation versus evidence]
The packet is stressed under a strict distinction:
\[
    \text{obligation supplied} \not\Rightarrow
    \text{obligation discharged}.
\]
If an adequacy criterion is merely named, the stress result may record
it as a useful control burden. It may not count it as evidence for
\(\EqSrc\), \(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), bridge readiness, candidate
readiness, benchmark recovery, or Gate Chair review.
\end{stressscreen}

\begin{stressscreen}[Finite source-side pressure]
Finite examples are accepted only if their objects, certificates,
quotient representatives, boundary anchors, and readouts are selected
from source-side ledger criteria. Any selection by target success,
observer protocol, proper time, benchmark rank, candidate behavior,
validation status, registry status, generated visibility, or handoff
status returns \(\Block\).
\end{stressscreen}

\begin{stressscreen}[Readout pressure]
A readout burden is accepted only after its codomain, equality relation,
and computation are defined from source coordinates and after
\[
    X\EqLC Y \Longrightarrow \ReadSrc(X)=\ReadSrc(Y)
\]
is proved for the declared certificate relation. A readout that is only
described as desirable is not a bridge surface.
\end{stressscreen}

\section{Finite Example Stress}

\begin{counterexample}[Target-favorable positive example]
Suppose a positive pair \(X,Y\in\ObjLC\) is chosen because it preserves
a downstream clock-like readout, a benchmark-recovery score, or a
candidate behavior. Even if the ledger transport is later written in
source notation, the example was selected by target success. The
Refuter returns \(\Block\). The audited packet correctly forbids this
move, but it has not yet supplied a positive example that survives the
screen.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Near-miss negative example]
Let \(Y'\) differ from \(Y\) by deleting a blocker record, weakening a
proxy guard, or moving a boundary anchor before comparison. Any positive
certificate from \(X\) to \(Y'\) would be created by erasing adverse
source data. The expected verdict is \(\Block\). The adequacy packet
requires such a negative example, but the concrete finite ledger has not
yet been supplied.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Boundary example overread]
The tri-family embedding can serve as a boundary example only if it is
kept inside its declared finite source family. Treating the boundary
case as a witness for general \(\EqSrc\), or as evidence for
\(\RetainH\) or \(\GenH\), collapses a local receipt into a global
claim and returns \(\Block\).
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Failed-certificate example]
Consider a finite certificate attempt whose forward step preserves
aggregate annotations but loses which refined branch carries a blocker,
or whose two locally plausible steps compose only after changing
quotient fiber data. The inverse or composition check fails for
source-side reasons. This is the required shape of a failed-certificate
example. The adequacy packet names the obligation but has not supplied
the witness ledger.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{stressfinding}[Examples remain obligations]
The audited adequacy packet is not refuted as a control packet by the
absence of concrete examples; its purpose was to state the obligation.
However, absence of the examples blocks any move from \(\AdeqLC\) to
scientific adequacy, bridge readiness, or promotion review.
\end{stressfinding}

\section{Readout-Invariance Stress}

\begin{counterexample}[Undefined codomain]
If \(\ReadSrc\) is described as a source-side readout but its codomain
\(R\), equality relation on \(R\), and computation from ledger
coordinates are not defined, then the statement
\[
    X\EqLC Y \Longrightarrow \ReadSrc(X)=\ReadSrc(Y)
\]
is not a proposition with a checkable truth value. The bridge ladder
therefore stops at rung 0.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Quotient-sensitive readout]
Let \(\ReadSrc\) count blocker records after quotient refinement. If a
valid certificate can refine quotient fibers while preserving total
blocker provenance but changes the representative-level count, then
\(\ReadSrc\) is not invariant unless the packet adds a source-side
representative-independence rule. The failure is source-side and does
not require target import.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Boundary-sensitive readout]
Let \(\ReadSrc\) depend on boundary anchors \(i,o\). If a certificate
preserves anchors only up to an unstated boundary identification, then
the readout can change under an apparently admissible transport. A
source-pure boundary-invariance lemma is required before any pass.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Target-interpreted readout]
If a proposed readout computes clock behavior, causal locality, matter
coupling, benchmark recovery, or candidate performance directly, then it
is not a source-side readout. It may become a later bridge target only
after a separate audited mapping from source readout to physical
interpretation. Used as input at this stage, it returns \(\Kill\).
\end{counterexample}

\begin{stressfinding}[Readout invariance remains unproved]
The packet defines a valid readout ladder but does not yet instantiate
the ladder. No concrete \(\ReadSrc\) and no invariance theorem are
available. Therefore the readout track remains in \(\Pause\).
\end{stressfinding}

\section{Bridge-Ladder and Authority Stress}

\begin{controlrule}[No rung collapse]
The following implications are not established:
\[
\begin{aligned}
    \text{source-side well-formedness} &\Longrightarrow
    \text{source-side invariance},\\
    \text{source-side invariance} &\Longrightarrow
    \text{bridge relevance},\\
    \text{bridge relevance} &\Longrightarrow
    \RetainH\text{ or }\GenH .
\end{aligned}
\]
Any packet that collapses these rungs returns \(\Block\).
\end{controlrule}

\begin{stressfinding}[Authority labels remain inert]
The Smuggling Auditor pass, registry rows, generated wiki note,
validator success, handoff status, and task closure are process
receipts. They do not supply finite examples, readout invariance,
bridge relevance, canonical ontology authority, benchmark status, or
Gate Chair verdicts.
\end{stressfinding}

\begin{stressfinding}[Kill criteria do not reject the program]
The adequacy packet's kill criteria close this branch only when target
primitives, authority labels, deleted ledger data, failed invariance, or
downstream selection are required for a positive result. A branch
\(\Kill\) does not imply global theory rejection.
\end{stressfinding}

\begin{stressfinding}[Repair criteria are not promotion evidence]
Repair criteria identify possible source-side repairs: a concrete
example ledger, certificate algebra, quotient rule, inverse checker,
composition checker, influence-preservation lemma, boundary-invariance
lemma, or source-pure readout definition. A repair proposal remains
draft/control until audited and stress-tested.
\end{stressfinding}

\section{Stress Verdicts}

\begin{verdict}[Control-object retention]
The audited \(\AdeqLC\) packet survives Refuter pressure as a
source-pure draft/control obligation ledger. It is useful because it
states finite example, failed-certificate, readout, invariance, kill,
and repair burdens without importing target primitives.
\end{verdict}

\begin{verdict}[Scientific adequacy pause]
The packet does not survive as completed scientific adequacy evidence.
Concrete finite examples, a concrete source-pure \(\ReadSrc\), a
readout-invariance theorem, and bridge relevance remain missing.
Scientific adequacy remains in controlled pause.
\end{verdict}

\begin{verdict}[Promotion and bridge blocks preserved]
General \(\EqSrc\), \(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), downstream bridge claims,
candidate reconstruction, canonical ontology edit, benchmark promotion,
Gate Chair review, completed-derivation language, and global theory
rejection remain blocked.
\end{verdict}
