\section{Full-frame endpoint nonselection}

\begin{theorem}[Fixed idempotents under the full frame group]
Let $W$ be a real vector space of dimension six.  If $P\in\operatorname{End}(W)$
satisfies $gPg^{-1}=P$ for every $g\in\mathrm{GL}(W)$, then $P=\lambda I$.
If also $P^2=P$, then $P\in\{0,I\}$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Commuting with every diagonal scaling eliminates all off-diagonal entries.
Commuting with permutation matrices makes every diagonal entry equal, so
$P=\lambda I$.  Idempotency gives $\lambda^2=\lambda$, hence
$\lambda\in\{0,1\}$.  Both endpoints commute with every frame change.
\end{proof}

The theorem does not make zero unique.  It proves the opposite: the full
source/equation-frame naturality available to the candidate admits two
endpoint sections.  Choosing zero rather than identity remains proposal data.

\begin{theorem}[Complement symmetry]
The map $C(P)=I-P$ is a conjugation-natural involution of $\Idem(W)$.  It
exchanges $0$ and $I$, sends rank $r$ to rank $6-r$, and exchanges image and
kernel.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For an idempotent $P$, $(I-P)^2=I-P$.  Also
$C(gPg^{-1})=g(I-P)g^{-1}$ and $C(C(P))=P$.  The splitting
$W=\im P\oplus\ker P$ gives the rank and image/kernel statements.
\end{proof}

Thus no complement-invariant algebraic criterion can distinguish the null
endpoint from the total endpoint.  A falsifier would be an independently
current-source-defined law on the full admitted arrow class that excludes
$I$ while admitting $0$; no such law is present.

\section{The inert-or-bridge-force dichotomy}

Let $U:X\to D$ forget the decoration $P$ and retain the old source datum $d$.
Call a downstream construction $B:X\to Y$ \emph{old-source factored} when
$B=b\circ U$ for some $b:D\to Y$.

\begin{theorem}[Fibre factorization]
There exists a map $b:\im(U)\to Y$ satisfying $B=b\circ U$ if and only if $B$
is constant on every fibre of $U$.  If $U$ is surjective, $b$ on $D$ is
unique.  Without surjectivity, values outside $\im(U)$ are additional data.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Factorization immediately implies fibre constancy.  Conversely define
$b(d)=B(x)$ for any $x$ with $U(x)=d$; fibre constancy makes the definition
well-defined.  The uniqueness statements follow from whether every $d$ has a
preimage.
\end{proof}

Apply the theorem to the same old datum decorated by $P=0$ and $P=I$.  Their
ranks are $0$ and $6$, their images are $0$ and $W$, their kernels are $W$ and
$0$, and their quotients $W/\ker P$ are $0$ and $W$.  Consequently rank,
image, kernel, quotient, $P$ itself, and any faithful descendant are not
constant on the forgetful fibre and do not factor through the unchanged
source reduct.

\begin{corollary}[Inert-or-read dichotomy]
If every old-language law ignores $P$, the extension can be model-conservative
but has no bridge force.  If a new downstream law reads $P$ or any faithful
descendant, it can have distinguishing force only by depending on the new

\section{Exact topology and rank-distance scope}

Every nonzero exact idempotent $P$ has eigenvalue one, so for any operator norm
subordinate to a norm on $W$, $\lVert P\rVert\ge 1$.  Hence zero is isolated
inside the exact idempotent locus, and identity is similarly separated from
lower-rank exact idempotents.  This is a correct algebraic statement.

It is not yet a source perturbation theorem.  Consider in a two-dimensional
block, embedded in dimension six,
\[
  P_0=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
  g_t=\begin{pmatrix}1&t\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
  P_t=g_tP_0g_t^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1&-t\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
For every $t$, $P_t^2=P_t$ and $\rank P_t=1$.  As $t\to0$ the matrices
converge in every finite-dimensional norm, but for $t\ne0$,
\[
  \rank(P_t-P_0)=1.
\]
Thus rank distance does not generate the ordinary norm topology and cannot be
called perturbative, physical, or empirical robustness without a separately
derived variation law.

The external approximate control makes the missing law explicit.  For
$0<\epsilon<1$,

The matrices approach zero and their idempotency defect approaches zero, but
they are outside the exact candidate.  Admitting them would require a source
