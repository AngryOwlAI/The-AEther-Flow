## Local And Global Witness Boundary

Finite/local witnesses and checker cases are useful for exposing malformed
branches and counterexample surfaces. They do not prove global adoption, and a
finite checker pass does not become theorem proof. Conversely, a finite/local
failure does not become a global no-go theorem unless a separate theorem proves
that stronger conclusion
(`research_control/tasks/RT-20260614-127/artifacts/159_M_SRC_GSC_FINITE_LOCAL_WITNESS_CLASSIFICATION.tex`;
`research_control/tasks/RT-20260614-129/artifacts/161_M_SRC_GSC_FINITE_SOURCE_COVER_COUNTEREXAMPLE_CATALOG.tex`).

The candidate's current strength is therefore conditional and structural:
finite/local stress evidence supports the fail-closed discipline and helps
identify obstruction labels, but it does not replace the human-gated adoption
review.
