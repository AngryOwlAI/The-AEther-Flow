## Basis

`RT-20260630-006` resolved the protected Gate Chair question and accepted the
matter-signature package only as scoped evidence/precondition. That result is
useful for continuation, but it is not source-law adoption, not canonical
matter semantics, not stress-energy semantics, not detector semantics, not a
matter action, not a coupling law, not matter coupling, and not Einstein

Gate Chair verdict. It is a post-gate route decision: choose the next bounded
non-promotional packet that can use the accepted evidence/precondition without
overreading it. The Theoretical Continuation Selector is the narrowest role for
that decision.
