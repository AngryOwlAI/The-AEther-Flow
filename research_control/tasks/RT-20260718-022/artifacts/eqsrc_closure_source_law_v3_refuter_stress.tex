\section{Nontrivial Two-Object Source-Only Witness}

\begin{definition}[Two-object thin-groupoid source interpretation]
Let \(\mathcal F_{\mathrm{src}}=\{0,1\}\). For each ordered pair \(i,j\), let
\(\Lambda(i,j)=\{\ell_{ij}\}\) and
\(\mathcal P(i,j)=\{p_{ij}\}\), each with equality congruence. Use the unique
endpoint-compatible ledger and proxy identity, inverse, and composition maps.

Declare one primitive source provenance edge
\[
 q:0\longrightarrow 1.
\]
Generate \(\PiSrc\) by the candidate's identity, inverse, and composition
grammar. Under its intended groupoid-style unit, inverse-cancellation,
double-inverse, and associativity equation schemes, every
\(\PiSrc(i,j)\) has exactly one congruence class. Thus the literal raw term
grammar is countably infinite, but its endpoint-indexed congruence quotient is
the finite thin groupoid with one arrow for every ordered pair.

Let \(\mathcal N_{\mathrm{src}}=\{n\}\),
\(\mathcal O_n=\{\Pass,\Block\}\), and
\(\mathcal O_n^{\Pass}=\{\Pass\}\). Let every proxy decision and negative
control pass. Initially let every ledger decision pass:
\[
 P_{i,j}(p_{ij})=\Pass,\qquad
 \delta_{n,i,j}(w)=\Pass,\qquad
 L_{i,j}(\ell_{ij})=\Pass.
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Nontrivial abstract model]
The displayed interpretation satisfies every clause of \(\Law\), and its
induced relation is the universal equivalence relation on \(\{0,1\}\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Every raw certificate is accepted. The unique component constructors respect
the singleton component congruences. Provenance identity, inverse, and
composition satisfy the stated groupoid equation schemes modulo
\(\equiv_{\Pi}\), so certificate unit, inverse, and associativity equations
hold. Accepted identity, inverse, and composition totality follow. The source
guard holds by the explicit root construction below. Each endpoint pair has
an accepted certificate, so the induced relation is universal.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Exact finiteness qualification]
This is a finite-object, finite-root, finite-congruence-quotient witness, not a
finite raw-syntax carrier: nested provenance constructors generate infinitely
many terms before quotienting. It proves abstract nonidentity instantiability
only. It is not a current-ontology interpretation, physical model, or empirical
result.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Provenance-equation qualification]
The witness uses the intended reading that the candidate's ``inverse-unit
equations'' include the two cancellation schemes
\(\mathsf{comp}[\mathsf{inv}[\pi],\pi]\equiv\mathsf{id}\) and
\(\mathsf{comp}[\pi,\mathsf{inv}[\pi]]\equiv\mathsf{id}\).
That reading matches the candidate's full inverse-compatibility clauses and
the prior audit. A proof-assistant translation must display these schemes.
If ``inverse-unit'' were read more weakly, the primitive cross-object
certificate would fail inverse compatibility, yielding a stronger scoped
formalization obstruction rather than this positive witness.
\end{remark}

\section{Transitive Source-Root Provenance Stress}

Let the source-root tuple be
\[
 S=(0,1,q,\{\ell_{ij}\},\{p_{ij}\},n,\Pass,\Block).
\]
Take an ambient interpretation space
\(\Omega=S_{\mathrm{space}}\times T\times W\), where \(T\) and \(W\) hold
arbitrary target and workflow coordinates. Every carrier, constructor,
congruence, decision, and provenance leaf above is a function of the
projection \(\pi_{\mathrm{src}}:\Omega\to S_{\mathrm{space}}\) alone.
Every internal node is a typed source constructor. Consequently the complete
definition graph factors through \(\pi_{\mathrm{src}}\).

No alias, hash, lookup, learned parameter, opaque oracle, target geometry,
detector value, benchmark outcome, registry field, role identity, validator

This is a transitive provenance pass for the constructed abstract
source-extension witness. It is not evidence that current ontology supplies
\(S\), \(q\), the component fibers, or the decision tables. A declaration that
these are ``source'' roots establishes the model's formal hygiene, not their
physical necessity or current-ontology derivation.

\section{Malformed Definitions and Typed Fail Closure}

\begin{proposition}[Typed-domain versus model-level rejection]
The displayed function
\[
 \Accept_{A,B}:\Cert(A,B)\longrightarrow\{\Pass,\Block\}
\]
is total on its typed raw-certificate domain. A malformed or ill-typed
external value is not an element of that domain and therefore does not
literally receive an object-level \(\Block\) value.
\end{proposition}

Missing carriers, partial maps, absent definition trees, backward dependency
edges, endpoint type errors, or forbidden provenance instead make a proposed
interpretation fail the candidate's model condition. This is sound
meta-level malformed-interpretation rejection. Object-level rejection of
arbitrary external inputs would require a separately declared tagged error or
option carrier. The distinction does not invalidate the typed conditional
equivalence theorem.

\section{Congruence, Accepted Totality, and Coherence}

Three independence pressures survive the audit:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Congruence does not imply accepted totality.}
  In a one-object singleton-component structure, make
  \(L_{A,A}(e_A)=\Block\). Acceptance remains total and congruence-invariant,
  but accepted identity totality fails.

  \item \textbf{Accepted totality does not imply full coherence.}
  The RT-20260718-021 two-ledger countermodel accepts every certificate and
  satisfies identity, inverse, and composition totality, yet constantly
  identity-valued ledger composition violates unit compatibility on its
  nonidentity ledger element.

  \item \textbf{Intra-model congruence does not imply inter-model stability.}
  Congruence invariance says equivalent certificates inside one interpretation
  share acceptance status. It does not relate two independently admissible
  source decision maps.
\end{enumerate}

The full \(\Law\) explicitly requires accepted totality and coherence, so the
first two pressures are not contradictions in a full model. They show that the
law's substantive assumptions cannot be recovered merely from acceptance
typing or congruence.

\section{Degenerate-Model Pressure}

The candidate admits the audited one-object singleton model, the two-object
universal model above, and the two-object discrete model constructed below.
More generally, under the intended groupoid reading, a finite equivalence
relation \(R\) can be represented by choosing a source-rooted spanning forest
for each \(R\)-class, taking the induced thin-groupoid quotient, and accepting
exactly the endpoint pairs in \(R\).

Thus \(\Law\) enforces equivalence-style closure on whatever acceptance data
are supplied, but it does not select which equivalence relation is physically
meaningful. Degenerate, discrete, and indiscrete relations are all compatible
with the abstract schema. No naturalness, uniqueness, observable
discriminator, or exact-GR recovery criterion follows.

\section{Law-Preserving Finite-Variation Countermodel}

\begin{countermodel}[Two-entry source-decision variation]
Keep every object, carrier, constructor, congruence, provenance term,
proxy decision, and negative control of the two-object witness fixed.
Define two ledger-decision interpretations:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
 & i=j & i\ne j\\
\hline
\mathcal M_{\mathrm U}:L_{i,j}(\ell_{ij}) & \Pass & \Pass\\
\mathcal M_{\mathrm D}:L_{i,j}(\ell_{ij}) & \Pass & \Block .
\end{array}
\]
The change consists of the two directed off-diagonal table entries
\((0,1)\) and \((1,0)\). Both tables are total, source-factorized, and
congruence-invariant.
\end{countermodel}

\begin{proposition}[Finite-variation selection obstruction]
Both \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm U}\) and \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm D}\) satisfy the
full candidate law, but their induced object relations differ:
\[
 \sim_{\mathcal M_{\mathrm U}}=\{0,1\}\times\{0,1\},
 \qquad
 \sim_{\mathcal M_{\mathrm D}}=\{(0,0),(1,1)\}.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The universal model was proved above. In \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm D}\), every
diagonal certificate is accepted and every off-diagonal certificate is
blocked. Accepted identity totality holds. The inverse of an accepted loop is
an accepted loop, and a composable pair of accepted certificates can only
compose within one endpoint, so accepted inverse and composition totality
hold. Unit, inverse, and associativity compatibility hold modulo the thin
groupoid congruence on accepted loops. All decisions remain source-only and
congruence-invariant. Hence \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm D}\) is also a full model,
but only identical objects are related.
\end{proof}

Exact v3 therefore supplies neither:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item a current-ontology-derived selector fixing \(L\), \(P\), and
  \(\delta_n\); nor
  \item a source-side admissible-variation relation, transport certificate,
  stability law, or naturality condition constraining finite changes to those
  decisions.
\end{itemize}

The countermodel refutes only the overread that exact v3 alone selects a
finite-variation-robust \(\mathrm{EqSrc}\). A narrower variation class could
restore robustness, but that class and its proof are new source-side payload
not present in v3.
