## Neighboring Program Summary

The literature families divide into seven neighboring programs.

First, causal/order-to-geometry work studies how causal ordering, timelike curves, or discrete causal order constrain topology, conformal structure, and manifold-like recovery. P10-SRC-001 through P10-SRC-003 and P10-SRC-005 are relevant to the AEther-Flow `M_src`, scoped `g_eff`, and no-target-certificate burdens because they show that causal or order information can be powerful only under explicit hypotheses.

Second, operational spacetime reconstruction asks how light rays and freely falling trajectories determine geometric structures. P10-SRC-004 is relevant because it makes light propagation and free fall central reconstruction inputs.

Third, universal-coupling and spin-2 bootstrap arguments connect gravitational universality to S-matrix, gauge, or self-coupling assumptions. P10-SRC-006 and P10-SRC-007 are relevant to matter coupling, stress-energy semantics, and the Einstein-equation route.

Fourth, field-equation uniqueness programs identify when an Einstein-like tensor is selected from locality, order, symmetry, dimension, and conservation assumptions. P10-SRC-008 is a constraint source for any route that tries to infer EFE-like dynamics from a metric object.

Fifth, thermodynamic or entropic EFE routes connect local horizon thermodynamics, entropy-area assumptions, heat flux, and a Clausius relation to the Einstein equation. P10-SRC-009 is relevant to the open EFE burden.

Sixth, detector and operational semantics programs make observer response depend on quantum fields, states, trajectories, and detector-field couplings. P10-SRC-010 and P10-SRC-011 are relevant to the positive matter semantics and `RR_E` transport/invariance boundaries.

Seventh, analogue and emergent-gravity sources compare systems whose effective excitations experience metric-like kinematics or induced dynamics. P10-SRC-012 through P10-SRC-014 are useful cautionary comparators: analogue resemblance can illuminate route design while failing to establish universal coupling, full GR dynamics, or benchmark status.

## Relevant Reconstruction Constraints

- Causal or order data can constrain topology and conformal structure, but scale, volume, differentiability, metric law, and dynamics require additional assumptions. This constrains any claim that `M_src` or scoped `g_eff` automatically discharges downstream metric or EFE burdens.
- Operational reconstruction depends on primitive access to light propagation and free-fall behavior. For AEther-Flow these cannot be imported as target spacetime facts; they would need source-side definitions and no-target certificates.
- Discrete causal-set comparison is useful for finite or local witness design, but causal-set programs also carry density, order, manifold-approximation, and volume assumptions. These assumptions are comparison constraints, not AEther-Flow ontology.
- Universal-coupling routes require a substitute for soft-graviton, spin-2, stress-energy, gauge, or self-coupling structures. AEther-Flow currently has scoped matter-semantics evidence/preconditions, not a source-derived universal coupling law.
- EFE uniqueness or thermodynamic derivations require strong assumptions: locality, dimension, second-order form, divergence-free tensor structure, horizon thermodynamics, entropy-area proportionality, and stress-energy flux semantics. The selected P10 sources do not derive those assumptions from current AEther-Flow source primitives.
- Detector-semantics literature constrains response-token interpretation. A source-side response relation is not detector semantics unless field state, detector coupling, trajectory, and readout semantics are explicitly source-derived or declared comparison-only.
- Analogue gravity constrains language around `g_eff`: an effective metric can support kinematic resemblance without proving dynamical GR, universal matter coupling, Einstein equations, exact-GR benchmark status, or completed derivation.
