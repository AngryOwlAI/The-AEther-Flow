\begin{proposition}[Componentwise balance compatibility]
\label{prop:component-balance}
% theorem_id: P7T07-PROP-COMPONENTWISE-BALANCE-COMPATIBILITY-001
Let \(\Omega_1,\ldots,\Omega_k\) be the connected components of the undirected
positive support of the P7-T06 symmetric coefficient record \(C\). For a
rational residual \(a\), the finite equation
\[
 L_Cq=a
\]
has a rational solution if and only if
\[
 \sum_{x\in\Omega_j}a_x=0
 \quad\text{for every }j=1,\ldots,k .
\]
In words, the residual must have zero sum on every connected component.
Thus global zero sum is sufficient only when the support is connected.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The finite symmetric Laplacian satisfies
\[
 q^{\mathsf T}L_Cq
 =\frac12\sum_{x,y}C_{xy}(q_x-q_y)^2 .
\]
Its kernel therefore consists exactly of vectors constant on each connected
component. Since \(L_C\) is symmetric, its image is the orthogonal complement
of that kernel, which is precisely the subspace of vectors whose sum vanishes
on every component. The matrix and residual are rational, so ordinary
rational row reduction supplies a rational solution whenever the
compatibility conditions hold.
\end{proof}
