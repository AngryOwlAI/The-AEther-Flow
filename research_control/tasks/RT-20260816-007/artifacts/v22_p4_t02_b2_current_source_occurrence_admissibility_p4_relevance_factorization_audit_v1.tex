\section{Exact factorization theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Image factorization]
Let $U:X\to D$ and $R:X\to Y$ be maps of sets.  There is a unique map
$f_{\im}:\im(U)\to Y$ satisfying
\begin{equation}
 R=f_{\im}\circ U
 \label{eq:factor}
\end{equation}
if and only if $R$ is constant on every fiber of $U$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
If \eqref{eq:factor} holds and $U(x)=U(x')$, then
$R(x)=f_{\im}(U(x))=f_{\im}(U(x'))=R(x')$.  Conversely, if $R$ is
fiber-constant, define $f_{\im}(d)=R(x)$ for any $x$ with $U(x)=d$.
Fiber constancy makes this well defined, and every $d\in\im(U)$ has a
preimage.  The same formula proves uniqueness on $\im(U)$.
\end{proof}

An all-$D$ factor additionally requires an extension of $f_{\im}$ away from
$\im(U)$.  If $U$ is surjective, the image factor is already the unique
all-$D$ factor.  If $U$ is not surjective and $Y$ has at least two elements,
different values on $D\setminus\im(U)$ give different extensions.  A
singleton codomain is the degenerate exception, so surjectivity must not be
advertised as a converse without hypotheses.

The structured version also requires independently declared source-arrow
actions on $X$, $D$, and $Y$ and commuting naturality squares.  Deleting
arrows because they fail to preserve the desired output is not a proof of
naturality.

\section{Conditional conservative theorem and sharp falsifiers}

Call an occurrence extension \emph{conservative at principal level} only when
it changes none of the following:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
 \item the background source-law datum and highest-derivative coefficients;
 \item the algebraic or differential constraint spaces;
 \item the internal gauge generators or equation redundancies; and
 \item the lawful reduced field, equation, and quotient data.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{proposition}[Conditional source-principal constancy]
For a typed product extension $X=D_{\mathrm{src}}\times E$ satisfying all
four conservative conditions, with $U(D,e)=D$, the actual P4--T01
recomputation satisfies
\begin{equation}
 R_{\mathrm{PS}}(D,e)=\operatorname{Prin}(D)
 =\operatorname{Prin}\bigl(U(D,e)\bigr).
\end{equation}
Hence $R_{\mathrm{PS}}$ is constant on every $U$-fiber and factors on
$\im(U)$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The hypotheses fix the reduced domain, reduced codomain, and every leading
coefficient.  Principal-symbol formation and the determinant-line operation
therefore receive identical inputs for all $e$ in one fiber.  Equation
\eqref{eq:p4t01} is unchanged.
\end{proof}

This proposition is conditional and does not source-generate $E$, the product
carrier, its arrows, or the conservative law.  The countercontrols are sharp.
At $k=(1,2,3,5)$ the six registered factors give $P_D(k)=60480$.  Replacing
$X_1=(1,1,0,0)$ by $X'_1=(1,2,0,0)$ gives $P_{D'}(k)=100800$.  This is a
coefficient change and therefore a change of source-law reduct, not an
occurrence-only conservative variation.  Likewise, adding a constraint,
gauge, or redundancy direction that removes one reduced channel changes the
determinant degree from six to five.  It is a lawful-reduction change, not an
element of the same conservative fiber.

Finally, extension multiplicity does not decide relevance.  Two unselected
roots over one reduct can carry a constant $R$ and factor, or a separating
$R$ and fail to factor.  Nonselection therefore implies neither
source-principal relevance nor source-principal irrelevance.
