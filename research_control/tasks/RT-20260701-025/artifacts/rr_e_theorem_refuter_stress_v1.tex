\section{Stress Theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Fail-closed \(\RRE\) stress theorem]
Let \(r,r'\in\AdmPMS(E)\) be finite/local source records in the P5 domain.
Under the P5-T04 stress family, every branch has one of the following source
outcomes:
\[
  \TrRR(r,r')\ \text{or}\ \InvRR(F;r,r')\ \text{or}\ \FactRR(F),
  \qquad
  r\SepRR r',
  \qquad
  \RRUnder,
  \qquad
  \Obstruction.
\]
No stress branch yields unrestricted \(\RRE\) irrelevance, detector-semantics
collapse, \(\MissingLaw\) adoption, matter semantics, detector semantics,
matter coupling, stress-energy semantics, matter action, \(\MetricData(E)\),
\(\geff\) scope expansion, Einstein equations, benchmark promotion, completed
derivation, future source-extension impossibility, or global theory rejection.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The P5-T02 factor-through lemma is formal and conditional: if a declared
source quotient and factorization are supplied, equality follows on quotient
fibers. The P5-T02 countermodel supplies two source records \(r_0,r_1\) with
equal non-\(\RRE\) source support, equal certificate data, and distinct
\(\Qsrc\) syntax. If no source transport, source invariance, or factorization
certificate is supplied, the source construction cannot identify the records
for arbitrary \(F\). Therefore stress branches either exhibit one of the
missing source witnesses, preserve separation, return \(\RRUnder\), or return
\(\Obstruction\) when they invoke forbidden target or process authority. The
codomain contains no adoption value and no downstream GR-recovery value.
\end{proof}

\section{Pressure Lemmas}

\begin{lemma}[Certificate deletion guard]
If a proposed \(\RRE\) identification of \(r,r'\) depends on \(\TrRR\),
\(\InvRR(F;r,r')\), or \(\FactRR(F)\), then deleting the named source
certificate invalidates the identification branch. The fail-closed result is
\(\RRUnder\) or \(\Obstruction\), not detector semantics.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The P5 target admits only source transport, source invariance, or
factor-through proof as identification authority. Removing that authority
leaves distinct retained \(\RRE\) syntax without a lawful source witness.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[No \(\geff\)-mediated \(\RRE\) collapse]
A map from records to \(\MetricData(E)\), \(\geff\), target metric behavior, or
benchmark fit cannot serve as \(\TrRR\), \(\InvRR\), or \(\FactRR\) unless a
separate source-side proof first constructs the relevant source quotient and
factorization without target imports.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The P5-T01 theorem target explicitly excludes target geometry, \(\MetricData(E)\),
\(\geff\), Einstein equations, benchmark behavior, and process authority as
premises of \(\RRE\) equivalence or irrelevance. Thus any branch that uses
those objects as the identification reason is outside the allowed source
language and returns \(\Obstruction\).
\end{proof}

or factorization proof.
