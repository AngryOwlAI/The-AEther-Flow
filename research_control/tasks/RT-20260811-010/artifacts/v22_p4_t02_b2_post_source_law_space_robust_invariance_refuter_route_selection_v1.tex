\begin{theorem}[Compact-total root-family coercive minimum]
\label{thm:minimum}
Let \(\mathcal R_P\) be a nonempty compact space and let
\(\kappa_P:\mathcal R_P\to\R_{>0}\) be finite and lower semicontinuous.  Then
\[
  \kappa_*(P):=\min_{r\in\mathcal R_P}\kappa_P(r)
\]
exists, is attained, and is strictly positive.  If an \(\EqSrc\) arrow acts
by a homeomorphism and transports \(\kappa\) naturally, it preserves
\(\kappa_*\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
A finite real-valued lower-semicontinuous function on a nonempty compact
space attains its minimum.  Every value lies in \(\R_{>0}\), so the attained
minimum is positive.  Natural transport composes \(\kappa_P\) with a
homeomorphism and therefore preserves the value set and its minimum.
\end{proof}

The exact rational fixture uses three total roots with values
\[

  \qquad \kappa(r_2)=\frac54.
\]
Thus \(\kappa_*=5/4\).  A three-cycle transports roots and their values;
declared variation and transverse maps close on the three-root set.  This
checks the theorem's algebra only.  It does not show that project ontology
derives the datum.
