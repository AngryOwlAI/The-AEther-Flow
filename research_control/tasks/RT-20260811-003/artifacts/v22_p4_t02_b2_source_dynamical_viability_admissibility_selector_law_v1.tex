\section{Uniform source-barrier theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Normalized bounded-step propagation]
\label{thm:barrier}
Let $\gamma,\Delta,a_\star>0$ and set
\begin{equation}
 \lambda=
 \left(1+2\gamma\Delta a_\star^2\right)^{-1/2}>0.
 \label{eq:lambda}
\end{equation}
For every $a\in\Us$ and every $0\leq t\leq\Delta$,
\begin{equation}
 \bs(\phi_t(a))\geq\lambda\bs(a)>0.
 \label{eq:bound}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The flow has the same sign as $a$ for every finite $t\geq0$.  Direct
substitution gives
\[
 \frac{\bs(\phi_t(a))^2}{\bs(a)^2}
 =\frac{a_\star^2+a^2}
 {a_\star^2+\left(1+2\gamma t a_\star^2\right)a^2}.
\]
Write $A=a_\star^2$, $u=a^2$, $c_t=2\gamma tA$, and
$C=2\gamma\Delta A$.  Comparing the last expression with
$\lambda^2=(1+C)^{-1}$ and clearing positive denominators leaves
\[
 (1+C)(A+u)-\bigl(A+(1+c_t)u\bigr)
 =CA+(C-c_t)u\geq0,
\]
because $A,u,C>0$ and $0\leq c_t\leq C$.  Both barriers are positive on
$\Us$, so taking positive square roots proves~\eqref{eq:bound}.
\end{proof}

The normalization is essential only for the displayed uniform factor on the
noncompact amplitude line.  With the unnormalized barrier $\sigma a$, the
one-step ratio tends to zero as $|a|\to\infty$.  The reference $a_\star$ is
therefore disclosed as certificate data rather than smuggled in as a physical
amplitude scale.
