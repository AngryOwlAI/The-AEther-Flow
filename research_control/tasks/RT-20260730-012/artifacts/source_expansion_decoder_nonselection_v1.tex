\begin{abstract}
This draft/control source-stage note tests what the registered proposal-only
one-mode amplitude flow determines before any comparison oracle is opened.
It derives an exact positive one-parameter counterfamily of normalized
expansion tokens, their logarithmic rates and deceleration tokens, and a
two-parameter amount-to-density bookkeeping family.  The same amplitude
history supports mutually distinct outputs, while the current source package
contains no selector for the decoder exponent, physical clock, volume map, or
constant-term interpretation.  The result is a precise current-source
nonselection obstruction, not a global impossibility result.
\end{abstract}

\section{Fixed source record}

The only dynamical input is the registered P5 proposal-only one-mode record
\[
 q_A(\theta)=A\sin\theta_1,\qquad
 \frac{dA}{d\lambda}=-\gamma A^3,\qquad
 \gamma>0,\quad A(0)=\Ainit>0 .
\]
Its exact forward solution is
\[
 A(\lambda)
   =\frac{\Ainit}{\sqrt{1+2\gamma\Ainit^2\lambda}},
 \qquad \lambda\geq0 .
\]
The source record has one global amplitude and no independent spatial labels.
We call this \emph{single-mode source uniformity}.  It does not establish a
physical spatial homogeneity or isotropy, and \(\lambda\) is not identified
with physical time.

\begin{definition}[Decoder counterfamily]
For every \(p>0\), define the normalized positive token
\[
 b_p(\lambda)
   :=\left(\frac{\Ainit}{A(\lambda)}\right)^p
    =\left(1+2\gamma\Ainit^2\lambda\right)^{p/2}.
\]
Define its update-index logarithmic rate and deceleration token by
\[
 \Etoken_p:=\frac{d}{d\lambda}\log b_p,\qquad
 \Qtoken_p:=-\frac{b_p b_p''}{(b_p')^2}.
\]
These are source bookkeeping tokens only.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Exact decoder nonselection]
\label{thm:decoder}
For the fixed amplitude record and every \(p>0\),
\[
 \Etoken_p(\lambda)
    =p\gamma A(\lambda)^2
    =\frac{p\gamma\Ainit^2}
           {1+2\gamma\Ainit^2\lambda},
 \qquad
 \frac{d\Etoken_p}{d\lambda}
    =-2p\gamma^2 A(\lambda)^4,
\]
and
\[
 \Qtoken_p=\frac{2}{p}-1.
\]
If \(p\neq p'\), then \(b_p\neq b_{p'}\) on every interval containing a
positive value of \(\lambda\).  Hence the same exact source history determines
no unique member of this counterfamily unless a selector for \(p\) is added.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Substitution of the exact amplitude solution gives the displayed expression
for \(b_p\).  Logarithmic differentiation and the source evolution equation
give
\[
 \Etoken_p=-p\frac{A'}A=p\gamma A^2
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 \Etoken_p'=2p\gamma A A'=-2p\gamma^2 A^4.
\]
Writing \(b_p=(1+c\lambda)^\alpha\) with
\(c=2\gamma\Ainit^2\) and \(\alpha=p/2\) yields
\[
 -\frac{b_p b_p''}{(b_p')^2}
   =-\frac{\alpha(\alpha-1)}{\alpha^2}
   =\frac{2}{p}-1 .
\]
For \(\lambda>0\), the base \(1+2\gamma\Ainit^2\lambda\) is greater than one,
so unequal positive exponents give unequal tokens.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Clock-calibration nonselection]
For every \(s>0\), the monotone calibration \(t_s=s\lambda\) changes the
logarithmic rate to
\[
 \frac{d}{dt_s}\log b_p=\frac{\Etoken_p}{s}.
\]
The source record therefore does not select a physical rate even after a
decoder exponent is fixed.
\end{corollary}

\section{Amount-to-density bookkeeping}

Let \(M_0\) be a conserved source amount.  For every \(r>0\), define
\[
 V_{p,r}:=b_p^r,\qquad
 \rho_{p,r}:=M_0 b_p^{-r}.
\]
Then the exact identity
\[
 \frac{d\rho_{p,r}}{d\lambda}
   =-r\Etoken_p\rho_{p,r}
\]
holds.  It is not a physical continuity equation: the current source package
does not supply a physical volume map or select \(r\).  Likewise, the fixed
amplitude equation contains no independent constant term and supplies no
source-derived map from such a term to a physical vacuum interpretation.

\section{Exact obstruction and scope}

The failed object is a total source-derived map from the proposal-only
amplitude record to a unique physical scale, clock, volume, matter density,
and constant-term interpretation.  Its absence is recorded as
\[
\texttt{OBST-P9T05-SOURCE-COSMOLOGY-DECODER-CALIBRATION-NONSELECTION-001}.
\]
The current source package does not derive that map.  Conservative
source-side extensions with independent local data, a physical clock,
spatial-volume semantics, or another selector remain open.  No source law is
adopted or rejected, and no future source extension is ruled out.
