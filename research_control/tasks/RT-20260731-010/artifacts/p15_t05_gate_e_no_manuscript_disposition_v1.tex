\[

\]
Let $D_{\mathrm{cnr}}(E_0)$ denote the task disposition

\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[P15T05-PROP-FIXED-GATE-E-NO-MANUSCRIPT-V1]
Assume:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(H\arabic*)]
  \item the fixed package $E_0$ is the package reviewed by P9-T09;
  \item the protected P9-T09 decision gives $G_E(E_0)=0$;
  \item P15-T05 permits manuscript preparation exactly when
  $P_M(E_0)\Longleftrightarrow G_E(E_0)$;
  \item no materially new reopening evidence or fresh Gate E reversal has
  entered the package;
  \item the current authorization is review authority only.
\end{enumerate}
Then $P_M(E_0)=0$, manuscript preparation is outside the lawful task scope,
and $D_{\mathrm{cnr}}(E_0)$ is the unique plan-conforming disposition among
the manuscript and no-manuscript branches.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By (H2), $G_E(E_0)=0$.  Substitution into the biconditional in (H3) gives
$P_M(E_0)=0$.  Therefore the manuscript branch is not permitted.  By (H4),
the fixed-package result cannot be replaced by a positive Gate E value in this
review.  By (H5), authorization cannot supply any missing scientific premise.
The P15-T05 definition of done requires \texttt{conditionally\_not\_required}
or a human-gated record when no manuscript is authorized.  Exact review
authority is present, so the unresolved-human-gate branch is inapplicable and
$D_{\mathrm{cnr}}(E_0)$ is the remaining plan-conforming disposition.
\end{proof}
