\section{Scope and Audit Question}

This audit responds to \texttt{handoff-0068}. The inspected packet is
\texttt{67\_LOCALIZATION\_SOURCE\_BASIS\_AXIOM\_SELECTOR\_DOMAIN\_EQSRC\_LEDGER\_COMPLETE\_SOURCE\_FAMILY\_GENERATOR\_PACKET\_FINITE\_EXAMPLE\_READOUT\_WITNESS.tex}.
That packet supplies a finite positive pair \(X_{+},Y_{+}\), a
near-miss negative object \(Y_{-}\), a finite boundary example,
failed-certificate witnesses, a concrete source-pure readout codomain
with componentwise equality, and a local theorem
\[
    X\EqLC Y \Longrightarrow \ReadLC(X)=\ReadLC(Y)
\]
for the declared certificate relation.

The audit question is:
\[
    \text{Does the witness/readout packet obtain its local result by
    importing target primitives or authority labels?}
\]

The answer is no under the stated source-ledger reading. The pass is
conditional and local: the packet is a useful audit object and source
consistency check, not a bridge theorem.

that transports labels, annotations, proxy guards, forbidden-channel

contraction-certificate data, and boundary anchors together. The audit
does not detect target metric, proper time, locality, observer
protocol, benchmark rank, candidate behavior, or downstream success as
a selection primitive for the pair.

ledger has a valid local certificate. It does not show that this finite

negative-control argument.

The negative example remains local. It tests one guard/blocker failure
mode and does not exhaust all adverse source variations.
\end{screen}

\(\EqSrc\), \(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), downstream bridge readiness,

\section{Residual Risks}

\begin{residualrisk}[Finite coverage remains unproved]
The witness suite supplies useful positive, negative, boundary, and
failed-certificate examples, but it does not prove coverage of the
source families needed for broader \(\EqSrc\) work.
\end{residualrisk}

\begin{residualrisk}[Readout may be overcomplete]
\(\ReadLC\) counts many preserved ledger coordinates. A future bridge
step might need a smaller, differently structured, or physically
interpretable readout. This audit does not supply that bridge step.
\end{residualrisk}

\begin{residualrisk}[Certificate/readout co-design tautology]
Because \(\ReadLC\) is built from coordinates already preserved by
\(\CertLC\), invariance is expected. That is acceptable for a
source-consistency check but insufficient as scientific adequacy.
\end{residualrisk}

\begin{residualrisk}[Quotient and refinement pressure remains]
The failed-certificate examples are locally useful, but quotient
representatives, refinement inverses, and contraction labels remain
stress-testable under additional finite variations.
\end{residualrisk}

\begin{residualrisk}[Bridge-ladder overread remains the main risk]
Source-side invariance, source-side adequacy, bridge relevance,
\(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), candidate reconstruction, and benchmark
recovery are distinct burdens. Collapsing them returns \(\Block\).
\end{residualrisk}
