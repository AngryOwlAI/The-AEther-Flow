`MatterSemanticsFailureObstruction_v1`. The stress result is a current-basis
`scoped_obstruction`: under the current source basis, the ontology does not
derive a source-intrinsic non-bottom matter-sector discriminator.

`blocked_adoption_open_continuation`. That means current adoption is blocked
while a conservative source-side law packet remains possible. Under the
selector role contract, a non-promotional constructive packet is preferred
when available. The next lawful constructive packet is therefore not a Gate
Chair verdict, not Einstein-equation work, and not generic support tooling. It
is a narrow ontology-law packet for the missing source-side primitive.

and select the next packet needed before matter-coupling or Einstein-equation
