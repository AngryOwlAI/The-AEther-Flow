# Cross-Manuscript Glossary Boundary v15

## Scope

This artifact implements v15 P18-T03. It is a shared control glossary for the
P18 physics-program manuscript outline and the P18 AI research-agent
methodology manuscript outline. It aligns with the P14 epistemic category
glossary and adds manuscript-specific usage boundaries.

This glossary is not a physics proof, not a canonical ontology edit, not a
Gate Chair verdict, not a manuscript submission, and not authority for
benchmark promotion or completed derivation.

## Use Rule

Both manuscript outlines must use this glossary as the local shared vocabulary
for ambiguous terms. If a manuscript needs a stronger meaning than the row
allows, it must cite a separate tracked authority source and preserve the exact
scope of that authority.

## Shared Glossary

| Term | Controlled meaning | Physics manuscript use | AI methodology manuscript use | Forbidden cross-manuscript overread |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AEther-flow ontology | A proposed source-first research ontology or interpretation used under tracked authority boundaries. | May describe the program's explanatory frame and source-side research ontology. | May describe the ontology only as the research object governed by workflow controls. | Do not present it as established physical ontology or as adopted by the AI workflow. |
| Exact-GR benchmark | The protected target of exact general-relativity benchmark compatibility or recovery obligations. | May state the benchmark as an obligation target and blocked promotion route. | May use it as an example of a protected benchmark burden. | Do not collapse benchmark target language into benchmark promotion or completed recovery. |
| Interpretation | A proposed explanatory frame for organizing source-side structures and research questions. | May frame AEther-flow as a research interpretation. | May discuss interpretation as a claim category requiring grounding. | Do not treat interpretation as derivation or physical theory. |
| Derivation | A source-backed argument establishing a target statement from declared assumptions under declared rules. | May discuss missing or scoped derivation burdens. | May discuss derivation as a claim type outside process authority. | Do not call validator receipts, route selection, or generated text a derivation. |
| Source-side object | An object whose status is restricted to declared source-side construction. | May use for scoped objects such as `M_src` under exact status language. | May use as an example of a claim that requires canonical source inspection. | Do not present source-side naming as physical-object status. |
| Source-extension object | A source-side object or datum admitted only within a declared source-extension scope. | May use for scoped `Resp_lc` or `g_eff` status where tracked sources permit it. | May use as a workflow example of scoped acceptance. | Do not promote source-extension status to downstream matter coupling or Einstein equations. |
| Scoped evidence/precondition | A bounded result usable only as evidence or a prerequisite under its declared boundary. | May summarize matter-sector and `RR_E` evidence as preconditions. | May describe evidence/precondition as a category protected from adoption overread. | Do not shorten evidence/precondition to adoption, proof, or physical semantics. |
| Gate Chair adoption | A tracked human-gated decision that changes exact scoped status. | May cite only exact Gate Chair scope when present. | May describe Gate Chair review as protected human authority. | Do not generalize a scoped Gate Chair result to source-law adoption, benchmark promotion, or completed derivation. |
| Validator receipt | Operational evidence that a file, registry, schema, or workflow passed a specified check. | May use validators as process integrity evidence. | May use validators as workflow evidence. | Do not treat validator PASS as scientific proof or Gate Chair authority. |
| Matter coupling | The still-protected downstream burden of universal matter coupling. | May state current matter-coupling status and open prerequisites. | May use as an example of a protected downstream claim. | Do not infer matter coupling from source-side objects, evidence/preconditions, or route progress. |
| Detector semantics | Physical detector or readout meaning not supplied by source-side naming alone. | May identify detector semantics as open or blocked where tracked sources say so. | May use as a smuggling-risk example. | Do not infer detector semantics from `Resp_lc`, `RR_E`, validators, or AI summaries. |
| Stress-energy semantics | Physical stress-energy meaning and downstream tensor semantics. | May list stress-energy semantics as an open burden. | May use as a protected physical-semantics example. | Do not infer stress-energy semantics from matter-sector evidence or method validation. |
| Benchmark promotion | A protected status upgrade that requires tracked benchmark authority. | May state benchmark promotion remains blocked unless authority changes. | May describe it as a human-gated status change outside agent autonomy. | Do not equate exact-GR target, compatibility language, or public status text with promotion. |
| Completed derivation | Completion of the first-principles derivation chain through required source and downstream burdens. | Must state that completed GR derivation is not present. | Must state that the workflow does not itself complete a derivation. | Do not imply completion from outlines, validators, memory, dependency graphs, or route selection. |

## Alignment With P14

This glossary inherits the P14 distinctions:

- interpretation versus derivation;
- model versus physical theory;
- exact-GR benchmark compatibility versus first-principles recovery;
- scoped source object versus physical object;
- evidence/precondition versus adoption;
- validator receipt versus scientific proof;
- publication surface versus authority source.

P18 adds a manuscript-facing rule: the physics manuscript may discuss the
scientific program only within tracked source status, and the AI methodology
manuscript may discuss workflow controls only as process discipline. Neither
manuscript may borrow stronger wording from the other.

## Required Cross-References

- The physics-program outline must cite this glossary when discussing
  ontology, benchmark, scoped source objects, evidence/preconditions, matter
  coupling, detector semantics, stress-energy semantics, benchmark promotion,
  and completed derivation.
- The AI methodology outline must cite this glossary when discussing
  validation, memory, role outputs, route selection, human gates, and
  protected physics claims.

## No-Delta Receipt

- Scientific claims changed: false.
- Physics promotion authorized: false.
- Proof authority changed: false.
- Benchmark promotion authorized: false.
- Completed derivation authorized: false.
- Public publication action authorized: false.
