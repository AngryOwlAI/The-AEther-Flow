# DDR-20260718-017: EqSrc Closure Source-Law Smuggling Audit

## Decision

Select `smuggling-auditor@0.2.0` through one task overlay for the bounded
`ontology-law-research-packet` required by `handoff-0741`. The audit tests the
proposal-only `EqSrcClosureLaw_src^cand` for target import, process-authority
laundering, certificate-as-adoption overread, and formal overstatement.

## Basis

`research_control/handoffs/handoff-0741.yaml` names a single immediate packet
and role. The candidate exists as source-extension data but is neither audited
nor adopted. A Smuggling Auditor is therefore the narrowest role that can
separate source-purity evidence from formal sufficiency and adoption authority.

## Claim Boundary

Allowed: inspect the candidate's source vocabulary; test whether its clauses
entail reflexivity, symmetry, and transitivity; give a minimal source-side
countermodel to any unsupported entailment; distinguish textual source purity
from formal sufficiency; preserve proposal-only and
`blocked_adoption_open_continuation` status; route a bounded repair.

Forbidden: canonical ontology edit; source-law adoption; general `EqSrc`
discharge; `RetainH` or `GenH` adoption; target atlas or target metric import;
registry, role, validator, approval, generated derivative, or checkpoint as
physics proof; matter coupling; Einstein equations; benchmark promotion; Gate
Chair verdict; completed derivation; global no-go or theory rejection.

## Stop Rule

Stop after the audit records either a validated next stress packet or one exact
repairable obstruction. Do not perform the repair in this AgentJob.
