\section{Stress Lemmas}

\begin{lemma}[Finite source perturbation survival or bottom]
Under a finite source-signature-preserving relabeling or restriction of
\(\EStar\), \(\KStar\) survives only when the relabeled or restricted records,
certificates, no-target certificate, detector block, and source tokens are
carried along as source-side data. If a mutated record is not in the declared
source domain and no source-side update is supplied, the candidate returns
\(\BottomSrc\) or an explicit missing-domain obstruction.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The graph of \(\KStar\) has exactly three declared source records. A
source-signature-preserving relabeling changes names but not the source-side
typing of records, certificates, or tokens. A restriction can remove graph
entries and yields only a smaller finite draft/control subcase. A new,
collapsed, or target-repaired record is not in the declared domain. Since the
candidate is a partial source-side map, no target geometry or process receipt
can extend its domain.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Certificate deletion and malformed replacement fail closed]
If a required source certificate in \(\SCLBundle(\EStar)\) is deleted or
replaced by a malformed certificate whose domain, codomain, witness, or
authority source is not source-side, the non-bottom branch of \(\KStar\) is
blocked.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Each graph value of \(\KStar\) contains named source certificates. Removing
one removes a premise of that graph value. Replacing one with target metric
data, detector protocol data, stress-energy data, matter-action data,
benchmark data, or process metadata violates the codomain
\(\SCLBundle(\EStar)\times T_{\EStar}^{\Src}\) and the no-target certificate.
The candidate supplies fail-closed markers rather than any target or process
repair.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Detector placeholder replacement is not detector semantics]
The block
\[
  \DetPlaceholder(\EStar)=
  (\mathrm{status}=\mathrm{missing\_and\_blocked},
   \mathrm{reason}=\mathrm{no\_source\_side\_detector\_replacement\_law})
\]
prevents detector-placeholder stress from becoming detector semantics.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The placeholder is an explicit absence marker. Erasing it leaves the candidate
without its declared detector component. Replacing it by empirical readout,
clocks, rods, calibration, proper-time normalization, or detector response
laws imports forbidden non-source premises. Therefore detector stress either
preserves the explicit block or fails closed.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Overread pressure does not produce matter coupling]
Stress survival of \(\KStar\) does not supply source-law adoption, detector
semantics, coupling-law adoption, matter-coupling derivation, stress-energy
semantics, matter action, Einstein equations, benchmark promotion, or proof

\begin{proof}

explicit detector block, source bookkeeping tokens, no-target guards, and
