\section{Status and exact question}

This document is a \texttt{draft/control}, \texttt{proposal-only}
formalization target.  It is source-extension data, not a canonical-ontology
candidate, an adopted law, or a field-equation derivation.  Its status pair is
\texttt{blocked\_adoption\_open\_continuation}.

The exact P8-T02 question is:
\begin{quote}
Which source-derived objects and mathematical hypotheses would be sufficient
to infer a local effective variational functional from the adopted P7
source-matter action, response precursor, and componentwise balance data,
without using a target atlas, target metric, Einstein--Hilbert action,
Einstein equations, or benchmark success as a premise?
\end{quote}

The answer below is a conditional target plus a fail-closed audit of its
antecedent.  It does not assert that the required objects presently exist.

\section{Typed source and effective objects}

\begin{definition}[Fixed source datum]
Let
\[
 \Dsrc=(\mathcal S_{\mathrm{src}},A_{\mathrm{src}},
 \delta A_{\mathrm{src}},R_{\mathrm{src}},B_{\mathrm{src}})
\]
denote the exact adopted P7 package in its declared finite source domains:
source states, the source-matter action, its admitted finite variation, its
response precursor, and its componentwise balance records.  Adoption supplies
their physical source-scope meaning by constitutive postulate.  It does not
supply any source-to-effective map.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Candidate effective closure datum]
For a declared scale \(\ell\) and approximation order \(N\), an effective
closure datum is the tuple
\[
 \mathfrak C_{\ell,N}=
 (X_\ell,\mathcal U_\ell,\mu_\ell,\Qeff,\Meff,\Conf,
 C_\ell,M_\ell,\mathsf G_\ell,\Eop,
 \mathsf B_\ell,\mathsf H_\ell,\Rem).
\]
Its components have the following types.
\begin{longtable}{p{0.19\linewidth}p{0.73\linewidth}}
\toprule
Symbol & Required type and meaning\\
\midrule
\(X_\ell\) &
A source-derived effective site or carrier base.  It may be discrete or
continuous, but its points, neighborhoods, and dimension may not be copied
from a target spacetime.\\
\(\mathcal U_\ell\) &
A specified local cover or locality relation on \(X_\ell\), with restriction
maps and overlap data.\\
\(\mu_\ell\) &
A source-derived integration or summation density on \(X_\ell\), including
orientation or sign data when required.\\
\(\Qeff,\Meff\) &
Typed gravitational-carrier and matter-carrier bundles, sheaves, or finite
analogues over \(X_\ell\).  No metric interpretation is implied by the

\(\Conf\) &
The admitted configuration space of sections or finite configurations,
including regularity class, gauge quotient if any, and allowed variations.\\
\(C_\ell\) &
A source-to-gravitational-carrier map
\(C_\ell:\mathcal S_{\mathrm{src}}^{(\ell)}
\rightarrow\Gamma(\Qeff)\), with exact domain, codomain, regularity, and

\(M_\ell\) &

\end{definition}

\section{Assumption envelope}

The closure target has the following independently auditable hypotheses.

\begin{enumerate}
\item[\(\mathbf{A0}\)] \textbf{Fixed source scope.}
Every input is traced to \(\Dsrc\) or to an explicitly marked
proposal-only source extension.  Process state and generated derivatives
carry no mathematical authority.

\item[\(\mathbf{A1}\)] \textbf{Source-derived carrier and locality.}
\(X_\ell,\mathcal U_\ell,\Qeff,\Meff\), their restriction maps, and their
dimensions are defined without target atlas or metric input.

\item[\(\mathbf{A2}\)] \textbf{Typed source-to-effective maps.}
\(C_\ell\) and \(M_\ell\) are defined on stated coarse-grained source domains;
their codomains, regularity, covariance or replacement behavior, fibers, and
failure sets are exact.

\item[\(\mathbf{A3}\)] \textbf{Local measure and finite derivative order.}
\(\mu_\ell\) and a jet order or finite-neighborhood radius exist, and the
projected response at a point depends only on that declared local datum.

\item[\(\mathbf{A4}\)] \textbf{Scale and correction control.}
The coarse-graining map, valid scale interval, truncation order, and
\(\|\Rem\|\leq\varepsilon_{\ell,N}\) bound are source-side statements.

\item[\(\mathbf{A5}\)] \textbf{Projected response operator.}
The first source variation descends through \(C_\ell\) and \(M_\ell\) to a
well-defined local operator \(\Eop\) on \(\Conf\), independent of
representatives up to the declared equivalence.

\item[\(\mathbf{A6}\)] \textbf{Inverse-variational integrability.}
The linearization of \(\Eop\) is formally self-adjoint modulo the declared
boundary form, or an equivalent set of local Helmholtz conditions holds.
These are hypotheses of the imported inverse problem of the calculus of
variations; P7 balance alone does not imply them.

\item[\(\mathbf{A7}\)] \textbf{Global gluing.}
Local Lagrangian densities supplied by \(\mathbf{A6}\) have exact overlap
transgressions, satisfy the required cocycle, and have vanishing declared
variational-cohomology obstruction.  Otherwise only patchwise
variationality is obtained.

\item[\(\mathbf{A8}\)] \textbf{Symmetry and conservation bridge.}
\(\mathsf G_\ell\) is source-derived; \(C_\ell\) and \(M_\ell\) are equivariant
or their controlled defect is specified; and a typed map carries
componentwise source balance to the candidate effective Noether or Ward
identity.  Conservation is a conclusion of this bridge, not a relabeling of
the P7 postulate.

\item[\(\mathbf{A9}\)] \textbf{Boundary and well-posed variation.}
\(\mathsf B_\ell\) cancels or controls the variational boundary form.  Gauge
zero modes, constraints, principal symbol or finite analogue, and admitted
initial or boundary data are stated before any well-posedness claim.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conditional local-action closure target}

\begin{target}[Local effective-action closure]
Assume \(\mathbf{A0}\)--\(\mathbf{A9}\) for one exact
\(\mathfrak C_{\ell,N}\).  Then the proof target is to construct, up to the
declared boundary terms, local field redefinitions, and variationally trivial
densities, a functional
\[
 \Seff[q,\psi]
 =\int_{X_\ell}\mu_\ell\,
   \mathcal L_{\ell,N}
   (j^r q,j^s\psi)
   +\Rem[q,\psi]
\]
whose first variation has Euler--Lagrange operator
\(\Eop+O(\varepsilon_{\ell,N})\) on the admitted configurations.
Local existence must follow from \(\mathbf{A6}\); global existence must follow
from \(\mathbf{A7}\).  Equivariance and the balance bridge in
\(\mathbf{A8}\) must yield the stated effective identity, subject to the
boundary data in \(\mathbf{A9}\).
\end{target}

This target is an implication to be proved in a later construction packet.
The expression above is a type signature, not a constructed action.  Even a
successful proof would establish only a controlled effective variational
structure in its stated regime.  It would not, by itself, identify
\(\Qeff\) with Lorentzian metrics or identify its Euler--Lagrange equations
with Einstein equations.

\section{Separate Einstein-leading recovery burden}

No GR-leading conclusion belongs in the antecedent of local-action closure.
After an action has been independently constructed, an Einstein-leading
comparison would require a second theorem with at least:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\(\mathbf{R1}\)] a source-derived map from the effective carrier to a
nondegenerate Lorentzian metric candidate, with dimension and signature
derived rather than copied;
\item[\(\mathbf{R2}\)] a source-derived invariance and locality theorem strong
enough to classify the leading low-derivative scalar densities;
\item[\(\mathbf{R3}\)] source-derived or independently calibrated coefficient
signs and normalizations, never values fixed merely by target matching;
\item[\(\mathbf{R4}\)] a source-to-effective matter-response map producing a
typed target-side response tensor and its conservation identity;
\item[\(\mathbf{R5}\)] a regime and norm in which extra modes, higher
derivatives, boundary terms, and nonlocal corrections are controlled.
\end{enumerate}
Only after \(\mathbf{R1}\)--\(\mathbf{R5}\) are proved may variation of a
derived leading action be compared with an Einstein-type equation.  The
Einstein--Hilbert action, Einstein equations, and exact-GR benchmark are
read-only comparison targets, never premises of either theorem.

\section{Current antecedent audit}

\begin{proposition}[Current record does not instantiate the target]
The canonical P5--P7 record inspected by P8-T02 does not establish
\(\mathbf{A1}\)--\(\mathbf{A9}\).  Consequently it does not presently derive
\(\Seff\) or an Einstein-type field equation through the target above.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
P5 supplies controlled source dynamics and response data.  P6 supplies a
scoped source-extension metric-structure record but no lawful unscoped
effective geometry; its Gate B certificate records zero of eight positive
components.  P7 supplies the exact source-matter action, admitted finite
variation, response precursor, and componentwise physical balance by
protected constitutive postulate.  None of these records supplies the
source-derived effective base and carriers of \(\mathbf{A1}\), both maps of
\(\mathbf{A2}\), the local measure and derivative expansion of
\(\mathbf{A3}\)--\(\mathbf{A4}\), the descended operator and Helmholtz/gluing
data of \(\mathbf{A5}\)--\(\mathbf{A7}\), the effective symmetry and
conservation bridge of \(\mathbf{A8}\), or the effective boundary and
well-posedness data of \(\mathbf{A9}\).  The antecedent is therefore not
established, so the consequent may not be inferred.  This is a scoped
failure-of-derivation statement, not a theorem that no such extension can
exist.
\end{proof}

The exact control obstruction is
\texttt{OBST-P8T02-LOCAL-EFFECTIVE-ACTION-CLOSURE-DATA-ABSENT-001}.
It blocks present action and field-equation claims while leaving conservative
source-extension construction open.

\section{Disposition}

P8-T02 completes the requested formalization: all fields, maps, phase-space
or configuration variables, symmetries, locality data, derivative order,
boundary data, correction controls, and failure branches have explicit
places in the target.  The present source record does not fill those places.
The next honest step is a bounded P8-T03 candidate-construction packet that
attempts to construct the missing closure datum or proves one exact scoped
obstruction.  Canonical-ontology or source-law adoption remains human-gated.
