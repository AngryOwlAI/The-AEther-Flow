\section{Source-Side Objects}

\begin{definition}[Source matter-like response sector]
For a source package \(E\), a source matter-like response sector is an element
\[
  S \in \SrcMatterSec(E).
\]
The set \(\SrcMatterSec(E)\) is a source-side indexing family for response
syntax that a later packet may try to couple. It is not an empirical matter
sector, not a detector model, not a stress-energy carrier, not a matter action,
and not a target-space field.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Coupling-token system]
For each \(S\in\SrcMatterSec(E)\), let
\[
  \Tok_E(S)
\]
be the source coupling-token records for \(S\). A record
\(j\in\Tok_E(S)\) has positive source content only through the source
semantics map
\[
  \Sem_E:\coprod_{S\in\SrcMatterSec(E)}\Tok_E(S)\to \mathcal{I}_E,
\]
where \(\mathcal{I}_E\) is a source-defined interpretation domain. The map
\(\Sem_E\) must be stronger than bare labels because it must distinguish at
least two source cases, and weaker than stress-energy because its codomain is
not a tensor, action, detector calibration, empirical matter field, or target
metric datum.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Admissibility and transition data]
For \(j\in\Tok_E(S)\) and \(j'\in\Tok_E(S')\), write
\[
  j\leadsto_E j'
  \quad\text{iff}\quad
  \Adm_E(j,j')=\mathsf{true}.
\]
The predicate \(\Adm_E\) is a source-side admissibility relation. Source
transition data are written
\[
  \Trans_E(\tau,j)=\tau_\ast j
\]
for declared \(\tau\in T_{\mathrm{src}}(E)\). The compatibility kernel
\(\Compat_E\) may reference prior scoped \(\geff\) source-extension input only
as a bounded source-side compatibility record. It may not import target metric
semantics or expand \(\geff\) scope.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Bottom and obstruction labels]
Every ambiguous, degenerate, target-repaired, detector-repaired,
benchmark-repaired, adoption-repaired, process-repaired, or underdeclared
branch maps to
\[
  \BottomSrc
  \quad\text{or to a precise label}\quad
  \OBMC\mbox{-}\ast .
\]
These labels are diagnostic control objects. They are not global no-go
theorems unless a later Refuter packet proves that stronger claim under an
authorized scope.
\end{definition}

\section{Obligation Classes}

The criteria below are divided into three classes.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=*]
  \item[Necessary criteria.] A later source-side matter-coupling candidate
  must satisfy these before it may be treated as a positive candidate for the
  \texttt{matter\_coupling} burden.
  \item[Candidate sufficient profiles.] These are finite/local profiles that
  would make a candidate eligible for Candidate Constructor repair or
  strengthening review. They do not imply adoption.
  \item[Future physical bridge obligations.] These name later obligations
  needed for empirical matter semantics, stress-energy, actions, conservation
  laws, or field equations. They are not discharged here.
\end{description}

No criterion is discharged merely by being listed in this artifact. A later
packet must either prove the criterion for a declared candidate and source
family, return a precise obstruction, or fail closed.

\section{Candidate Sufficient Profiles}

\begin{criterion}[Finite/local source-coupling eligibility profile]
A candidate \(C_E\) is eligible for the next Candidate Constructor repair or
strengthening packet only if, for a declared finite/local family
\(\mathcal{F}_E\subseteq\SrcMatterSec(E)\), it supplies:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item definitions of \(\Tok_E(S)\), \(\Sem_E\), \(\Adm_E\), \(\Trans_E\),
  \(\Compat_E\), and bottom labels for every \(S\in\mathcal{F}_E\);
  \item evidence for NC-MC-001 through NC-MC-008 on \(\mathcal{F}_E\);
  \item an explicit list of undischarged future physical bridge obligations;
  \item a no-target-import certificate for every non-bottom branch; and
  \item a limitation record stating that eligibility is not adoption.
\end{enumerate}
\end{criterion}

\begin{criterion}[Candidate sufficient profile for repair]
A repair or strengthening packet may claim a candidate sufficient profile only
when it proves finite/local eligibility and identifies exactly which criteria
remain provisional. The profile is sufficient only for subsequent audit or
stress routing. It is not sufficient for coupling-law adoption or
matter-coupling derivation.
\end{criterion}

\begin{theorem}[Criteria-first eligibility theorem]
Let \(C_E\) be a later draft/control candidate for source-side matter coupling.
Suppose \(C_E\) satisfies NC-MC-001 through NC-MC-008 on a declared finite/local
family \(\mathcal{F}_E\), supplies a candidate sufficient profile, and records
all future physical bridge obligations as undischarged unless separately
proved or explicitly gated. Then \(C_E\) is eligible only for bounded
Candidate Constructor repair or strengthening, Smuggling Auditor review, or
Refuter stress routing:
\[
  C_E \in \Eligible(E,\mathcal{F}_E).
\]
This eligibility does not adopt a coupling law, derive or adopt matter
coupling, import stress-energy semantics, adopt \(\MetricData(E)\), change
scoped \(\geff\), derive Einstein equations, promote benchmark status, or
claim completed derivation.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
NC-MC-001 gives source-intrinsic interpretation and blocks bare-label
emptiness. NC-MC-002 gives a nontrivial admissibility predicate. NC-MC-003
requires source transition coherence or fail-closed bottom behavior. NC-MC-004
prevents hand-tailored sector rules. NC-MC-005 prevents target metric import
and preserves scoped \(\geff\). NC-MC-006 blocks stress-energy or Bianchi
substitution for source conservation. NC-MC-007 separates source syntax from
empirical matter semantics. NC-MC-008 closes forbidden repair branches. The
candidate sufficient profile then supplies a finite/local domain over which
these checks can be audited or stressed. None of these hypotheses supplies a
human-gated adoption verdict, empirical matter semantics, stress-energy
tensor, matter action, variational field equation, benchmark verdict, or
completed derivation. Therefore the only authorized conclusion is eligibility
for bounded follow-up routing.
\end{proof}
