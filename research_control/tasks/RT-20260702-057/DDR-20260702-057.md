# DDR-20260702-057: v15 P2-T01/P2-T02 Theorem Setup And Manifest

## Decision

Route one bounded v15 P2 packet to `ontology-formalizer@0.2.0` through a
task overlay. The packet implements P2-T01 and P2-T02 together: it locks the
narrow theorem target and creates the source-side matter-semantics object and
certificate manifest.

## Basis

`handoff-0509` records a clean `PASS_consistent` P1-T01 registry audit and
routes next to a bounded v15 P2 selected narrow source-side matter-semantics
equivalence theorem packet under explicit certificates. `handoff-0505` remains
the deferred scientific-route anchor for the same route.

The v15 plan permits P2-T01 and P2-T02 to be one bounded AgentJob under
current workflow rules. Combining them is lower risk than attempting the
P2-T03 theorem immediately because the exact theorem vocabulary, allowed
source evidence, certificate classes, and fail-closed branches must be fixed
before proof or obstruction work.
