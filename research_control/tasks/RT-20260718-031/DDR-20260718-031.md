# DDR-20260718-031: EqSrc Cross-Complex Reflection-Scope Repair

## Decision

Select `ontology-formalizer@0.2.0` through one task overlay for the bounded
`ontology-law-research-packet` required by `handoff-0755`. Produce one
proposal-only v3 repair of the exact chain-homology candidate. Preserve every
surviving theorem and claim boundary; do not adopt the law or proceed to
Refuter stress.

## Basis

RT-20260718-030 established
`OBST-EQSRC-INTRINSIC-DISCRIMINATOR-CHAIN-MAP-REFLECTION-001`. The v2
candidate defines relation predicates only for endomaps of one cycle space,
then applies them to a map between two chain packages. It also correctly
characterizes reflection by injectivity of an induced quotient map while a
later fail-closed branch incorrectly rejects every noninvertible chain map.

For a typed chain map \(F:E\to E'\), the induced map
\(H_1(F):H_1(E)\to H_1(E')\) exists and relation reflection holds exactly
when it is injective. The RT-030 four-state witness shows that whole-chain
invertibility is not necessary. The Ontology Formalizer is the narrowest role
authorized to make this proposal-only repair. A fresh Smuggling Auditor
review must follow before any Refuter stress.

## Selected Repair

For supplied packages \(E,E'\), define
\(\operatorname{RelPres}_{E,E'}(G)\) and
\(\operatorname{RelRefl}_{E,E'}(G)\) for a typed map
\(G:Z_1(E)\to Z_1(E')\). Prove that preservation is equivalent to a unique
induced quotient map, and that reflection under this factorization is
equivalent to injectivity of the induced map.

Specialize to a chain map \(F\), whose induced quotient map is \(H_1(F)\).
Replace the false noninvertibility failure rule with failure when
\(H_1(F)\) is not injective or its injectivity obligation is unmet. Retain
chain isomorphism as a sufficient certificate, not a necessary condition.

This repair does not derive the chain package, its differential, homology as a
physical discriminator, the chain-map category as physical covariance, or
boundary moves as physically admissible variations.
