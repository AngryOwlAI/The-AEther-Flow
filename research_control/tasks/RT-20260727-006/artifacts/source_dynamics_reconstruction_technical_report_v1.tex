\section{Compositional status-barrier theorem}

\begin{definition}[Source entailment at fixed description]
\label{def:entailment}
Let \(D\) be an admitted source description and let \(Y\) be an operational
output space.  We say that \(D\) entails \(y\in Y\) through a deterministic
source-derived rule if there is a single-valued rule \(F\), defined only from
admitted source data and declared equivalences, such that \(F(D)=y\).
If two admissible completions agree on \(D\) and its equivalence class but
require distinct values of an observable component of \(Y\), that component
is not entailed by \(D\).
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[P15T04-THM-FIXED-PACKAGE-COMPOSED-STATUS-BARRIER]
\label{thm:barrier}
Fix \(\mathcal P_{5:6}\) from Definition~\ref{def:package}.  Suppose:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item every deterministic P5 reconstruction under review factors through
  the sole amplitude coordinate \(A:U\to\mathbb R\);
  \item the positive local-geometry interface invokes the rank consequence
  only where at least two independently variable response directions are
  required at a source point;
  \item a lawful unscoped operational geometry must supply a unique
  Lorentzian cone up to a source-derived observable-preserving quotient,
  source naturality and gluing, uniform finite-variation robustness, clock
  and rod calibration, stable coarse-graining, and universal matter
  coupling;
  \item the registered P6 structural mismatch and same-premise separating
  witnesses are admissible mathematical controls for testing entailment,
  without adoption of any comparison completion.
\end{enumerate}
Then the unchanged package does not certify a deterministic map to a unique,
source-natural, robust, operationally calibrated unscoped Lorentzian
geometry.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By the P5 factorization theorem,
\(\rank D(B\circ A)\leq1\) for every deterministic reconstruction of the
stated class.  Such post-processing cannot create the independently variable
local response directions, restriction/gluing data, or a new source
discriminator absent from the fixed package.

Independently, each P6 witness pair agrees on the admitted source description
while differing in at least one required output: characteristic versus
quadratic cone, conformal class, transverse rod ratio, signature,
nondegeneracy, naturality, or inverse conditioning.  By
Definition~\ref{def:entailment}, a single-valued source-derived rule cannot
assign two distinct observable outputs to the same admitted description.
The relevant output is therefore not entailed unless an additional
source-derived discriminator or declared quotient identifies the difference.
The fixed package contains neither.

The rank result prevents deterministic repackaging of the P5 amplitude from
supplying the missing independent data, and the P6 witnesses establish
non-entailment even after the one-ray transport candidate is included.
Consequently the full required operational-geometry tuple is not certified
by \(\mathcal P_{5:6}\).
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact scope.}
Here ``composes'' means that two independently necessary certification
conditions fail; it does not mean literal composition of a P5 reconstruction
map with a P6 comparison form.  Theorem~\ref{thm:barrier} joins
already-registered conditional, structural, and same-premise separating
results for the unchanged package.  It does not prove that no
richer ontology can derive geometry, that every future reconstruction must
factor through one amplitude, or that a new source law, quotient theorem,
operational protocol, or robust variation class is impossible.  The correct
status is \texttt{blocked\_adoption\_open\_continuation}.
It is not a global no-go theorem.
