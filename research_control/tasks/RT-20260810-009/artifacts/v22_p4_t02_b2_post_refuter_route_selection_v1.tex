\section{What independent positive scaling preserves}

Let $S$ be a finite nonempty sector set and let
\[
 X=(\RR\setminus\{0\})^S,
 \qquad G=(\RR_{>0})^S.
\]
The group $G$ acts on $X$ componentwise.  Write
$\sgnmap:X\to\{-1,+1\}^S$ for the ordered sign map.

\begin{theorem}[Independent-positive-scale factorization]
For any set $Y$, a pointwise map $B:X\to Y$ is invariant under $G$ if and
only if there is a unique map $b:\{-1,+1\}^S\to Y$ such that
\[
 B=b\circ\sgnmap.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
If $r,r'\in X$ have the same ordered signs, then
$\lambda_s=r'_s/r_s>0$ for every $s$, so $r'=\lambda\cdot r$ for one
$\lambda\in G$.  Invariance makes $B$ constant on every sign fiber and hence
defines $b$ uniquely.  Conversely, positive coordinate scaling preserves all
signs, so every composite $b\circ\sgnmap$ is invariant.
\end{proof}

Thus $\Qsign$ is the maximal pointwise invariant under fully independent
positive retrivialization.  This does not prove that every lawful bridge is
pointwise or fully $G$-invariant.  Zero loci, derivatives, overlap transport,
refinement, and nonlocal data lie outside this theorem.  A broader
irrelevance-theorem packet would have to type those additional uses rather
than declare them irrelevant by premise.

\section{Common-line descent beyond literal equality}

Take a source cover $\{U_i\}$ and suppose sector $s$ has positive local
transition functions $g^s_{ij}$ satisfying its ordinary overlap cocycle.  A
common-line reduction consists of positive patch functions $a^s_i$ and one
positive common cocycle $g_{ij}$ satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:reduction}
 a^s_i g^s_{ij}=g_{ij}a^s_j
 \qquad\text{for every }s,i,j.
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Common-line cocycle-reduction criterion]
Equation~\eqref{eq:reduction} has a solution if and only if, relative to one
reference sector $t$, every ratio cocycle is a positive coboundary:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ratio}
 \frac{g^s_{ij}}{g^t_{ij}}
 =\frac{b^{st}_j}{b^{st}_i}
\end{equation}
for positive patch functions $b^{st}_i$, with the sector ratios chosen
coherently.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Divide~\eqref{eq:reduction} for sectors $s$ and $t$ and set
$b^{st}_i=a^s_i/a^t_i$ to obtain~\eqref{eq:ratio}.  Conversely, choose the
reference cocycle as the common cocycle, set $a^t_i=1$, and set
$a^s_i=b^{st}_i$.  Equation~\eqref{eq:ratio} rearranges to
\eqref{eq:reduction}.  Coherence of the sector ratios makes the result
independent of the chosen comparison chain.
\end{proof}

This criterion is broader than the RT008 fixed-trivialization condition.
Raw sector factors need not be literally equal; they must be reducible to one
common transition by declared positive cochains.  The theorem proves an
existence criterion only.  It does not select a reduction naturally from
current source ontology.

\section{One possible source mechanism}

Let $G_{\rm src}$ be a proposed source transition group or groupoid with
cocycle $h_{ij}$, and let
\[
 \chi:G_{\rm src}\longrightarrow\RR_{>0}
\]
be a positive character.

\begin{lemma}[Positive-character associated-line descent]
The functions $\lambda_{ij}=\chi(h_{ij})$ are positive, invertible, and obey
the overlap cocycle.  They therefore define an oriented associated real line
$L_\chi$.  A sector response line equipped with a source-natural intertwiner
into $L_\chi$ shares this common descent cocycle.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Positivity and invertibility follow from the codomain group.  The homomorphism
identity gives
$\lambda_{ij}\lambda_{jk}=\chi(h_{ij}h_{jk})=\chi(h_{ik})=\lambda_{ik}$.
The associated-line descent statement is then the ordinary intertwining
condition.
\end{proof}

Current ontology supplies neither $G_{\rm src}$, $\chi$, $L_\chi$, nor the
sector intertwiners.  The lemma makes a future construction target coherent;
it does not make that target available or natural.

\section{Descent and variation are independent}

\begin{lemma}[Logical independence]
A common positive response-line cocycle does not determine an admissible
variation law, and an admissible variation restriction does not determine a
common response-line cocycle.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For the first direction, put all response lines in one trivial line with
identity transitions but admit the full independent sector-triple tangent
space.  RT008's arbitrarily small transverse perturbation is then admissible
and changes rank two to rank three despite perfect descent.  For the other
direction, admit only the zero variation while changing local response
trivializations by unequal positive factors.  Variation is vacuously stable,
but $\Qray$ still changes and fails common-line descent.
\end{proof}

The future packet must therefore specify a source equation or generator
$E_{\rm src}:C_{\rm src}\to W_{\rm src}$ before response evaluation and derive
a nonvacuous variation quotient such as
\[
 A_{\rm src}(c)=
 \ker(D E_{\rm src}|_c)/\operatorname{im}(D\,\mathrm{gauge}_{\rm src}|_c).
\]
An empty, zero-only, diagonal-only, midpoint-preserving, or response-preserving
class is not a successful robustness law.
