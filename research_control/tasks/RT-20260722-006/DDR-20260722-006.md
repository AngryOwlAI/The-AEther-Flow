## Boundary

The corpus must include direct controls, aliases or euphemisms, fragments
split across records, positional arrays, equivalent mathematical forms,
misleading no-target certificates, validator-as-proof assertions, and
prompt-level interpretation attacks. Benign source-only controls must remain
accepted. The comparison must report confusion counts and false-positive and
false-negative rates for both the current lexical linter and the bounded
semantic probe.

The probe is adversarial test tooling only. Passing the corpus does not prove
that all semantic target imports are detected, does not replace blind or human
review, and does not establish scientific correctness, source-law adoption,
ontology status, `M_src`, `g_eff`, matter coupling, Einstein equations,
benchmark promotion, publication, or a completed derivation.

bounded semantic probe, a benign control becomes a semantic false positive,

validation or checkpoint gate fails.
