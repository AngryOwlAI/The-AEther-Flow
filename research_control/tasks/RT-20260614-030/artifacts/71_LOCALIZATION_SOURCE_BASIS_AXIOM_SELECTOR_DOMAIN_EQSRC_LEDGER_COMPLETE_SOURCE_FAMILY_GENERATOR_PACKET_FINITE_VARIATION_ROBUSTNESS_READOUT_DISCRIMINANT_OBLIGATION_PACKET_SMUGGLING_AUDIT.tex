\begin{abstract}
This draft/control Smuggling Auditor artifact audits the
\texttt{RT-20260614-029} finite-variation robustness and
readout-discriminant obligation packet. The audit checks whether the
source-side adverse variation suite, representative-independence
burden, boundary-invariance burden, quotient-composition and
inverse-provenance burdens, readout minimality and discriminating-power
criteria, negative-witness coverage criteria, and local failure
conditions obtain their apparent force by importing target metric,
target proper time, target locality, observer protocol, benchmark rank,
candidate behavior, validation status, generated visibility, registry
status, handoff status, or authority labels. The audit records a local
conditional \(\Pass\) for the obligation packet as a source-pure
draft/control audit object only. Finite-variation robustness remains in
controlled \(\Pause\), \(\NegLC\) remains in \(\Repair\), and
representative independence, boundary invariance, quotient-composition
robustness, inverse provenance, readout minimality, and readout
discriminating power remain undischarged. The result does not establish
bridge relevance, does not discharge general \(\EqSrc\), \(\RetainH\),
or \(\GenH\), does not authorize candidate reconstruction, does not
edit canonical ontology, does not promote benchmark status, does not
request Gate Chair review, does not permit completed-derivation
language, and does not globally reject the research program.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and Audit Question}

This audit responds to \texttt{handoff-0071}. The inspected packet is
\texttt{70\_LOCALIZATION\_SOURCE\_BASIS\_AXIOM\_SELECTOR\_DOMAIN\_EQSRC\_LEDGER\_COMPLETE\_SOURCE\_FAMILY\_GENERATOR\_PACKET\_FINITE\_VARIATION\_ROBUSTNESS\_READOUT\_DISCRIMINANT\_OBLIGATION\_PACKET.tex}.
That packet converts the \texttt{RT-20260614-028} Refuter robustness
pause into a source-side obligation suite for the local \(\EqLC\)
finite witness line.

The audit question is:
\[
    \text{Does the obligation suite obtain its control usefulness by
    importing target primitives or authority labels?}
\]

The answer is no under the stated reading that the packet is an
obligation suite, not discharged robustness evidence. The pass is
conditional and local: the packet is a useful source-side control
object and audit target, not a bridge theorem or a promotion packet.

\section{Screen Ledger}

\begin{screen}[Variation admissibility]
The packet defines \(\VarLC\) as a finite source-side adverse variation
suite and explicitly rejects variation selection by target metric,
proper time, locality, observer protocol, benchmark convenience,
candidate behavior, downstream success, validation status, registry
status, generated visibility, handoff status, or authority label. The
audit finds no local hidden target primitive in the admissibility rule.

The pass is narrow. The list of variation families is a testing
interface. It does not prove that the listed families are exhaustive,
that every member has been supplied, or that robustness has been
discharged.
\end{screen}

\begin{screen}[Representative-equivalence laundering]
The packet introduces \(\Theta_Q\) only as a future source-side
equivalence relation on quotient representatives and requires its
definition, lifted readout relation or conservative exact equality,
composition preservation, and inverse-provenance preservation. The
audit finds this structure protective because representative
equivalence is treated as an obligation rather than as available
evidence.

Any later use of \(\Theta_Q\) to relax representative equality without
a source-only definition and preservation proof returns \(\Block\).
Any selection of \(\Theta_Q\) because it improves target behavior,
benchmark status, candidate performance, or bridge convenience returns
target import.
\end{screen}

\begin{screen}[Boundary-equivalence laundering]
The packet introduces \(\Theta_{\partial}\) only as a future
source-side equivalence relation on ordered boundary anchors and
requires boundary transport, certificate preservation, inverse
recovery, and an explicit statement of exact versus quotiented readout.
The audit finds no local boundary-equivalence laundering because the
packet does not treat boundary relaxation as already justified.

The risk remains live. A later boundary quotient that borrows target
geometry, observer protocol, causal structure, clock behavior, matter
coupling, benchmark rank, or candidate behavior returns \(\Block\).
\end{screen}

\begin{screen}[Quotient composition and inverse provenance]
The packet requires a source-side finite category or composition ledger
for \(\CompLC\) and enough source-ledger information for \(\InvLC\) to
recover blockers, influence records, quotient representatives,
boundary anchors, and contraction labels after refinement and
composition. The audit finds no local import of target structure in
these obligations.

The obligations remain undischarged. Naming a composition ledger is not
the same as supplying identities, associativity, representative
matching, or inverse history. Any downstream use before those data are
provided returns \(\Pause\) or \(\Repair\), not \(\Pass\).
\end{screen}

\begin{screen}[Readout minimality and discriminating power]
The packet separates \(\MinLC\) from \(\DiscLC\). This separation is
protective: overcomplete readouts and under-discriminating readouts are
different failure modes. The audit finds no local readout-discriminant
overclaim because the packet states that equality of readout values is
not a converse for \(\EqLC\) unless the required source-side
discriminant burden is supplied.

The discriminant burden is not discharged. A future claim that
\(\ReadLC\) equality rules out all adverse finite variations without a
finite row-by-row source-side discriminant table returns \(\Block\).
Keeping a coordinate only because it helps downstream target behavior
or benchmark success returns target import.
\end{screen}

\begin{screen}[Negative-witness coverage]
The packet records that the existing near-miss negative witness covers
proxy weakening and blocker erasure but not influence provenance swaps,
representative substitution, noncomposing quotient refinement, boundary
perturbation, readout-coordinate relaxation, readout-coordinate
deletion, or endpoint-equal provenance shifts. The current status
\(S_{\mathrm{lc}}(\NegLC)=\Repair\) is therefore appropriate.

The audit finds no negative-suite laundering because incompleteness is
stated directly. Any future robustness claim that treats the existing
near-miss witness as exhaustive returns \(\Block\).
\end{screen}

\begin{screen}[Failure-condition ambiguity]
The packet defines local \(\Pass\), \(\Pause\), \(\Repair\), and
\(\Kill\) criteria for named obligations. The audit finds the criteria
mostly protective because they separate open burdens from repairable
defects and local branch failure.

The ambiguity is local-necessity scope. A \(\Kill\) result is allowed
only after the named burden is shown necessary for the declared source
objective and all source-only repairs are exhausted. It cannot be
inflated into global theory rejection or used to reject unrelated
branches.
\end{screen}

\begin{screen}[Authority labels and generated surfaces]
The inspected packet uses draft/control status, task identifiers,
validation status, registration, generated wiki visibility, handoff
state, and role labels as process metadata only. The audit finds no
positive use of those labels as mathematical or physical evidence.

Any inference from validation, registration, generated visibility,
handoff status, task numbering, or role label to canonical \(\EqSrc\),
\(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), bridge readiness, candidate readiness,
benchmark promotion, Gate Chair approval, or completed derivation
returns \(\Block\).
\end{screen}

\begin{screen}[Bridge-ladder separation]
The packet repeatedly states that source-side obligation criteria are
not downstream bridge claims. The audit finds this separation
protective. Finite-variation robustness, if later discharged, would
still not by itself establish \(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), clock behavior,
causal structure, matter coupling, candidate reconstruction, or
benchmark recovery.

Collapsing source-side robustness, readout adequacy, bridge relevance,
candidate reconstruction, and benchmark recovery into one ladder step
returns \(\Block\).
\end{screen}

\section{Audit Findings}

\begin{auditfinding}[No local hidden target primitive import detected]
Inside the inspected obligation packet, admissible variations and
future repairs are constrained to source-ledger data. The audit does
not detect target metric, target proper time, target locality, observer
protocol, benchmark rank, candidate behavior, downstream success, or
authority metadata as a positive primitive.
\end{auditfinding}

\begin{auditfinding}[Equivalence relaxations remain obligations]
Representative equivalence and boundary equivalence are not accepted as
available repairs. They are future source-side obligations requiring
definitions, transport or preservation laws, and inverse-provenance
support.
\end{auditfinding}

\begin{auditfinding}[Readout discriminating power remains open]
The packet correctly separates readout minimality from readout
discriminating power and does not treat \(\ReadLC\) equality as a
converse for \(\EqLC\). The discriminant table and negative controls
remain future burdens.
\end{auditfinding}

\begin{auditfinding}[Obligations are not robustness evidence]
The packet supplies criteria for later source-side discharge or local
failure. Reading those criteria as completed finite-variation
robustness would be authority laundering and returns \(\Block\).
\end{auditfinding}

high-risk surfaces. They must be selected by source-side equivalence
criteria and not by target success or bridge convenience.
\end{residualrisk}

\begin{residualrisk}[Composition and inverse-history pressure]
The composition ledger and inverse-provenance data are still absent.
Endpoint readout equality can miss source history unless those data are
supplied or proved irrelevant.
\end{residualrisk}

\begin{residualrisk}[Readout discriminant pressure]
The finite discriminant table has not been built. A future readout
repair can fail by under-including inverse provenance or by retaining
overcomplete coordinates for downstream reasons.
\end{residualrisk}

\begin{residualrisk}[Negative-suite incompleteness]
Negative-witness coverage remains incomplete. \(\NegLC\) remains in
\(\Repair\) until missing variation-family members are supplied or
source-side irrelevance is proved.
\end{residualrisk}

\begin{residualrisk}[Local kill inflation]
The failure criteria can only close the local branch under the declared
source objective. They do not authorize global theory rejection or
promotion of an alternative branch.
\end{residualrisk}

\begin{residualrisk}[Bridge-ladder overread]
Source-side obligations, source-side robustness, bridge relevance,
\(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), candidate reconstruction, and benchmark
recovery remain distinct burdens. Collapsing them is still the primary
scientific-control risk.
\end{residualrisk}
