## Decision

Create `RT-20260705-057` and bind exactly one AgentJob,
`AJ-RT-20260705-057-001`, to
`project-control-maintainer@0.2.0` through a task overlay.

## Reasoning

The latest tracked handoff, `handoff-0629`, routes immediate v17 continuation
to P3-T04: renderer update for positive-first status cards. The implementation
plan names a tooling-engineer role family, but the registered role available
for project-control renderer and validation machinery is
`project-control-maintainer@0.2.0`. That role is narrower than a scientific
role and is sufficient for renderer code, generated control artifacts, focused
tests, and registry receipts.

This decision does not authorize canonical ontology edits, science-source
edits, source-law adoption, detector semantics, coupling-law adoption, matter
coupling, stress-energy semantics, matter action, Einstein equations,
benchmark promotion, Gate Chair verdicts, or completed-derivation claims.
