# DDR-20260627-003: Current Ontology Title-Page Correction

## Current Objective

Implement the user's explicit follow-up source-format correction for the 8
current managed ontology documents under `ontology/tex` and their managed PDF
derivatives under `ontology/pdfs`.

This supersedes no scientific content from `DDR-20260627-002`. It is a narrower
title-page presentation correction under a new one-time authorization.

## Role-Fit Matrix

| Candidate role | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ontology-source-format-retrofit` | Accepted | A one-job provisional role is the least broad mechanism for the current-only source-format correction. It expires after `AJ-RT-20260627-003-001`. |
| Project-Control Maintainer | Rejected | It can maintain control records and registries but cannot directly perform canonical ontology TeX or PDF derivative source-format writes. |
| Documentation Curator | Rejected | The packet is not a source-backed explanatory Markdown or HTML publication change. |
| Physics roles | Rejected | The work is formatting and attribution presentation only; no scientific derivation or claim-gate decision is being made. |

## Decision

Create and execute one human-gated provisional AgentJob:
`AJ-RT-20260627-003-001`.

The selected provisional role may:

- update `tex_shared/aether_flow_apa_frontmatter.tex` for the requested title
  font, creator line, and project-title-line removal;
- mechanically update the 8 current `ontology/tex/*.tex` title declarations to
  use `Ontology: <existing article label>`;
- rebuild only the corresponding current `ontology/pdfs/*.pdf` derivatives
  through the managed PDF derivative script;
- refresh generated registries and wiki metadata through the normal bootstrap
  path.

The selected provisional role may not:

- edit article body prose;
- edit `legacy_ontology/**`;
- edit historical `research_control/tasks/**` TeX or PDF artifacts;
- add `GR Derivation:` to any current file;
- change physics claims, ontology status, benchmark status, Gate Chair status,
  or derivational conclusions;
- register a permanent role or widen future source-format authority.
