nontrivial boundary transport, quotient composition beyond exact
intermediate matching, concrete inverse-token witnesses, readout

reconstruction, does not edit canonical ontology, does not promote
benchmark status, does not request Gate Chair review, does not permit
completed-derivation language, and does not globally reject the
research program.
\end{abstract}

ontology.

\section{Screen Ledger}

\begin{screen}[Hidden target import]
The repair packet defines representative records \(R(X)\), boundary
records \(B(X)\), exact composition records, inverse-provenance
tokens, and audit readout coordinates entirely as source-ledger fields.
It explicitly excludes target metric, target proper time, target
locality, observer protocol, benchmark rank, candidate behavior,
validation status, registry status, generated visibility, handoff
status, and authority labels from the mathematical record.

The audit finds no local hidden target primitive in the supplied
\(\Exact\) baselines. The pass is narrow: exact tuple equality is
source-pure precisely because it is conservative, not because it
settles nontrivial quotient invariance.
\end{screen}

\begin{screen}[Representative-equivalence overread]
The packet distinguishes \(\Theta_Q^{=}\) from \(\Theta_Q^{+}\).
\(\Theta_Q^{=}\) is exact equality of representative records and can
support only the identity-policy branch of \(\RepLC\). The packet
keeps \(\Theta_Q^{+}\) bracketed until a source-only equivalence
definition, lifted readout rule, certificate-composition preservation,
inverse-provenance preservation, and negative witness are supplied.

The audit finds this distinction protective. Any later use of
\(\Theta_Q^{=}\) as evidence for nontrivial representative
independence, or any choice of \(\Theta_Q^{+}\) because it improves
target behavior, bridge convenience, benchmark rank, or candidate
performance, returns \(\Block\).
\end{screen}

\begin{screen}[Boundary-equivalence overread]
The packet defines \(\Theta_{\partial}^{=}\) as exact equality of
boundary records and treats nontrivial boundary relaxation
\(\Theta_{\partial}^{+}\) as unsupplied. Exact identity transport is
source-pure because it copies the same ordered boundary tuple through
defined certificate steps.

The audit finds no local target geometry import in the exact boundary
baseline. The open risk is status inflation: exact boundary equality
does not prove boundary robustness, boundary quotient invariance,
physical locality, clock behavior, causal structure, or matter
coupling.
\end{screen}

\begin{screen}[Composition-ledger authority laundering]
The packet supplies a partial exact ledger
\(\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{lc}}^{=}\) whose composition is defined only
when intermediate source objects, representative records, and boundary
records match exactly. Associativity is asserted only for triples whose
matching checks already pass.

The audit finds the ledger locally source-pure. It also finds that
the ledger is not a general composition theorem. Any later use of the
partial ledger to justify nonmatching quotient composition, omitted
intermediate histories, endpoint-equal provenance shifts, or bridge
composition returns \(\Block\).
\end{screen}

\begin{screen}[Inverse-provenance overclaim]
The inspected packet requires inverse tokens to recover blockers,
influence records, quotient representatives, boundary anchors, and
contraction histories from endpoint and provenance data. This is a
requirement, not a supplied finite witness family.

The audit finds no positive target import in the requirement. The
requirement remains in \(\Repair\) until concrete source-side tokens
or source-side irrelevance proofs are supplied. Treating the token
schema as inverse-provenance discharge returns \(\Block\).
\end{screen}

\begin{screen}[Audit-readout substitution]
\(\ReadAudit\) is introduced as a diagnostic repair readout, not as a
replacement for canonical \(\ReadLC\), bridge evidence, or a
benchmark-facing observable. The finite discriminant table is a repair
design that states what coordinates must be tested.

The audit finds this separation protective. Substituting
\(\ReadAudit\) for \(\ReadLC\), using audit coordinates as empirical
observables, or treating the table as proof of \(\MinLC\) or
\(\DiscLC\) returns \(\Block\).
\end{screen}

\begin{screen}[Schematic negative-witness overclaim]
The packet supplies schematic rows for influence shifts,
representative substitution, nonmatching composition, boundary
perturbation, readout-coordinate deletion, readout-coordinate
relaxation, and endpoint-equal provenance shifts. It states that each
row still requires a finite \(\ObjLC\) object pair or certificate-step
family checked against \(\CertLC\).

The audit finds no local overclaim because schematic status is stated
directly. Any future robustness claim that treats schemas as completed
\(\NegLC\) coverage, or as a substitute for concrete missing-family
witnesses, returns \(\Block\).
\end{screen}

\begin{screen}[Local status inflation]
The local status ledger marks exact representative equality, exact
boundary equality, and exact defined composition as \(\Pass\) only for
their exact branches. It leaves nontrivial quotient branches,
concrete inverse tokens, readout minimality, readout discriminating
power, and negative-witness instantiation in \(\Pause\) or
\(\Repair\).

The audit finds the status ledger acceptable only with that branch
restriction. A summary that says ``\(\RepLC\) passed'' without
``for \(\Theta_Q^{=}\) only'', or ``\(\BndLC\) passed'' without
``for \(\Theta_{\partial}^{=}\) only'', launders exact-control status
into robustness status and returns \(\Block\).
\end{screen}

\begin{screen}[Authority labels and generated surfaces]
The inspected packet uses task identifiers, role names, registration,
validation, generated wiki visibility, and handoff state as process
metadata only. The audit finds no local inference from those labels to
mathematical or physical evidence.

Any inference from validation, registration, generated visibility,
handoff status, task number, or role identity to canonical \(\EqSrc\),
\(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), bridge readiness, candidate readiness,
benchmark promotion, Gate Chair approval, completed derivation, or
global rejection returns \(\Block\).
\end{screen}

\begin{screen}[Bridge-ladder collapse]
The packet repeatedly separates source-control exact interfaces from
finite-variation robustness, bridge relevance, candidate
reconstruction, and benchmark recovery. The audit finds that separation
protective.

Even if future tasks instantiate every missing witness and discharge
the local finite-variation suite, that would still not by itself prove
\(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), clock behavior, causal structure, matter
coupling, candidate reconstruction, or benchmark recovery. Collapsing
these steps returns \(\Block\).
\end{screen}

\section{Audit Findings}

\begin{auditfinding}[No local hidden target primitive import detected]
Inside the inspected repair packet, the supplied exact baselines and
ledger rules are expressed as source-record equality, ordered source
composition, and source-ledger token requirements. The audit does not
detect target metric, target proper time, target locality, observer
protocol, benchmark rank, candidate behavior, downstream success, or
authority metadata as a positive primitive.
\end{auditfinding}

\begin{auditfinding}[Exact baselines are control devices only]
The exact representative and exact boundary baselines are legitimate
source-control devices. They do not supply nontrivial quotient
relations, robustness under variation, or bridge-relevant invariance.
\end{auditfinding}

\begin{auditfinding}[Composition is partial and exact]
The composition ledger conditionally passes as a partial exact ledger.
It does not repair nonmatching quotient composition, omitted histories,
endpoint-equal provenance shifts, or any downstream bridge
composition.
\end{auditfinding}

\begin{auditfinding}[Audit readout and negative rows remain repair scaffolds]
\(\ReadAudit\) and the schematic negative rows are useful repair
scaffolds. They are not canonical readout replacement, readout
minimality proof, readout discriminating-power proof, or completed
negative-witness coverage.
\end{auditfinding}

packet as a pass for \(\RepLC\), \(\BndLC\), or \(\CompLC\) without
the exact-branch restriction, the transaction will overstate the
scientific result.

\begin{residualrisk}[Quotient design pressure]
Future \(\Theta_Q^{+}\) and \(\Theta_{\partial}^{+}\) definitions
remain high-risk surfaces. They must be selected by source-side
equivalence and transport criteria, not by target success or bridge
convenience.
\end{residualrisk}

\begin{residualrisk}[Inverse-token and readout pressure]
Concrete inverse-token witnesses and coordinate-removal tests are still
absent. Endpoint equality and audit-table completeness can conceal
source-history losses until those finite witnesses are built and
tested.
\end{residualrisk}

\begin{residualrisk}[Negative-suite incompleteness]
The schematic negative suite is not completed coverage. \(\NegLC\)
remains in \(\Repair\) until the missing rows are instantiated or
source-side irrelevance is proved.
\end{residualrisk}

Source-control repair, source-purity audit, finite-variation
robustness, bridge relevance, \(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), candidate
reconstruction, and benchmark recovery remain distinct burdens. The
audit does not shorten that ladder.
\end{residualrisk}

into a robustness overclaim.
