\section{Metric-equivalence decision test}

\begin{definition}[Metric-bearing and metric-completing data]
Let \(D\) be typed data on the source side. It is \emph{metric-bearing} when
a recovery map depending on no new physical input determines a Lorentzian
conformal class \([g(D)]\). It is \emph{metric-completing} when it also
determines a representative metric, including scale. If \(D\) is assumed
rather than derived from independently sourced fields and laws, a construction
from \(D\) is postulation or reconstruction rather than primitive-free
emergence.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Metric-recoverability classification]
Under the types stated below:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
  \item an invertible coframe \(e^I{}_a\) together with a fixed internal
  Lorentzian form \(\eta_{IJ}\) is metric-completing;
  \item a nondegenerate symmetric Lorentzian quadratic form is
  metric-completing, up to the declared tangent/cotangent variance convention;
  \item a gauge- and constraint-reduced universal characteristic polynomial
  whose only physical characteristic factor is a nondegenerate Lorentzian
  quadratic form, considered up to positive multiplication, is
  metric-bearing; and
  \item a constitutive tensor is metric-bearing only when separately proved
  closure and no-birefringence hypotheses reduce its Fresnel polynomial to the
  square of one such Lorentzian quadratic factor.
\end{enumerate}
Consequently, assuming any of these metric-bearing data without an independent
source derivation forbids an emergence classification.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For (a), the recovery map is explicit:
\[
  g_{ab}=\eta_{IJ}e^I{}_a e^J{}_b.
\]
Invertibility of the coframe and Lorentzian signature of \(\eta\) make
\(g\) a Lorentzian metric. For (b), the quadratic form is already a metric or
inverse metric after fixing variance. For (c), the null set of one
nondegenerate Lorentzian quadratic factor determines its cone and hence its
conformal class; multiplying the polynomial by a positive scalar does not
change that set. For (d), the stated constitutive hypotheses give
\[
  \mathcal P(k)=\bigl(Q^{ab}k_a k_b\bigr)^2,
\]
so the same argument recovers \([Q^{-1}]\). Each recovery uses no further
physical input. Therefore an assumed datum already contains the recovered
metric content, and changing its name cannot convert postulation into
emergence.
\end{proof}

Generic constitutive tensors, higher-degree or multicone polynomials,
sector-dependent cones, degenerate forms, and unreduced symbols fail closed.
They do not establish one physical Lorentzian cone.

\subsection{Volume is not a conformal metric}

\begin{proposition}[Same-volume nonconformal counterfamily]
On a fixed real four-dimensional vector space with ordered basis, define
\[
 g_s=\operatorname{diag}(-e^{2s},e^{-2s},1,1),\qquad s\in\mathbb R.
\]
Every \(g_s\) is Lorentzian and has determinant \(-1\), hence the same
oriented volume density. Under the identity comparison,
\(g_s\) and \(g_r\) are conformally equivalent only when \(s=r\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The determinant is
\((-e^{2s})(e^{-2s})(1)(1)=-1\). If
\(g_s=\Omega^2g_r\) for a positive scalar \(\Omega\), comparison of the
third diagonal entry gives \(\Omega^2=1\). Comparison of the first entry then
gives \(e^{2s}=e^{2r}\), hence \(s=r\). Thus one volume density does not
determine a conformal class.
\end{proof}

This is a pointwise linear-algebra counterfamily, not a physical spacetime
model or global impossibility theorem.

\begin{theorem}[Volume completes conformal scale]
Let \([h]\) be a Lorentzian conformal class on an \(n\)-dimensional vector
bundle and let \(\omega\) be a positive orientation-compatible volume density.
For any representative \(h\), there is a unique positive \(\Omega\) such that
\(g=\Omega^2h\) has volume density \(\omega\). It is
\[
  \Omega=\left(\frac{\omega}{\operatorname{vol}_h}\right)^{1/n}.
\]
In four dimensions the exponent is \(1/4\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
In dimension \(n\), conformal rescaling gives
\(\det(\Omega^2h)=\Omega^{2n}\det h\), so
\(\operatorname{vol}_{\Omega^2h}=\Omega^n\operatorname{vol}_h\).
The displayed positive factor is therefore necessary and sufficient, and
positivity makes it unique.
\end{proof}

Thus volume alone is not metric-equivalent, but volume plus an assumed
conformal class is metric-completing and carries the corresponding assumption
cost.
