\section{Conditional quantitative certificate}

Suppose a regular local presentation of \(K\) is
\[
 K=\{x:\rho_i(x)\geq0,\ i=1,\dots,m\}.
\]
At a boundary point, write \(I(x)=\{i:\rho_i(x)=0\}\).  Assume that the
\(\rho_i\) only present an independently sourced \(K\); they do not create
its authority.

\begin{theorem}[Finite conormal stability certificate]
\label{thm:certificate}
Assume the declared solution class has the regularity needed for the
first-exit argument.  If there are source-provenanced numbers
\(\alpha_i(x)>\delta_i(x)\geq0\) such that, for every relevant boundary
point, every \(F\in\mathcal A\), every \(E\in\Delta(x)\), and every active
face \(i\in I(x)\),
\begin{align}
 d\rho_i(x)[F(x)] &\geq \alpha_i(x), \\
 |d\rho_i(x)[E]| &\leq \delta_i(x),
\end{align}
then
\[
 d\rho_i(x)[F(x)+E]\geq\alpha_i(x)-\delta_i(x)>0.
\]
Consequently the perturbed boundary-velocity set lies in the strict inward
tangent sector represented by the active faces, and \(K\) is forward
invariant on the declared solution domain.  Replacing each active certificate
by a positive regraduation \(\widetilde\rho_i=c_i\rho_i\), \(c_i>0\) at the
boundary, preserves every sign and the resulting certificate orbit.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Linearity of the differential gives
\[
 d\rho_i[F+E]
 =d\rho_i[F]+d\rho_i[E]
 \geq \alpha_i-|d\rho_i[E]|
 \geq \alpha_i-\delta_i>0.
\]
At any first exit from \(K\), at least one active defining function would
have a nonpositive forward derivative, contradicting the strict inequality.
For positive regraduation, on \(\rho_i=0\) one has
\(d(c_i\rho_i)=c_i,d\rho_i\); hence the sign is unchanged.  The theorem is
conditional on the declared regularity, coverage of all boundary strata, and
independent source provenance of the data.
\end{proof}

The exact rational fixture used for conformance takes the positive orthant in
three algebraic source coordinates, nominal boundary velocities
\((2,3,5)\), and admitted conormal variation bounds \((1,1,2)\).  The
residual margins are \((1,2,3)\).  This fixture checks the theorem's algebra;
it is not evidence that the project source ontology supplies the datum.

\section{Balanced-normal-variation obstruction}

\begin{proposition}[Zero margin plus balanced normal variation]
\label{prop:balanced}
Let \(x\in\partial K\) admit a nonzero outward conormal \(\nu\).  If the
nominal law is tangent, \(\nu(F(x))=0\), and the independently admitted
variation set contains \(E\) and \(-E\) with \(\nu(E)\neq0\), then strict
robust invariance fails at \(x\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The two admitted normal velocities are
\[
 \nu(F+E)=\nu(E),\qquad
 \nu(F-E)=-\nu(E).
\]
They have opposite nonzero signs, so at least one points outside the strict
inward tangent sector.  No target norm or magnitude is needed.
\end{proof}

This proposition is the full-law-space analogue of the RT005 boundary-sign
bifurcation, but it neither reconstructs nor repairs the signed-cubic model.
It states the exact future fail branch: a zero-margin law cannot be called
robust while its source-derived variation class contains both normal signs.
