\section{Marked-Presentation Separation}

\(\Dmbeta\). Let \(\beta_1\) and \(\beta_2\) be incompatible branch-origin
markings on \(D_m\). Then the corresponding candidates
\(\Witness(D_m^{\beta_1})\) and \(\Witness(D_m^{\beta_2})\) are not two
values of a unique unmarked witness law on \(D_m\). They are candidates on
distinct marked source presentations.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}

alone; it is the marked presentation \(D_m^\beta\). If \(\beta_1\) and
\(\beta_2\) are incompatible, the source presentations differ in the explicit
branch-origin component. The candidate therefore does not choose between
source-automorphic middle branches of an unmarked reconvergent package.
Forgetting \(\beta\) removes the domain condition and returns \(\BottomSrc\)
or preserves the prior branch-discriminator obstruction.
\end{proof}
