## Selected Role

Use one Validator Engineer task overlay. It may add the planned aggregate
receipt module, one focused regression module, task-local acceptance evidence,
and governed derivatives. The implementation is necessarily larger than a
single local conditional because the canonical P6-T08 contract requires six
distinct terminal scenarios, obligation provenance, deterministic ordering,
hashed child evidence, bounded rendering, rollback reporting, and explicit
authority fields. The boundary remains only two implementation files.

## Claim Boundary

The packet may report transaction selection, execution, cache, obligation,
artifact, performance, residue, rollback, and tree-identity evidence as
operational project-system data. It may not execute gates, alter their status,
replace legacy validation authority, treat skipped work as satisfying an
applicable obligation, ingest raw logs into the compact receipt, or treat
validation PASS as scientific evidence.

## Validation

PASS requires focused complete, partial, failed, cancelled, cache-hit, rollback,
skipped-obligation, deterministic-order, bounded-summary, and raw-log-reference
tests; task-local legacy/planner shadow parity evidence for the aggregate
status rules; documentation impact, research control, signals, whitespace,
memory, and governed checkpoint PASS.
