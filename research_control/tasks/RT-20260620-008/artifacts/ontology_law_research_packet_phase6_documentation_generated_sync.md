# Ontology-Law Research Packet Phase 6 Documentation And Generated Sync

## Analysis

Phase 6 implements the closing documentation and generated-sync step from
`implementations_plans/ontology_law_research_packet_routing_implementation_plan.md`.
The route is already installed across active control guidance and schemas. The
remaining documentation risk is interpretive: a future operator might treat
validator and fixture PASS evidence as proof that a candidate source-side law
is true or adopted.

This packet therefore adds one source clarification to
`research_control/README.md`. The clarification states that ontology-law route
validator and fixture evidence is operational receipt evidence only. It can
show that a completion preserved route labels, blocked adoption, exact-GR
recovery obligations, no-target-import audit scope, and human-gate boundaries.
It cannot prove mathematical correctness, source completeness, exact-GR
sufficiency, or adoption.

## Change Made

`research_control/README.md` now states that validator and fixture evidence for
`ontology-law-research-packet` is receipt evidence only. The text explicitly
separates route-label and boundary compliance from mathematical correctness,
source completeness, exact-GR sufficiency, and adoption.

Because `research_control/README.md` is a registered source inspected by older
memory-preflight receipts, the packet also refreshes historical
`MD-README-RESEARCH-CONTROL` `source_hash` receipts to the current registered
hash. This is receipt maintenance only; it does not change prior task
verdicts, role outputs, or scientific status.

## Boundary Preserved

This packet does not:

- edit canonical ontology TeX;
- modify benchmark sources;
- adopt `AtlasGlue_src^+`, `AtlasGlueDisc_src^+`, `M_src`, `g_eff`, matter
  coupling, or Einstein equations;
- issue a Gate Chair verdict;
- change validator behavior;
- change checkpoint behavior;
- create a physics research artifact;
- register a new permanent ontology-law role; or

  registry sidecars.
