## Reasoning

The active handoff `handoff-0274` requires a selector after the Gate Chair
accepted the criteria-aligned candidate only as evidence/precondition. The
post-evidence options include repair, scoped obstruction, route freeze,
broader protected adoption review, bounded recovery-criteria packet, or
another non-promotional route.

Repair is not selected because the criteria-aligned candidate has already been
constructed, audited, stress-survived, and accepted only as scoped evidence.
Scoped obstruction is not selected because no new failure or contradiction is
recorded by the accepted evidence status. Route freeze is not selected because
a constructive non-promotional recovery-bridge criteria packet remains
available. Broader protected adoption review is not selected because accepted
evidence/precondition status is not adoption readiness and the bridge from
candidate evidence to matter-coupling recovery obligations has not been
formalized.
