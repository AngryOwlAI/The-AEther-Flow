## Rationale

propagation or downstream physics routes. `handoff-0453` and
`current_frontier.md` already expose the layered validation and authorization
fields introduced by P4-T02 and clarified by P4-T03. Because handoffs are
immutable after creation, the correct P4-T04 action is a superseding
task-local backfill receipt that audits the latest active handoff and current
frontier, then regenerates current-frontier state from a new handoff that names
the receipt.

downstream physics promotion, or completed-derivation claims.
