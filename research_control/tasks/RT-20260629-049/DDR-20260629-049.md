# Director Decision Record: DDR-20260629-049

## Decision

Execute P5-T01 as one bounded `theoretical-continuation-selector@0.1.0`
task overlay.

The selected next objective is P5-T02: matter-semantics target formalization.
The selected target is a source-side matter-sector discriminator obstruction
target, with explicit `NoStressEnergyImport_v1(E)`,
`NoMatterActionImport_v1(E)`, and `NoDetectorImport_v1(E)` obligations. The
next packet must formalize the target and failure conditions only. It must not
adopt detector semantics, stress-energy semantics, a stress-energy tensor, a
matter action, a coupling law, Einstein equations, benchmark status, or a
completed derivation.

## Basis

`handoff-0343` requires P5-T01 after P4-T06 selected the matter-semantics
preflight branch. P4 did not produce a stable adopted compression candidate.
It produced a stress-survived scoped current-basis obstruction with
same-milestone continuation open. The P5-T01 default therefore points away
from immediate detector, stress-energy, matter-action, conservation, universal
coupling, or Einstein-equation work and toward a precise source-side
matter-sector primitive target.

Prior matter-coupling draft/control artifacts already record source-side
matter-like sectors and stress-energy-interface criteria, but those artifacts
remain proposal-only criteria or candidate data. They do not authorize
stress-energy semantics, matter actions, detector semantics, coupling-law
adoption, matter coupling, `MetricData(E)`, `g_eff` scope expansion, Einstein
equations, benchmark promotion, or completed derivation. P5-T01 therefore
selects the narrowest preflight packet: formalize what a source-side
matter-sector discriminator target would have to be, including fail-closed
conditions if the current source ontology does not derive it.

## Milestone And Burden

Target derivation milestone: `matter_coupling`.

Milestone burden: select the first source-side matter-sector semantics packet
before coupling-law or Einstein-equation work.
