after the retired Visual Atlas/topic process was removed. Local memory and
historical task records still referenced older claim-gates derivatives, but
those were not active source authority and were not used as the creation

## After

The new page is a comparison and boundary map for claim gates, negative
results, and freeze criteria. It gives the reader:

- a claim-lifecycle state model;
- a scoped-obstruction versus global-no-go comparison;
- an allowed-claim versus forbidden-claim table grounded in the claim boundary
  registry pattern;
- a Gate Chair human-gate explanation grounded in the role registry; and
- a source-first checklist for summarizing failed or frozen lines.

## Boundary Preservation

The packet preserves:

- `draft/control` as noncanonical working status;
- `source-only` and `source-extension data` as limited source-side qualifiers;
- freeze labels as scoped route-control labels;
- validator pass state as structural control evidence only;
- generated HTML and GitHub-facing Markdown as noncanonical reader surfaces;
- Gate Chair status as human-gated; and
- claim boundaries as registry-controlled rather than page-created.

## Remaining Risks

- Local Obsidian and memory-index freshness warnings may persist after
  bootstrap; those are derivative retrieval warnings and not authority.
