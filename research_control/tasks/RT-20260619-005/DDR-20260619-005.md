contract and validator guard. The Phase 2 plan names the physics status and

promotion, Gate Chair verdicts, science draft execution, first-principles
derivation claims, physics claim promotion, generated-output authority,
contract or validator behavior changes, Phase 3 implementation, or corpus-wide
migration.
