\section{Finite/Local Non-Implication Lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Finite/local survival does not imply global \(\Msrc\) adoption]
Let \(W\) be a finite or local witness satisfying scoped source-cover,
regularity, soldering, transition-token, checker, or finite-variation checks
inside the tracked \(\Mgsccand(E)\) route. Then the existence or survival of
\(W\) does not by itself imply adopted global \(\Msrc\). Adoption requires a
source-only globalization theorem or equivalent proof package discharging the
carrier, topology or analogue, chart-support, transition, separation,
countability or regularity, soldering, finite-variation, bottom, and
no-target-import obligations in the adoption theorem envelope, followed by the
required audit, stress, and human-gated adoption decision.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The adoption theorem envelope in \texttt{RT-20260614-124} lists adoption-level
obligations A1--A9 and marks the theorem as unproven. The Gate Chair review in
\texttt{RT-20260614-123} explicitly did not grant full \(\Msrc\) adoption and
retained \(\Mgsccand(E)\) only as reviewed source-extension bridge-candidate
data. The transition-token packet in \texttt{RT-20260614-126} states candidate
conditions only and does not adopt transition structure. A finite/local
witness can instantiate at most a scoped portion of these obligations. It
therefore cannot discharge the global theorem, audit, stress, and human gate
by itself.
\end{proof}
