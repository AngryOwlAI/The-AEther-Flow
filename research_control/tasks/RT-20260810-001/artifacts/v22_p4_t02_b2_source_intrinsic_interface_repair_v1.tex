\begin{abstract}
This draft/control packet executes one bounded source-intrinsic repair of the
five defects recorded against the proposal-only P4--T02 B2 populated
descriptor.  It replaces the presentation-dependent component norm by an
intrinsic compact-open first-jet topology, constructs a constant bridge into

admits independent sector variations.  Two new source-side theorems then give

cannot select a projective tangent line naturally under the full local

strategy is locally frozen under the current records.  No global no-go theorem,
ontology adoption, complete B2 instance, adequacy verdict, physical cone,
effective metric, P4--T03 authority, or Distance-to-GR advance follows.
\end{abstract}

\section{F2: source-intrinsic first-jet topology}

Let \(U\) be the proposal-only smooth source patch and let
\(F_s\rightarrow U\) be a smooth sector bundle.  The topology below depends on
the smooth bundle and its jet bundle, not on a coordinate component norm.

\begin{definition}[Compact-open \(C^1\) first-jet topology]
Embed the space of \(C^1\) sections by
\[
 j^1:\Gamma^1(F_s)\longrightarrow C^0\!\left(U,\Jet(F_s)\right).
\]
Give the target its compact-open topology and \(\Gamma^1(F_s)\) the induced
topology.  A subbasis consists of sets
\[
 \mathcal N(K,O)=\{u\in\Gamma^1(F_s):j^1u(K)\subset O\},
\]
where \(K\subset U\) is compact and \(O\subset\Jet(F_s)\) is open.  Finite
intersections of such sets form basic neighborhoods.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Presentation invariance]
For a smooth source re-presentation \(\phi:U\to U'\) and the corresponding
bundle isomorphism, pullback of sections is a homeomorphism in the topology
above.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Jet prolongation is functorial: the first jet of a pulled-back section is the
pullback of its first jet.  Bundle isomorphisms map compact sets to compact
sets and open jet sets to open jet sets.  Thus subbasic neighborhoods pull
back to subbasic neighborhoods, and the same argument for the inverse gives a
homeomorphism.  No target atlas, component norm, metric, or physical scale is
used.
\end{proof}

This repairs F2 as a topological typing defect.  It does not make the source
patch canonical and does not prove stability of any physical cone.

Consequently, an arbitrary normalized rational preparation is not silently an

\begin{definition}[Exact constant bridge]
For \(s\in\Seq\), let
\[
 B_s^0:(\RR^3)^{G_0}\longrightarrow
 \Dom(\mathrm{P7Response}_s)

return \(p_s^0\) and the exact declared intervention, horizon, token alphabet,

\end{definition}

The map \(B_s^0\) is total and exactly typed, but it discards all sample
information.  Any nonconstant bridge therefore needs an explicit extension of
the finite preparation set.

Fix the carrier \(\mathcal B_3=\{b_0,b_1,b_2\}\), a finite nonempty proposal
grid \(G_0\), and \(N\ge1\).  Define the signed clipped rationalizer
\[
 q_N(t)=\operatorname{sgn}(t)
        \frac{\lfloor N\min\{|t|,1\}\rfloor}{N},
 \qquad \operatorname{sgn}(0)=0.
\]
Its image is the finite set
\(\{-1,-(N-1)/N,\ldots,0,\ldots,(N-1)/N,1\}\).

\begin{definition}[Finite-extension rationalized bridge]
For a sample \(a=(a_{xi})\in(\RR^3)^{G_0}\), set
\[
 z_i(a)=\sum_{x\in G_0}|q_N(a_{xi})|,
 \qquad Z(a)=\sum_{i=0}^2z_i(a),
\]
and
\[
 \mu_i(a)=
 \begin{cases}
 z_i(a)/Z(a),&Z(a)>0,\\
 1,&Z(a)=0\text{ and }i=0,\\
 0,&Z(a)=0\text{ and }i\ne0.
 \end{cases}
\]
Let \(P_{s,N}^{\rm ext}\) be the finite image of these preparation records,
together with \(p_s^0\).  Let \(\mathrm{P7Response}_{s,N}^{\rm ext}\) denote
the proposal-only protocol obtained by replacing \(P_s\) by
\(P_{s,N}^{\rm ext}\) while leaving the exact control, intervention, horizon,
token alphabet, total readout, success set, and failure classifier unchanged.
Then
\[
 B_{s,N}^{\rm ext}:(\RR^3)^{G_0}\longrightarrow
 \Dom(\mathrm{P7Response}_{s,N}^{\rm ext})
\]
returns the extended request with preparation \(\mu(a)\).
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Finite typing and equipment naturality]
For every sample and \(s\in\Seq\), \(B_{s,N}^{\rm ext}\) is defined,
\(\mu_i\in\QQ_{\geq0}\), \(\sum_i\mu_i=1\), and
\(P_{s,N}^{\rm ext}\) is finite.  Simultaneous carrier permutation with
transported initial address and protocol context commutes with both bridge
layers.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The finite image of \(q_N\), finite grid, and finite carrier give only finitely
many rationalized sample arrays and hence finitely many normalized
preparations.  Nonnegativity and unit sum follow by normalization or the
declared \(\delta_{b_0}\) zero branch.  A transported carrier permutation
permutes componentwise rationalization and the three sums while preserving
\(Z\); it also transports the constant initial preparation.  Thus both typed
squares commute.
\end{proof}

nontrivial typed map only as a finite proposal extension.  The extension is not

\section{F3: natural leading-line selection}

The RT023 operator inserted one common nowhere-zero vector field \(\tau\) in
every sector.  That assumption makes the common principal generator
\(p_\tau(k)=k(\tau)\) true by construction.  A repair must instead derive
each sector line independently from declared data and then compare the
outputs.

\begin{definition}[Sectorwise natural line selector]
For each \(s\in\Seq\), a repaired selector would be a map
\[
 \Lambda_s:(D_s,U,x)\longmapsto
 \Lambda_s(D_s,U,x)\in\PP(T_xU),
\]
where \(D_s\) contains only fixed finite P7 data and declared source data.  It
is source-presentation natural if every allowed local diffeomorphism \(\phi\)
satisfies
\[
 \Lambda_s(\phi_*D_s,U',\phi(x))
 =\PP(D\phi_x)\Lambda_s(D_s,U,x).
\]
The selector may not restrict the presentation group to the stabilizer of an
undeclared line.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[No natural line from fixed finite controls and a bare source patch]
\label{thm:no-selector}
Let \(\dim U\ge2\).  Suppose the source datum at \(x\) consists of a bare
smooth patch together with finite P7 control data that are unchanged by local
source-presentation germs fixing \(x\).  No selector into \(\PP(T_xU)\) is
natural under every such germ.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Every \(A\in\GL(T_xU)\) is the derivative at \(x\) of a local
diffeomorphism germ fixing \(x\).  Because the finite control record and bare
pointed patch supply no tangent-line datum, such a germ leaves the selector's
input record fixed.  Naturality would therefore require the selected line
\(\ell\) to satisfy \(\PP(A)\ell=\ell\) for all
\(A\in\GL(T_xU)\).  But no projective line is fixed by all of \(\GL(T_xU)\):
given a nonzero \(v\in\ell\), choose \(w\notin\ell\) and an invertible shear
with \(A(v)=v+w\).  Then \([A(v)]\ne[v]\), a contradiction.
\end{proof}

The theorem concerns this input type and full presentation group.  It does
not forbid a future conservative source extension from adding or deriving a
line distribution.  It does forbid disguising an undeclared line as
coordinate freedom.

\section{F4: independent sector-split variation}

Let \(\mathcal L_s=\Gamma^0(\PP(TU))\) carry its compact-open topology and
give \(\prod_{s\in\Seq}\mathcal L_s\) the product topology.  The common-line
locus is the diagonal
\[
 \Delta=\{(\ell_R,\ell_S,\ell_D):
                 \ell_R=\ell_S=\ell_D\}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[The common-line diagonal has empty interior]
\label{thm:empty-interior}
If \(\dim U\ge2\) and at least two sector line fields may vary independently,
then \(\Delta\) has empty interior in the product compact-open topology.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
It suffices to perturb at one source point inside any chosen product
neighborhood.  Choose tangent vectors \(e_0,e_1\) with linearly independent
values at that point.  For arbitrarily small nonzero \(\epsilon\), the triple
\[
 \ell_R^\epsilon=[e_0+\epsilon e_1],\qquad
 \ell_S^\epsilon=[e_0],\qquad
 \ell_D^\epsilon=[e_0-\epsilon e_1]
\]
lies arbitrarily close to \(([e_0],[e_0],[e_0])\) but not in \(\Delta\).
Local bump functions extend the pointwise perturbations to line fields inside
any prescribed compact-open neighborhoods.  Hence every neighborhood of a
diagonal point contains a non-diagonal triple.
\end{proof}

At the point, the corresponding fiber-linear principal ideals are
\[
 \langle k_0+\epsilon k_1\rangle,\qquad
 \langle k_0\rangle,\qquad
 \langle k_0-\epsilon k_1\rangle.
\]
For \(\epsilon\ne0\) their generators are not associates.  Consequently no
nowhere-zero degree-zero transition units can identify all three generators,
and the inverse and cocycle construction cannot start.  A diagonal-only
variation rule would remove precisely the negative controls needed to test
whether commonity was derived; it is not a repair unless a locking law first
derives that restriction.

\section{Precise obstruction and route consequence}

\begin{obstruction}[Natural line locking]
\label{obst:natural-line}
Under the current P7 records and bare smooth source patch, the populated B2
descriptor cannot obtain a source-presentation-natural sectorwise leading-line
selector whose commonity is robust under independent sector variation.
\end{obstruction}

\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:no-selector} rules out selection of even one natural
projective tangent line from the stated input under the full presentation
group.  Independently, Theorem~\ref{thm:empty-interior} shows that stipulated
equality of sector lines is not robust in the lawful product variation class.
The explicit split destroys associate generators and therefore the proposed
transition units.  The missing premise is exactly a
source-presentation-natural line-locking law.  No such primitive occurs in the
current P7 records or bare source patch.
\end{proof}
