under a beta-separated marked finite graph scope. To construct a
draft/control effective metric, one must also specify how that evidence
selects an effective metric carrier, metric-like record, signature, scale,

source-only rule selecting Lorentzian signature or a signature-like
classification. It contains no source-only scale normalization independent of
proper time, detector semantics, or benchmark behavior. It contains no
continuum or smooth-transition structure sufficient to support an effective

form or metric field.

proper time, empirical detector semantics, or exact-GR benchmark would violate

remain a possible future source-extension route, but that law is not present
