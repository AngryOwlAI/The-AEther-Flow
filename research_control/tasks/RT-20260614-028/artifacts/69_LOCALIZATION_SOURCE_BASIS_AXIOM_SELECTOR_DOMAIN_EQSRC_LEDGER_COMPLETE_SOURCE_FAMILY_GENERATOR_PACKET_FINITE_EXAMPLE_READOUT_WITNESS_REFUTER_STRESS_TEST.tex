\begin{abstract}
This draft/control Refuter artifact stress-tests the audited
\texttt{RT-20260614-026} finite example and source-readout witness
packet and the \texttt{RT-20260614-027} Smuggling Auditor local
conditional pass. The finite positive pair and the local theorem
\[
    X\EqLC Y \Longrightarrow \ReadLC(X)=\ReadLC(Y)
\]
survive as local source-ledger consistency evidence for the declared
\(\CertLC\) relation. They do not survive as finite-variation
robustness evidence, bridge relevance, general \(\EqSrc\),
\(\RetainH\), or \(\GenH\). Adverse source-side finite variations show
that representative independence, boundary invariance, readout
minimality, readout discriminating power, certificate inverse
robustness, quotient-composition robustness, and negative-witness
coverage remain open. Candidate reconstruction, canonical ontology
edit, benchmark promotion, Gate Chair review, completed-derivation
language, and global theory rejection remain blocked.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and Stress Question}

This stress test responds to \texttt{handoff-0069}. It treats the
finite witness/readout packet as a local source-side construction and
the Smuggling Auditor result as a local source-purity screen. It does
not treat validation, registration, generated visibility, role labels,
or handoff status as physical or mathematical evidence.

The stress question is:
\[
    \text{Does the audited finite witness/readout packet survive
    adverse finite-variation pressure as bridge-relevant robustness
    evidence?}
\]

The answer is no. The packet is retained as a local witness and source
consistency check, but finite-variation robustness and bridge relevance
remain in \(\Pause\).

\section{Stress Method}

\begin{stressscreen}[Local witness versus family robustness]
The positive pair \(X_{+},Y_{+}\) is accepted only as a local
transport witness. A single transported pair does not prove coverage of
the relevant source families, representative choices, refinement
histories, quotient compositions, or boundary identifications. Any move
from the pair to general \(\EqSrc\), \(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), bridge
readiness, candidate readiness, or benchmark recovery returns
\(\Block\).
\end{stressscreen}

\begin{stressscreen}[Readout invariance versus readout adequacy]
The theorem \(X\EqLC Y\Rightarrow \ReadLC(X)=\ReadLC(Y)\) is tested as
a one-way source consistency theorem. It is not tested as a converse,
not as minimality, not as bridge relevance, and not as physical
interpretation. Any later use of \(\ReadLC\) as a clock, causal,
matter-coupling, candidate, or benchmark observable requires a separate
audited bridge.
\end{stressscreen}

\begin{stressscreen}[Adverse finite variations]
The stress cases below vary only source-side data: guards, blockers,
influence records, quotient representatives, refinement inverses,
composition records, boundary anchors, and readout coordinates. They do
not use target metric, target proper time, target locality, observer
protocol, benchmark rank, candidate behavior, validation status, or
authority labels.
\end{stressscreen}

\section{Adverse Variation Stress}

\begin{counterexample}[Guard weakening and blocker erasure]
The near-miss object \(Y_{-}\) already deletes a blocker and weakens a
proxy guard. The packet correctly returns \(\Block\) because
\(\CertLC\) requires those source coordinates to be preserved.

The stress result is narrow: this verifies one negative mode. It does
not cover variations that preserve blocker counts while moving blocker
provenance between refined branches, split a blocker across aggregate
records, or keep the proxy multiset fixed while changing which branch
the proxy guards. Those variants require explicit inverse and
provenance checks.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Influence deletion with preserved counts]
Let \(Y_{\eta}\) keep the multiset of influence labels
\(\{\eta_u,\eta_v\}\) but exchange which influence record is attached
to which refined branch after a quotient refinement. If \(\ReadLC\)
counts only the influence multiset, then
\(\ReadLC(X_{+})=\ReadLC(Y_{\eta})\) can hold while the certificate
inverse data needed to recover branch-level influence provenance fails.

This does not refute the one-way invariance theorem. It blocks any
converse reading and shows that \(\ReadLC\) equality is not by itself
\(\EqLC\), bridge relevance, or robustness evidence.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Quotient representative variation]
Let \(q_u\) and \(q_u'\) be two source quotient representatives that a
future quotient semantics might identify. The current \(\ReadLC\)
uses exact multiset equality on \(Q\). If \(q_u\neq q_u'\) as ledger
tokens, the readout blocks equality even when a later quotient theory
would call them equivalent. If equality is relaxed to identify them,
the current proof of readout invariance no longer follows from the
existing componentwise codomain.

The stress result is \(\Pause\): either retain exact representative
accounting as a conservative local check or supply a source-side
representative-independence lemma. The current packet supplies the
former, not the latter.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Ambiguous refinement inverse]
The failed-certificate example with a refined \(u\)-branch records
\(k_u\) only at aggregate level. A variant can preserve every multiset
count in \(\ReadLC\) while changing which refined branch carries the
blocker provenance. The inverse remains ambiguous.

Therefore \(\ReadLC\) equality is too weak to certify inverse
recoverability. The certificate relation may still reject the variant,
but the readout alone cannot be used as a robustness certificate.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Noncomposing quotient refinement]
Let \(s_1:X_{+}\to Z_1\) and \(s_2:Z_1\to Y_{+}\) each preserve a
locally plausible source quotient label, but let the intermediate
representative chosen by \(s_1\) and the representative consumed by
\(s_2\) differ. The composition can fail even when the endpoint readout
multisets match.

The stress result blocks quotient-composition overread. A future repair
must state the source-side composition law and prove that the readout
is invariant under that law, or else record the failure explicitly.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Boundary-anchor perturbation]
\(\ReadLC\) records exact ordered boundary anchors \((i_X,o_X)\). A
source object with boundary anchors \((i_0',o_0')\) that are intended
to be equivalent under a later boundary identification is blocked by
the current exact codomain. Conversely, relaxing boundary equality
without a source-side boundary-invariance lemma breaks the current
proof.

The finite boundary example therefore protects against overread but
does not establish boundary-domain robustness.
\end{counterexample}

\section{Readout Adequacy Stress}

\begin{stressfinding}[Codomain overbreadth]
\(\ReadLC\) records boundary anchors and multisets of annotations,
proxy guards, forbidden guards, blockers, influence records, quotient
data, and contraction labels. This is intentionally conservative and
closely mirrors the coordinates protected by \(\CertLC\). The
invariance theorem is therefore expected.

That overbreadth is useful for a local source-consistency audit. It is
not evidence that the readout is minimal, physically interpretable, or
bridge relevant.
\end{stressfinding}

\begin{stressfinding}[Possible under-inclusion]
Because \(\ReadLC\) counts selected coordinates, it can omit
certificate history, branch-level provenance after refinement,
composition witnesses, and representative-choice data. Equality of
\(\ReadLC\) values cannot be read as equality of certificates or as
\(\EqLC\). A future discriminant packet must say which data are
intentionally ignored and prove that the omission is source-side safe.
\end{stressfinding}

\begin{stressfinding}[Negative-witness robustness remains open]
The near-miss negative object tests one defect family: proxy weakening
plus blocker erasure. It does not test all adverse finite variations
that keep readout counts stable while changing provenance, inverse
recoverability, composition, representative choice, or boundary
anchoring. The negative suite remains incomplete for robustness.
\end{stressfinding}

\section{Authority and Bridge-Ladder Stress}

\begin{controlrule}[No authority-label overread]
The Smuggling Auditor pass, validator success, registry rows, generated
wiki note, task closure, handoff status, and role labels remain process
receipts. They do not supply source-family coverage, readout
minimality, representative independence, bridge relevance,
\(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), candidate reconstruction, benchmark status, or
Gate Chair authority.
\end{controlrule}

\begin{controlrule}[No bridge-ladder collapse]
The following implications remain unproved:
\[
\begin{aligned}
    \text{finite local witness} &\Longrightarrow
    \text{source-family robustness},\\
    \text{source-readout invariance} &\Longrightarrow
    \text{bridge relevance},\\
    \text{bridge relevance} &\Longrightarrow
    \RetainH\text{ or }\GenH .
\end{aligned}
\]
Collapsing any rung returns \(\Block\).
\end{controlrule}

\begin{stressfinding}[Branch kill is not global rejection]
If a future repair cannot supply representative independence, boundary
invariance, quotient-composition robustness, or readout discriminant
limits without target primitives, that branch may return \(\Kill\) or
\(\Pause\). This does not globally reject the research program.
\end{stressfinding}

\section{Stress Verdicts}

\begin{verdict}[Local witness retained]
The audited finite witness/readout packet survives as a local
source-side witness and source-consistency theorem for the declared
\(\CertLC\) relation. The Smuggling Auditor local conditional pass
remains intact.
\end{verdict}

\begin{verdict}[Robustness pause]
The packet does not survive as finite-variation robustness evidence.
Representative independence, boundary invariance, quotient-composition
robustness, inverse provenance, readout minimality, readout
discriminating power, and negative-witness coverage remain open.
Scientific adequacy remains in controlled \(\Pause\).
\end{verdict}

\begin{verdict}[Promotion and bridge blocks preserved]
General \(\EqSrc\), \(\RetainH\), \(\GenH\), downstream bridge claims,
candidate reconstruction, canonical ontology edit, benchmark
promotion, Gate Chair review, completed-derivation language, and global
theory rejection remain blocked.
\end{verdict}
