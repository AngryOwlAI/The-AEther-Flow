# Research-Control Templates

This folder contains reusable templates for creating new research-control
records.

## What Belongs Here

- Blank or skeletal templates for tasks, Director decisions, AgentJobs,
  execution roles, completions, handoffs, approvals, and parent-child conflict
  reviews.

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Active task records.
- Completed receipts.
- Generated registry rows.
- Project-specific task decisions that belong under `research_control/tasks/`.

## How To Use

Copy a template into the correct task-local folder, fill in the task-specific
fields, then validate the completed record. Do not treat a template as active
state just because it contains an example field.

## Authority Boundary

Templates are scaffolding. Active authority comes from completed task-local
records, registered rows, role contracts, validators, and human gates where
required.
