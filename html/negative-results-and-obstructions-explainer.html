Negative Results And Obstructions

Failed, frozen, and obstructed routes are first-class research outputs when they are scoped. They preserve constraints without becoming global rejection, future impossibility, benchmark promotion, or completed derivation.

Frozen Route Versus Scoped Obstruction

Frozen route

Meaning: a specific attempted route should not be reused without new payload, redesign, or gate action.

Forbidden overread: no future route can work.

Scoped obstruction

Meaning: current premises or the current route fail for a named reason and scope.

Forbidden overread: the entire ontology is false.

Minimal countermodel

Meaning: a bounded witness shows that a proposed claim fails as stated.

Forbidden overread: the countermodel supplies downstream physics.

Certificate gap

Meaning: required source transport, invariance, factorization, or no-target certificates are absent or malformed.

Forbidden overread: certificates can be skipped.

Why The Project Keeps Negative Results

Negative results preserve technical memory. A failed source tuple, selector, witness, or certificate route can still improve the next packet by removing an unsafe shortcut from the search space.

The useful claim is narrow: this route did not discharge this burden under these assumptions. The unsafe claim is broader: the project has failed, future source-extension work is impossible, or downstream GR structure has been settled.

What Remains Open

The inventory does not close every future route. It leaves open redesigned finite toy metric-response routes, source-side response selector laws, certificate-indexed RR_E transport or invariance routes, future explicit certificate construction, and conservative source-extension candidates.

Those routes still need tracked source artifacts, audits, validators, and any required human gates before they can support stronger claims.

Safe Public Wording

Use narrow language

  • this route is frozen;
  • this obstruction is scoped;
  • this countermodel blocks the stated claim as written;
  • this certificate gap fails closed;
  • exact source artifacts remain authority.

Avoid broad language

  • the program is rejected;
  • future source-extension work is impossible;
  • the benchmark is promoted or suspended;
  • matter coupling, Einstein equations, or completed derivation follows from a negative-result record.
