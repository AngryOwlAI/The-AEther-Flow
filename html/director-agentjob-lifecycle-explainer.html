Phase 3A Control Records

Director Decisions And AgentJob Lifecycle

A lifecycle guide for the durable record chain that binds tasks, decisions, AgentJobs, execution roles, completions, handoffs, and registries into one inspectable transaction history.

Transaction Evidence, Not Broad Authority

The lifecycle records what was authorized, what was done, what was checked, and what should happen next. Each record narrows the transaction boundary.

Lifecycle States

Decision

A Director Decision Record follows .agents/schemas/DIRECTOR_DECISION_SCHEMA.md and explains why one role is selected.

Execution Contract

An AgentJob follows .agents/schemas/AGENT_JOB_SCHEMA.md and defines the bounded executable contract.

Role Binding

The execution-role record follows .agents/schemas/EXECUTION_ROLE_SCHEMA.md and binds role semantics to one job.

Completion

The completion records transaction evidence and remaining limits. It does not prove broader claims.

Registry Refresh

Registry rows make the records discoverable and checkable by future agents.

Stop Or Supersede

If the boundary is wrong, create a new bounded record. Do not rewrite the old decision into a different history.

Supersede Rather Than Rewrite

research_control/AGENTS.md states that Director decisions, AgentJobs, completions, approvals, and handoffs are immutable after activation or creation. That rule protects evidence from retroactive cleanup.

A repair can update registries, create a superseding decision, or open a new bounded task. It should not silently change what a prior job authorized or concluded.

Allowlists And Stop Conditions

Boundary Safe use Unsafe use
Write allowlist Change only the paths named by the AgentJob. Infer permission for neighboring files or later packets.
Forbidden paths Keep ontology, science drafts, control contracts, or other protected lanes out of scope when forbidden. Hand-edit protected sources because the current task is nearby.
Claim boundary State only the narrow transaction claims that were authorized. Promote benchmark, ontology, role, or generated-output authority.
Completion evidence Use it to inspect what one job did and checked. Treat it as broad proof or reusable permission.

Operator Mistakes

Mistake Risk Safer action
Editing an activated AgentJob to fit new work. The old authority boundary is rewritten. Supersede with a new decision and job.
Using a role template as current permission. The actual execution-role record is bypassed. Inspect registries/ROLE_EXECUTION_REGISTRY.csv and the job-local record.
Adding public HTML without brief and source spec. The page becomes an orphan public surface. Route a Documentation Curator publication packet.
Folding several objectives into one job. The one-job boundary becomes ambiguous. Split the work into bounded packets.

Safe Operating Summary

Safe

The lifecycle creates a narrow, inspectable record chain for one bounded transaction and preserves correction through supersession.

Unsafe

Historical records can be edited for convenience, completion evidence proves broad claims, or allowlists become reusable permissions.
