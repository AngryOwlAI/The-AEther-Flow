Parent-Child Parallel Synthesis

A parent-child physics job is still one bounded AgentJob. The decomposition adds internal perspectives for review and fusion; it does not add authority.

This page explains `parent_child_parallel_synthesis`. It does not change the one-job rule, schemas, validators, routing behavior, role authority, write permissions, or physics claim status.

Invariant Internal Lanes Authority Conflict Limits Sources

One External Transaction

The public-facing control invariant remains singular even when the inside of the job uses parent and child perspectives.

One DDROne Director decision selects the bounded job.
One AgentJobOne executable contract owns reads, writes, validators, and outputs.
One Role RecordOne execution-role record constrains the job.
One CompletionOne validation receipt closes the transaction.
One Fused OutputOne final artifact enters handoff and registry references.

Internal Lanes

Parent Review And Fusion

The parent derives child perspective duties from the selected execution role, enforces the shared boundary, reviews conflict, and fuses the final artifact.

The parent cannot add write paths, claim boundaries, source classes, validators, or human-gate exemptions.

Child Perspective Outputs

Child units provide analytical coverage, such as physicist-mathematician and physicist-philosopher perspectives.

Child outputs are supporting draft/control artifacts. They are not independent AgentJobs or execution-role records.

Inherited Authority

Inherited Element Meaning Boundary
Execution-role record The task-local role contract for the outer job. No child execution-role records.
Claim boundary The allowed and forbidden claim surface for the transaction. No child-local claim expansion.
Write allowlist The only paths the job may change. No extra child write paths.
Validators The checks required before completion. PASS is blocked by unresolved declared conflict.

Conflict Review

1Child drafts
Each child writes within inherited constraints.
2Parent review
The parent identifies agreement, disagreement, and missing burdens.
3Resolution
Blocking conflict receives bounded review or revision.
4Fusion
The final artifact preserves consensus, unique contributions, and limitations.

A declared blocking conflict that remains unresolved prevents PASS completion. This is a control rule, not an editorial preference.

What This Mode Cannot Do

Unsafe Interpretations

  • Multiple AgentJobs hidden inside one invocation.
  • Child outputs as independent authority.
  • Child execution units as separate role records.
  • A way around Smuggling Auditor, Refuter, or Gate Chair review.
  • A route to benchmark promotion or completed-derivation language.

Safe Interpretation

Parent-child synthesis improves the review geometry of a single physics AgentJob. It preserves the old external completion model while making internal disagreement visible.
