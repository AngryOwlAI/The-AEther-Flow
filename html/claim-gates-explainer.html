Claim Lifecycle State Model

Each state narrows the permissible summary. It does not broaden scientific authority.

Proposal A bounded packet names a construction, witness, audit, selector, or review target. draft/control
Audit A source-side screen checks for hidden target imports, authority laundering, or missing assumptions. source screen
Refutation Or Stress A reviewer tries to expose nonuniqueness, fragility, underdetermination, or missing primitives. adversarial
Completion One AgentJob records outputs, validators, claim boundaries, and next-route evidence. receipt
Handoff The next bounded route is named for future work. not executed
Freeze A repeated-burden or scoped-obstruction route is preserved so it is not repeated casually. scoped
Human Gate Protected authority such as Gate Chair approval, benchmark promotion, or ontology adoption remains human-gated. protected
Public Summary A reader-facing explanation must stay inside the evidence and claim-boundary record. noncanonical

Negative Results Preserve Knowledge

A negative result is useful because it makes future routing sharper. It can show that a source tuple underdetermines a selector, that a bridge attempt needs a missing primitive, or that a packet needs audit, stress, redesign, or human-gated adoption.

What It Can Say

A specific packet did not discharge its burden under the stated assumptions, source tuple, and no-target-import rules.

What It Cannot Say

The whole ontology is rejected, future source extensions are impossible, or Gate Chair approval has been issued.

Scoped Obstruction Versus Global No-Go

Phrase type Safe reading Unsafe inflation
Scoped obstruction A specific route failed under stated assumptions and source restrictions. The entire ontology is false.
Freeze label A route should not be repeated without new payload, redesign, or gate action. No future route can work.
Refuter stress result A construction showed fragility, nonuniqueness, or dependency on missing data. The project has no valid physics program.
Validator pass The repository control checks passed. Physics was proved.

The burden map's candidate label NDCL-RESP-LC-SELECTOR-UNDERDETERMINATION is scoped. It does not reject the entire ontology or prove future source-extension impossibility.

registries/AGENT_ROLE_REGISTRY.csv records gate-chair@0.1.0 as human-gated. A Documentation Curator page, validator pass, screenshot, completion record, or generated derivative cannot execute that gate.

The correct summary for protected decisions is narrow: if the explicit human-gated record is absent, the gate has not fired.

Source-First Summary Checklist

  1. Identify the exact burden the packet attempted to discharge.
  2. Identify the source tuple, primitive, extension, construction, or route tested.
  3. Classify the result as proposal, audit, stress test, completion, handoff, freeze, or gate.
  4. Read the claim boundary before summarizing status.
  5. Preserve qualifiers such as draft/control, source-only, source-extension data, local, exact-branch, and human-gated.

Safe And Unsafe Summaries

Safe

AEther-Flow preserves proposals, audits, refutations, stress tests, completions, handoffs, freeze labels, and human gates so each claim stays scoped to its source evidence and authority boundary.

Unsafe

A frozen route proves the whole theory false, validators prove physics, generated documentation can promote claims, or Gate Chair approval has occurred without an explicit human-gated record.
