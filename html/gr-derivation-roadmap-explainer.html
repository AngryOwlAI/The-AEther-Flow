The roadmap names the objects and gates that would have to exist before AEther-Flow could claim a first-principles derivation of ordinary GR or promote an exact-GR benchmark from source structure.

This generated noncanonical reader surface explains tracked control state. It does not update the Distance-to-GR ledger, adopt M_src, derive g_eff, derive matter coupling, derive Einstein equations, promote a benchmark, or issue a Gate Chair verdict.

Safe And Unsafe Summaries

Safe

The roadmap tracks separate source ontology, localization, response, manifold, metric, matter-coupling, equation, finite toy, and benchmark-promotion burdens while preserving draft/control and human-gated boundaries.

Unsafe

AEther-Flow has derived GR, M_src or g_eff is adopted, validator passes are physics evidence, or a scoped freeze label proves the whole ontology false.
