Adoption Versus Derivation

Adoption

Operational boundary

Observable-scale behavior is ordinary GR: one operative metric, universal matter coupling, ordinary causal structure, and no empirical deviation claim.

Compatibility

Ontology held beside the benchmark

The ontology may interpret what the benchmark is about, but compatibility does not force unique recovery from substrate data.

Derivation

Open source-side burden

A derivation would need source construction of metric, coupling, equations, and closure without importing target behavior by hand.

Promotion

Protected endpoint: not ready

Gate E records six inconclusive cases and zero passes. Benchmark promotion is denied as not ready, and a completed derivation may not be claimed.

Six Labels That Must Not Collapse

Ontology

Proposed source interpretation and architecture; not established physical reality.

Exact closure

Ordinary GR adopted target-side; not source recovery or Gate E promotion.

Formal equivalence

Declared mathematical relation; not provenance, physical meaning, or emergence.

Genuine emergence

Needs provenance, dynamics, uniqueness or quotient status, operational meaning, and robustness.

Operational meaning

Exact observer, detector, protocol, calibration, or protected-semantic link; not source derivation.

Empirical novelty

Distinct observable prediction with authorized comparison evidence; not supplied here.
