Project Overview

AEther-Flow is easiest to enter as two linked missions under one source-authority spine. The physics mission keeps ordinary general relativity as the exact public benchmark while substrate derivation remains open. The AI research-agent mission builds a governed workflow for theoretical work, documentation, validation, memory, and handoff evidence.

This overview is a route map. It helps a reader choose the correct source lane or page family before summarizing claims, changing files, or trusting generated derivatives.

Boundaries To Preserve

  • Exact-GR benchmark compatibility is not a completed substrate derivation.
  • A generated page can be clearer than a source file without becoming authority.
  • A validator pass is bounded check evidence, not a scientific verdict or editorial guarantee.
  • A role, skill, or tool being present does not expand a task-local allowlist.
  • Memory, wiki notes, semantic extracts, Obsidian mirrors, and `.local` caches support retrieval only.
  • The publication process improves reader surfaces without changing source authority.
