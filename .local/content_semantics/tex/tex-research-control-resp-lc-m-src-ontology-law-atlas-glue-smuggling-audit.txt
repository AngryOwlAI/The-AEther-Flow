\[

\section{Component Audit}

\begin{audit}[Input signature]
The input signature uses source patch carriers \(U_i\), response-labelled
source germs \(\Germ_i\), source overlap predicates \(\Ovsrc_{ij}\), and
source transition tokens. As written, it explicitly excludes target topology,
target smooth atlas, target coordinate transitions, metric scale, proper time,
empirical observer protocols, benchmark solutions, generated derivatives,
registry authority, role authority, and validation authority.
\end{audit}

\begin{audit}[Profile semantics]
The profile maps \(\Prof_i:U_i\to P_i^{\mathrm{src}}\) are permitted to use
source tokens and finite source-token tuples. The proposal forbids target
coordinates and benchmark behavior as profile values. This is source-pure as
written only if the profile values are explicit source data rather than labels
chosen by target resemblance, validation status, role identity, registry
visibility, or generated notes.
\end{audit}

\begin{audit}[Candidate transition families]
The candidate transition families \(\Cand_{ij}\) are partial maps between
source carriers satisfying source overlap and profile compatibility. No target
smoothness, metric compatibility, proper-time calibration, or benchmark
matching is required by the definition. The guard is that overlap predicates
and profile compatibility must remain supplied source-side predicates.
\end{audit}

\begin{audit}[Selector preorder]
The selector \(\Sel_{ij}\) returns a selected transition only when a unique
source-minimal candidate exists. It returns \(\Bot\) under ties,
incomparability, or missing source data. This fail-closed behavior blocks
selection by target geometry, benchmark success, validation status, role
authority, registry metadata, generated visibility, or arbitrary convention.
\end{audit}

\begin{audit}[Inverse and cocycle clauses]
The inverse and cocycle clauses are source-token equalities for selected
transitions on declared source overlaps. They are not imported smooth-atlas
compatibility theorems, differentiability assumptions, target coordinate
transition laws, or metric compatibility equations.
\end{audit}

\begin{audit}[Finite-variation preservation]
\(\Var\) and \(\Stab\) are defined as admissible source-side perturbation and
stability obligations. The proposal does not prove arbitrary finite-variation
robustness from current ontology. It merely states the obligation that a
future stress test must attempt to break.
\end{audit}

\begin{audit}[Conditional quotient support]
The repaired source quotient support theorem is conditional on supplied law
data. It yields \(\Qsrc\) relative to profile compatibility, selector
uniqueness, inverse compatibility, cocycle compatibility, and finite-variation
preservation hypotheses. It is not \(\Msrc\) adoption, not a target manifold,
not a smooth atlas, and not a downstream metric result.
\end{audit}

\section{Audit Lemmas}

\begin{lemma}[No target-import detected as written]
Under the written dependency basis of \(\MSL\), the proposal does not require
target topology, target smooth atlas data, target coordinate transitions,
target metric, proper time, empirical observer protocols, detector semantics,
matter coupling, benchmark solution structure, validation status, role
authority, registry authority, or generated derivative authority as premises.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The definitions quantify over source patch carriers, source germs, source
overlap predicates, source transition tokens, source profile maps, source
candidate transition families, source selector preorders, source inverse and
composition operations, and source finite-variation classes. The selector
fails closed at \(\Bot\) whenever the source data do not determine a unique
choice. The proof of conditional quotient support begins only after those
source-law data are supplied. No step calls target charts, target metric
components, proper-time functionals, empirical calibration, benchmark
solutions, validation receipts, role labels, registry metadata, or generated
derivatives. Therefore no forbidden target or process-authority import is
detected as written.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Source-authority guard]
The audit pass does not prove that the current canonical ontology derives
\(\MSL\). The current ontology does not derive the source-authorized
atlas-glue law required for \(\AtlasGlueDisc\), \(\AtlasGlue\), or \(\Msrc\).
The proposal can be treated only as proposal-only source-extension data unless
and until a protected human gate authorizes adoption.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The proposal defines a law tuple and then proves conditional consequences
relative to that tuple. A conditional consequence from supplied law data is
not a derivation of the law data from the current ontology. Treating the words
``source-law'', a validation pass, a registry row, a role label, or a handoff
as adoption authority would change the dependency basis. Therefore source
authority remains blocked while same-milestone continuation remains open.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Finite-witness overread guard]
The earlier finite witness \(\Wfin\) may motivate concrete audit clauses, but
it does not prove the general proposal-only law, arbitrary finite-variation
robustness, \(\AtlasGlueDisc\) adoption, \(\AtlasGlue\) adoption, or full
\(\Msrc\) construction.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
\(\Wfin\) is a finite supplied datum with explicit profiles, selector
preorders, inverse checks, cocycle checks, quotient support, and failure
branches. The proposal \(\MSL\) is a general source-law tuple for source
profile semantics, selectors, transitions, inverse and cocycle clauses, and
finite-variation preservation. Passing audit on a finite supplied datum does
not derive the general law or discharge robustness beyond that datum.
Consequently the finite witness cannot be overread as adoption evidence for
\(\MSL\), \(\AtlasGlueDisc\), \(\AtlasGlue\), or \(\Msrc\).
\end{proof}

\begin{guard}[Exact-GR recovery boundary]
Exact-GR recovery remains an obligation and may not be used as a premise for
the source-law tuple. The audit detects no explicit use of observer-level
recovery, benchmark success, \(g_{\mathrm{eff}}\), matter coupling, or
Einstein-equation behavior in \(\MSL\) as written. Any future use of those
objects as source-law premises would fail this audit boundary.
\end{guard}
