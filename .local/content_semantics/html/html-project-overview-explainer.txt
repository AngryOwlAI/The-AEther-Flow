Project Overview
AEther-Flow is easiest to enter as two linked missions under one source-authority spine. The physics mission keeps ordinary general relativity as the exact public benchmark while substrate derivation remains open. The AI research-agent mission builds a governed workflow for theoretical work, documentation, validation, memory, and handoff evidence.
This overview is a route map. It helps a reader choose the correct source lane or page family before summarizing claims, changing files, or trusting generated derivatives.
Reader scope: first-entry orientation only. This page cannot certify a derivation, expand a role, change routing, change validators, grant write permission, or make generated documentation authoritative.
