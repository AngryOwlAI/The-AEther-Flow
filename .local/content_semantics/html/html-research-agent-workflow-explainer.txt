The research system turns questions, handoffs, and improvement signals into bounded agent work with explicit authority and receipts.
Summary of Research System
The research system is the governed workflow that turns a question, continuation state, or project-improvement signal into bounded agent work with explicit roles, decisions, registries, artifacts, validation, completion records, and handoffs. Its function is to separate physics continuation from project-system maintenance, resolve tracked state before acting, assign one bounded AgentJob, constrain that job with role authority and allowlists, and preserve completion evidence for the next handoff. Optional parent-child synthesis can add analytical perspectives inside one AgentJob, but it cannot create new authority, extra jobs, or independent outputs. The system matters because AEther-Flow is not an informal chat log or autonomous proof engine. It is a controlled research program where progress, obstructions, generated derivatives, and negative results must remain reproducible and auditable.

State Entry
research_control/AGENTS.md
research_control/README.md
Research work begins from tracked state, not memory of a conversation alone. Program state, handoffs, task records, and registries define what is active, what is completed, and what boundary controls the next action. Local caches and generated reading surfaces can help an operator find context, but they do not override tracked control state. That rule matters because continuation work often spans many tasks; without a state entry discipline, an agent could restart old work, skip a blocker, or treat a stale derivative as the current decision.
Director Decision
registries/DIRECTOR_DECISION_REGISTRY.csv
research_control/README.md
The Director decision converts a request or handoff into a role selection and bounded objective. It records why a role fits, which roles were rejected, whether a human gate is required, what claim boundary applies, and which AgentJob will carry the executable contract. The decision is not a result in itself. It is the controlled selection of who may act and under what constraints, so the project can later audit why a physics role, documentation role, validator role, or project-control role was used.
AgentJob Lifecycle
.agents/schemas/AGENT_JOB_SCHEMA.md
registries/AGENT_JOB_REGISTRY.csv
An AgentJob is the unit of accountable work. It names the task, role, status, reads, writes, forbidden paths, expected outputs, validators, and completion record. Optional role decomposition stays internal to the same job and must end in one fused output when PASS is claimed. The lifecycle prevents open-ended improvement from becoming uncontrolled rewrite. A job can complete, block, or preserve an obstruction, but it must leave enough evidence for the next operator to verify what happened.
Role Execution
.agents/schemas/EXECUTION_ROLE_SCHEMA.md
registries/ROLE_EXECUTION_REGISTRY.csv
The execution-role record binds a reusable role contract to one task. It may use the registered role directly, add a bounded task overlay, or define a one-job provisional role that expires after the job. This distinction prevents role names from becoming vague permission. A Documentation Curator, Project-Control Maintainer, Refuter, or Smuggling Auditor may all be useful, but each carries different authority. The task-local record states which permissions are present, removed, expanded, or forbidden for the exact job.
Validation and Handoff
research_control/templates/COMPLETION_TEMPLATE.yaml
scripts/research_control/validate_research_control.py
Validation checks that the job stayed inside its boundary and produced the promised evidence. Completion YAML records the verdict, output paths, command results, validation status, and next recommendation. A handoff then preserves the next state so the following turn can continue from source-controlled evidence. PASS means the transaction met its declared workflow and validator requirements; it does not by itself promote a physics claim, register new authority, or erase unresolved scientific burdens.
Registry Update
registries/RESEARCH_TASK_REGISTRY.csv
registries/AGENT_JOB_REGISTRY.csv
registries/ROLE_EXECUTION_REGISTRY.csv
Registries make the research system queryable. Task, Director decision, AgentJob, role execution, claim-boundary, Markdown, HTML, and generated-output registries record object IDs, paths, hashes, status, relationships, owners, and validation timestamps. That registry spine lets humans and agents audit what changed and find related outputs without treating every generated note as authority. Registry updates are therefore not clerical decoration; they are part of making long-running theoretical work reproducible.
