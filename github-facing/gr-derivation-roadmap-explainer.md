## Source-Backed Summary

The GR derivation roadmap is the project's control surface for the open physics burden. It keeps the public target conservative: ordinary GR remains the observable benchmark, while a first-principles derivation from AEther or AEther-flow substrate structure remains unresolved. The roadmap names the physics objects that must exist before benchmark promotion can be considered, records their current status in the Distance-to-GR ledger, and requires future physics AgentJobs to declare the milestone and burden they are trying to advance. This prevents a useful candidate, a workflow completion, or a generated explanation from being mistaken for a completed derivation. It also gives negative results a stable place: repeated selector underdetermination, scoped obstruction, source-extension pressure, or finite toy-model failure can freeze a route without rejecting the entire ontology.

## What This Feature Does

The roadmap turns the open derivation problem into explicit milestones. It names source ontology, `EqSrc`, `ObsLoc_lc`, `Resp_lc`, `M_src`, `g_eff`, matter coupling, Einstein equations, benchmark promotion, and finite toy metric-response scaffolding as separate burdens. A future physics AgentJob must say which milestone it targets and what burden it intends to discharge.

## Why The Project Needs It

The project needs this roadmap because the physics lane is speculative and incomplete. Without a milestone ledger, a local witness, a draft object, a negative result, or a polished explanation could be misread as progress toward all of GR. The roadmap keeps the project honest by recording what is present, what is blocked, and what would still need a gate.

## How It Works

The burden map defines the milestone chain and accepted status vocabulary. The Distance-to-GR ledger stores the persistent row state. Research-control guidance requires future physics completions to update the expanded burden matrix and include a new mathematical payload. Repeated-burden or scoped-obstruction results must evaluate freeze criteria rather than repeating the same unresolved bridge step.

## What It Is Not

It is not a derivation of `g_eff`, not a matter-coupling theorem, not an Einstein-equation derivation, not benchmark promotion, and not a Gate Chair verdict. A frozen route is a controlled negative result for a specific line, not a global rejection of every future source-extension or ontology path.
