# Negative Results And Obstructions

AEther-Flow preserves negative results because failed, frozen, and obstructed
routes are useful source-backed constraints. They tell future work what not to
reuse casually, where a premise failed, and which claims remain blocked.

They do not reject the research program. They do not prove future
source-extension impossibility. They do not promote benchmark status or
complete the derivation.

## Frozen Route Versus Scoped Obstruction

| Term | Meaning | Forbidden overread |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frozen route | A specific attempted route should not be reused without new payload, redesign, or gate action. | No future route can work. |
| Scoped obstruction | Current premises or the current route fail for a named reason and scope. | The entire ontology is false. |
| Minimal countermodel | A bounded witness shows that a proposed claim fails as stated. | The countermodel supplies downstream physics. |
| Certificate gap | Required source transport, invariance, factorization, or no-target certificates are absent or malformed. | Certificates can be skipped. |
| Open continuation | A route remains possible through redesigned primitives, certificates, source extensions, or human-gated authority. | The route is already complete. |

## Why The Project Keeps Negative Results

Negative results preserve technical memory. A failed source tuple, selector,
witness, or certificate route can still improve the next packet by removing an
unsafe shortcut from the search space.

The useful claim is narrow: this route did not discharge this burden under
these assumptions. The unsafe claim is broader: the project has failed, future
source-extension work is impossible, or downstream GR structure has been
settled.

## What Remains Open

The inventory does not close every future route. It leaves open redesigned
finite toy metric-response routes, source-side response selector laws,
certificate-indexed `RR_E` transport or invariance routes, future explicit
certificate construction, and conservative source-extension candidates.

Those routes still need tracked source artifacts, audits, validators, and any
required human gates before they can support stronger claims.

## Safe Public Wording

Use narrow language:

- this route is frozen;
- this obstruction is scoped;
- this countermodel blocks the stated claim as written;
- this certificate gap fails closed;
- exact source artifacts remain authority.

Avoid broad language:

- the program is rejected;
- future source-extension work is impossible;
- the benchmark is promoted or suspended;
- matter coupling, Einstein equations, or a completed derivation follows from
  a negative-result record.
