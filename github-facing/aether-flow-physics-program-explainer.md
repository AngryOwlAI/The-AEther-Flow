# AEther-Flow Physics Program

The AEther-Flow physics program asks whether ordinary general relativity can be
interpreted, and eventually derived, from a deeper four-dimensional AEther /
AEther-flow ontology. The current project state is deliberately conservative:
exact GR is preserved as the observable-scale benchmark, while the
first-principles substrate derivation remains open. The useful reading is a
five-layer status model: ontology vocabulary, exact-GR benchmark discipline,
derivation burden, negative-result preservation, and protected claim gates
must stay distinct when the program is summarized.

## Program Map

| Human gates | Protect ontology adoption, benchmark promotion, and Gate Chair verdicts. | Generated documentation cannot bypass gates. |

## Physics Track And Agent Track

The physics track supplies the hard research problem: recover ordinary GR or an
exact-GR benchmark from source-side AEther / AEther-flow structure without
importing the target metric or benchmark behavior by hand. The AI
research-agent track supplies the operating discipline: bounded AgentJobs,
role contracts, claim boundaries, refutation, source-first memory, and
negative-result preservation.

Those tracks help each other, but they do not collapse into one another. A
workflow result can organize evidence; it cannot by itself become physics
evidence. A generated page can make a status legible; it cannot change the
status.

## Negative Results And Freeze Discipline

No-go, obstruction, and freeze records are part of the physics program's
discipline. They prevent repeated work from laundering a weak packet into a
claim and preserve information about what failed. A freeze label can block a
route or mark a scoped obstruction. It does not automatically reject the whole
ontology or prove future source-extension impossibility.

For example, the burden map treats response localization, source manifold
construction, effective metric generation, matter coupling, Einstein
equations, and benchmark promotion as separate burdens. Blocking one route
does not complete or refute the entire chain.

## Source-Side Work Versus Claim Promotion

Source-side mathematical work may define a draft object, test a witness,
classify a source extension, or build a finite toy analogue. Claim promotion is
different. Promotion requires the appropriate source authority, claim boundary,
audit or refutation sequence, and human-gated decision when the target is
ontology adoption, benchmark promotion, or Gate Chair closure.

The project therefore allows creative construction while keeping public claims
disciplined. Inventive work belongs in controlled source artifacts first; public
summaries inherit only what those sources actually authorize.
