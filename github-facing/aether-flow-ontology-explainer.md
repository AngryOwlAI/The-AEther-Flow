# AEther-Flow Ontology

AEther-Flow ontology is the project's vocabulary for what the relativistic
mathematics is about. It names a deeper four-dimensional substrate, its ordered
motion, and the observer-level experience of space, time, and expansion. That
vocabulary is useful only when it remains separated from mathematical
derivation and empirical prediction. The current ontology lane explains what
the project is about; it does not by itself force the exact-GR benchmark.

## Why Older Aether Intuitions Mislead

The project does not revive a static nineteenth-century medium. The live
ontology moves the subject into a four-dimensional substrate whose ordered
motion is not directly seen as a preferred three-dimensional wind. The older
intuition fails if it pictures AEther as a substance flowing through already
given observed space.

The safer reading is inverse: observed space is the observer-level slice, and
the flow language belongs to the deeper substrate description. This is why
public summaries should avoid saying that space is literally an ordinary fluid
or that matter is simply drifting in a detectable flow.

## Gravity Language

The ontology note permits gravity-as-reorganization language: matter may be
interpreted as locally reorganizing the surrounding AEther-flow, with
gravitational attraction appearing at the observer level. That is a heuristic
ontology statement unless a registered TeX source states a stronger
mathematical claim.

The current missing layer is equation-level. The project needs a source-side
construction of an observer normal/readout orbit from local AEther response
data, or an equivalent source-side bridge, before response factorization,
same-metric matter, nonmetric mode control, or S-time closure can be promoted.

## Target-Side Congruence Is Not A Source Bridge

The protected P14-T04 decision gives the flow-geometry congruence a narrow
positive status. A future-directed unit timelike `u^mu` is admissible as a
generally non-unique target-side interpretive representative inside the
adopted GR benchmark. It can organize expansion, shear, vorticity,
acceleration, and observer-relative decomposition language.

That status does not identify `u^mu` with the unresolved source slot
`Phi_src`, select a congruence from the source ontology, establish pure-gauge
status, add an independent dynamical field, or complete a source-to-target
bridge. For a fixed target solution, fixed base observables, and fixed
protocols, the stated predictions are representative independent.
Congruence-explicit or observer-relative quantities can still differ when the
observer field or protocol changes.
